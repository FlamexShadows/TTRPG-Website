# Sparks of Dynasty — 200 Unique Monsters Bestiary

A complete roster of **200 unique fantasy monsters** built strictly to the Sparks of Dynasty monster template, organized by Creature Type across Threat Ratings **TR 1–20** and **Minion / Elite / Solo** tiers.

## Roster Summary
- **Total Monsters:** 200
- **By Tier:** Minion (66), Elite (94), Solo (40)
- **By Creature Type:** Angel (10), Beast (18), Cryptid (14), Draconid (10), Elemental (16), Fiend (16), Golem (12), Humanoid (22), Magical Beast (20), Outsider (8), Plant (12), Slime (10), Spirit (14), Undead (18)

## Quick Rules Reference
- **Minion:** `3 × TR` stat pts, half HP (rounded down), weapon dice + half attack stat mod (rounded down), 0–2 basic abilities (no saves, reactions, spells, or maneuvers), 0 magic pts, 1 max turn, counts as `½ × TR`.
- **Elite:** `5 × TR` stat pts, full HP, weapon dice + attack stat mod, `TR ÷ 2` magic pts (max 10), 1 max turn, counts as `1 × TR`.
- **Solo:** `7 × TR` stat pts, full HP, weapon dice + attack stat mod, `TR` magic pts (max 10), 2 max turns, counts as `2 × TR`.
- **Action Costs:** Basic Attack `3 AP`, Utility `2 AP`, Big Area/Control `4 AP` (`1/round`). Area & bonus elemental dice do not add modifiers; successful saves halve area damage.
