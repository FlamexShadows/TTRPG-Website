# Shop Expansion Catalog — 310 New Items

An expansion catalog for **Sparks of Dynasty**, featuring **100 new mundane items** for the [General Store](../Webpages/general-store.html) plus **30 new items for each of the other 7 shop tabs** (**310 items total**), with zero duplicates of items already in [shops-data.js](../Webpages/shops-data.js). Prices are in **GP** (and weights in **kg** where applicable).

- **General Store:** 100 items
- **General Smithing:** 30 items (Weapons, Armor, Shields)
- **Grand Elixirs:** 30 items (Potion Ingredients, Potions)
- **Marionette's Market:** 30 items (Magic Items by Slot & Power)
- **Church:** 30 items (Holy Goods, Rites & Blessings)
- **Cloud's Pet Shop:** 30 items (Mounts, Pets & Companions)
- **Black Market:** 30 items (Poisons, Contraband & Illicit Goods)
- **Services:** 30 items (Hired Help, Travel, Crafting & Appraisals)
