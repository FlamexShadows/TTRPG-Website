1. **Character attack and DC formulas.** The rules say combat DCs use your primary stat, but there are no formulas for a character's spell attack, spell save DC, maneuver attack or maneuver save DC. The Sheet Formulas chapter doesn't list them either. Monsters have all four.

   Character attack formula is str (or finesse if weapon light and higher than strength) + level if proficent + magical bonus + class features - penalties.

   Spell/Manuver DC Formula: 10 + Primary Statistic + Magical Bonus - Penalty.

   Spell/Manuver Attack Bonus: Level + Primary Statistic +Magical Bonus - Penalty.

2. **Class save bonus.** Every save adds a "class save bonus," but the rules never say how much it is. The character sheet uses Good, Average and Poor rates (full level, half, a third). That needs to be written into the rules.

   Good Save: Level + Statistic + Magical Bonus - Penalty
   Average Save: (Level/2 Rounded Down) + Statistic + Magical Bonus - Penalty
   Poor Save: (Level/2 Rounded Down) + Statistic + Magical Bonus - Penalty

3. **Classes and multiclassing.** The classes only exist as website pages. The rules need a Classes chapter with hit dice, primary and secondary bonuses, save rates, slots and Mana or Stamina. The sheet allows two classes, but the rules never say whether or how multiclassing works.

   Multiclassing Rules: Grants classes access to features equal to levels in class and stats follow the chart. So if Billy Bob the level 15 warrior multiclasses into Hunter he gets Hunter class profienices. His saves for each class are seperate but combined. For example at level 16 Billy Bob would have his daves for Mage count at 15 and his saves for Hunter be level 1.

   Billybob would gain +2 finesse and +1 wits, 3 skill levels, 2 manuvers selected as if he was single classed in Hunter. He also gains 2 stamina and his hit dice would be the d6. Billybob can choose a subclass in hunter and gains its first level class and subclass feature.

   Because Billybob's total level is 16, he would still gain his +3 level bonus to stats and would still gain access to his 16th level feat.
