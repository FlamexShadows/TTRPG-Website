The caster mixes together a healing paste of herbs. The paste lasts for 24 hours, and each casting of the spell creates enough of a dosage to treat 10 Tiny creatures, 5 Small creatures, 2 Medium Creatures or 1 Giant or larger creature. Applying the Paste takes 1 minute. After the application, the creature regenerates 1d4 health at the start of each of its turns.
