---
name: "Wrath of the Tides"
range: "Sight"
casting_time: "1 Minute"
rank: "Grand"
school: "Hydromancy"
subtype: "Water"
requirements: "Words of Power, Sigils, Water"
duration: "Instant"
concentration: "No"
---
The caster raises a wall of seawater 20 meters tall and 100 meters wide and sends it crashing forward up to 500 meters. Each creature caught in the wave takes 10d6 bludgeoning damage and is swept up to 20 meters and knocked down; a creature that succeeds on a Resistance (Body) save takes half damage and is not swept. Small structures are destroyed and flames are extinguished. The water drains away over the following minute.
