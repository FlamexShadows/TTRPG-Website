---
name: "Pillars of Ruin"
range: "1 Kilometer"
ap: "4"
rank: "Grand"
school: "Heavenly"
subtype: "Retribution"
requirements: "Words of Power"
duration: "Instant"
concentration: "No"
---
The caster speaks a holy word chanting for the destruction of 4 areas of its choice it can see within range of itself. A beam of light parts the sky causing all within a 4 meter square to be smitten by the heavens. Any creature within the area must succeed a Resistance (Body) or take 15d12 holy damage. Evil aligned creatures instead take 20d12. On a failure a creature with no magical armor is blind. A creature struck by multiple pillars is only affected by one. A creature reduced to 0 hit points is slain and is reduced to a shadow in place of where they previously stood.
