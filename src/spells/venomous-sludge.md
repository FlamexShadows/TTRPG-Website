range: "10 Meters"
ap: "4"
rank: "Minor"
school: "Biomancy"
subtype: "Evolution"
requirements: "Words of Power, Sigils, 1 Mutagen"
duration: "10 Minutes"
concentration: "No"
---
The caster coats a willing creature a thick venomous mucus. Any creature that makes a melee attack against the target must succeed on a Evasion save or take 2d6 poison damage. Any creature that swallows the target when covered must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or be forced to regurgitate the caster.
