---
name: "Rebirth"
range: "Touch"
casting_time: "1 Hour"
rank: "Major"
school: "Nature"
subtype: "Protection"
requirements: "Words of Power, Sigils"
duration: "Instant"
concentration: "No"
---
The caster touches a creature giving its body to nature and allowing its soul to be reborn anew. If the soul is willing it is placed into a pregnant creature of the same species with a developing baby within 100 kilometers. The creature is reborn as the baby forgetting its past life but is given a second chance at a new one.
