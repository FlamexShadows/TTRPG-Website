---
name: "Confession"
range: "5 Meters"
ap: "4"
rank: "Major"
school: "Enchantment"
subtype: "Charm"
requirements: "Words of Power"
duration: "Permanent"
concentration: "No"
---
The caster targets a creature compelling it to tell what it believes to be the truth. The creature must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save or be forced to answer 3 questions truthfully. Until the creature answers the question it is Silenced. This spell has no effect or is dispelled on a creature under the effects of a Free Speech and can be removed by a Purge spell.
