The most mighty spell a caster may evoke upon the world. The caster calls upon the weave creating a visible streak in the sky identical to a comet passing by to create an effect they desire. The base use of this spell is to remove any condition, effect, raise a statistic of the caster's choice by 5 to a maximum of 20 or cast any Major or lesser Aeromancy, Pyrokinesis, Geokinesis, Arcanomancy, Necromancy, or Transmutation schools. Alternatively the caster may wish for something greater.

The spell can create an effect such as producing 500,000 gold or undoing certain events or something else the caster may wish for. If the spell is successful doing so comes at a risk of cracking the caster source vat. The caster must roll a 1d100. On a 50 or higher the caster has put too much strain on their source causing it to crack and the caster loses an amount of mana the GM determines depending on how much was granted.
