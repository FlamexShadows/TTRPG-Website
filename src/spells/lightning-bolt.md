requirements: "Words of Power"
duration: "Instant"
concentration: "No"
---
The caster creates a bolt of lightning that shocks creatures within a line. The caster shoots a 1-meter wide and 10 meters long bolt that passes through up to 4 creatures. Any creature within the area affected by this bolt must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save. On a failure, the creature takes 5d6 air damage. On a success, the creature takes half as much damage.
