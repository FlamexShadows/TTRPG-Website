---
name: "Static Cling"
range: "10 Meters"
ap: "2"
rank: "Minor"
school: "Aeromancy"
subtype: "Lightning"
requirements: "Words of Power"
duration: "1 Minute"
concentration: "No"
---
The caster charges a creature within range with crackling static. The target takes 1d8 air damage. If the target is wearing metal armor or holding a metal weapon, it must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or become Shocked until the end of its next turn.
