duration: "10 Minutes"
concentration: "No"
---
The caster chooses an object within their hand. Uttering words of power they conduct the energy from the material used as a component heating the object. Alternatively, they can use the heat from the object in their hand and transfer it to the component, igniting and burning the component and cooling the object.
