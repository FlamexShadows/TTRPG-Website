name: "Hypnosis"
range: "15 Meters"
ap: "4"
rank: "Minor"
school: "Enchantment"
subtype: "Charm"
requirements: "Words of Power, Sigils"
duration: "1 Minute"
concentration: "Yes"
---
The caster attempts to influence all creatures within a 5 meter cube with mesmerizing lights. All creatures within the radius of these lights must succssed a Resistance (Mind) save or become stunned for 1 minute. This effect ends when a creature uses 3 AP to snap it out of its stupor, it takes damage or is subjected to a harmful effect.
