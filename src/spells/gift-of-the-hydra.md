name: "Gift of the Hydra"
range: "Self"
ap: "6"
rank: "Adept"
school: "Biomancy"
subtype: "Evolution"
requirements: "Words of Power, 5 Mutagen"
duration: "1 Minute"
concentration: "Yes"
---
The caster grants themselves the resilience of a hydra. The caster becomes immune to death effects from losing limbs or organs. In addition, the caster regains 10 health at the start of each of their turns. The effects of this spell are halted if the caster takes fire damage or begins corroding until the end of its next turn.
