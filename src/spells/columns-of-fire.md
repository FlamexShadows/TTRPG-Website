name: "Columns of Fire"
range: "20 Meters"
ap: "5"
rank: "Adept"
school: "Pyromancy"
subtype: "Flame"
requirements: "Words of Power, Sulfur"
duration: "Instant"
concentration: "No"
---
The caster creates up to 5 pillars of fire that erupt from the ground. The caster chooses five 1 meter points of their choice within range. Any creature that is standing in the area must succeed on an Evasion save or take 6d10 fire damage.
