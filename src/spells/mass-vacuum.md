This spell functions as <a href="vacuum.html">Vacuum</a> but affects a 30 meter cube instead of a single target. Spells that require air or water such as <a href="wall-of-wind.html">Wall of Wind</a>, <a href="tornado.html">Tornado</a>, or <a href="tsunami.html">Tsunami</a> have no effect on creatures inside mass vacuum.
