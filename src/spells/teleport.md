<div class="spell-text-html">
<p>The caster utters a chant folding the space of their current location and connecting it to another allowing for instantaneous travel to anywhere on the current plane they reside.
The caster chooses an area they are aware of and chooses up to five willing creatures and rolls a percentile die.
The die determines how successful the teleportation is.
</p>
