<div class="spell-text-html">
<p>The caster targets a creature causing the creature's body to dimorph ... </p>
<ul>
<li><strong>1-10:</strong> The creature grows an extra arm for the duration. If it has a weapon available for the arm each time it takes the attack action it makes an extra attack. </li> <br>
<li><strong>11-20:</strong>  The creatures body density morphs to unproportionate levels. The creature adds +2 to its AC.</li><br>
<li><strong>21-30:</strong> The creatures brain begins to expand. The creature adds +5 to its Intelligence, and Wits but takes a -5 Charisma.</li><br>
<li><strong>31-50:</strong> The creatures Muscles expand and surges with unnatural adrenaline. The creature adds +5 to its Strength and Fortitude but takes a -5 penalty to Finesse.</li><br>
<li><strong>51-70:</strong> The creature's body morphs causing it great pain in the process. The creature becomes crippled</li><br>
<li><strong>71-100:</strong> The creature's body morphs into an unnaturally slim yet perfected version of its race. The creature gains a +5 to Finesse and Charisma but suffers a -5 penalty to Strength and Fortitude  </li> <br>
</ul>
</div>
