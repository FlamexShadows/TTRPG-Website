name: "Crown of Honey"

ap: "4"
rank: "Adept"
school: "Transmutation"
subtype: "Creation"
requirements: "Words of Power, Sigils"
duration: "Instant"
concentration: "No"
---
The caster attempts to blind a creature by conjuring a pool of honey and hurling it in the creature's eyes. The creature must succeed on an Evasion save or become blind while it has the honey on its face. Removing the honey costs 4 AP.
