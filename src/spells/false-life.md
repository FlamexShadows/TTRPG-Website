The caster coats themselves in vitality allowing them to take more damage. This spell increases and adds to the caster's Physical and Magical armor by 5. Additionally, If the caster is undead, they gain temporary flesh allowing them to appear alive. This skin is illusionary and has no further effect on any spells, magic items, or other effects used on the caster.
