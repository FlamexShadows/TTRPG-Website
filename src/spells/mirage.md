---
name: "Mirage"
range: "1 Kilometer"
ap: "3"
rank: "Major"
school: "Otherworldly"
subtype: "Illusion"
requirements: "Words of Power, Sigils"
duration: "1 Week"
concentration: "No"
---
The caster creates illusionary terrain that alters the appearance, sound and smell and all other senses in the environment in the area of 1 kilometer. The caster can make a desert appear as a lush forest or grasslands appear as rocky terrain or make a river cut into a lake in the desert. Alternatively, the caster can make the area appear alien and devoid of logic. Any creature that enters the area must succeed on a survival check or become lost in the area as they are magically looped in circles making escape impossible. The creature may repeat this check once per day on a successful check the creature and any creature that follows it are able to successfully leave the area.
