---
name: "Summoner's Circle"
range: "Touch"
casting_time: "1 Hour"
rank: "Adept"
school: "Arcanomancy"
subtype: "Apotropaic"
requirements: "Holy or Unholy Relic worth 500 Gold, Salt, Sigils"
duration: "24 Hours"
concentration: "No"
---
The caster creates a ritual circle imbuing it with magical power that traps a creature inside. Any creature summoned by the caster that answers the call is trapped within the circle. The trapped creature is incapable of leaving or damaging the salt circle while it is whole, and the spell's duration is active. Creatures trapped by this spell are aware if it is currently active. Most creatures that answer summons are wary of summoner circles. Creatures trapped within a summoner circle are less willing to negotiate while it is active, resulting in all charisma checks taking a penalty equal to the threat rating of the summon. When upcast Summoner's Circle turns the duration to 1 month at Major and 1 year at grand.
