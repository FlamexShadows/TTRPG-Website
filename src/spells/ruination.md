The caster targets a creature attempting to unmake its essence. The target must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save or become severed. If it fails, the save by 5 or more the creature dies, and its essence erased. A creature killed by ruination cannot be resurrected by any means short of a Genesis followed by a resurrection spell or by wishing upon a star.
