The caster chooses ice that weighs no more than 10 kilograms within range, reshaping it to a form they desire. This can change the shape of ice to become a sculpture, geometric shape, become cloudy or clear. This can alter ice in more diverse ways including animating decorations or small sculptures. Ice shows animated in this way last no longer than 1 minute. When upcast to Adept or higher the weight of the allowed ice shape doubles for each tier higher.
