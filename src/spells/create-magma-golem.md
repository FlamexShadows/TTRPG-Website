<div class="spell-text-html">
<p>The caster transforms magma into a golem. The golem has 10 hit points per kilogram of magma used and can be used to a maximum of 10 kilograms. The golem must remain in an area of extreme heat or begin cooling taking 10 damage at the start of each of its turns. The golem can only make skill checks that are Finesse, Fortitude, and Strength checks though it is still subject to Resistance (Mind) saves. The golem uses half the casters spell casting modifier for all checks, attack and damage rolls.</p>
<p>The Magma golem is vulnerable to cold and immune to psychic and fire damage. This golem still counts as a golem for the purposes of create golem spell limitations.</p>
<p>A golem created by Create Magma Golem can only be healed by being having heat object cast onto it healing the golem for 2d6 health per casting of the spell.</p>
