name: "Embers of Reflection"
range: "Self"

rank: "Minor"
school: "Pyromancy"
subtype: "Ash"
requirements: "Words of Power, Sigils"
duration: "10 Minutes"
concentration: "Yes"
---
The caster coats themselves in smoldering ash granting them resistance 10 to fire damage. Up to the damage mitigated, the caster may redirect any fire damage to any creature or object it can see within 15 meters of themselves. If reflected at a creature they must succeed on a Evasion save or take the fire damage.
