---
name: "Genetic Memory"
range: "Touch"
casting_time: "1 Hour"
rank: "Grand"
school: "Biomancy"
subtype: "Evolution"
requirements: "Words of Power, Sigils, 1 Mutagen"
duration: "Instant"
concentration: "No"
---
The caster consumes a sample of a creature's flesh and unwinds the memory written in its blood. The caster permanently gains one non-magical trait of that creature, such as gills, Dark Sight, webbed limbs, or venomous fangs. A caster can only hold one trait gained this way at a time; gaining a new trait replaces the old.
