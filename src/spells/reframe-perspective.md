---
name: "Reframe Perspective"
range: "5 meters"
ap: "3"
rank: "Minor"
school: "Enchantment"
subtype: "Charm"
requirements: "Sigils"
duration: "10 Minutes"
concentration: "No"
---
The caster targets a creature within range. The creature must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save or have its attitude towards the caster shifted to the attitude of the caster more negative or positive. If the target is already hostile toward the creature the spell fails to take effect.
