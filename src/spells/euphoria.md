ap: "3"

school: "Enchantment"
subtype: "Charm"
requirements: "Sigils"
duration: "10 Minutes"

The caster targets a creature within range attempting to overwhelm it with pleasure. The target creature must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save or become stunned for the duration of the spell. The target may repeat its save at the end of each of its turns.
