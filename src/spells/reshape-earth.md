The caster reshapes the earth around them. Reshape earth's primary purpose is to move the earth weighing no more than 10,000 kilograms. The caster can push this magic further beyond this point if they wish, risking their body and mind in the process due to the intense strain. A caster may terraform the terrain creating a mountain, a volcano, a vast and grand cave system or destroying one. They may also transform the land filling it with rich nutrients and cause plant life to flourish. In addition, the terraformed land can be filled with exotic materials such as diamonds, jade, gold, platinum, and other valuable resources the caster desires that occur naturally in the soil. If the caster performs such a great feat, they must roll a percentile die. On a 60 or higher the strain on their body and mind is too much for them. The caster permanently loses 6 in their casting modifier and memory and loses the ability to cast Geokinesis spells permanently.
