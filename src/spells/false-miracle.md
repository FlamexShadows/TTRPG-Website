The caster weaves a deceptive semblance of the divine, drawing upon the corrupting influence of the underworld granting another creature what it desires. An object materializes or a seemingly wondrous effect is made by the caster as they finish casting the spell functioning identically to <a href="upon-a-star.html">Upon a Star</a>. The produced effect is never truly what the target desires though the twisted nature determined by the caster. The miracle presents itself immediately while its corruption may not present itself immediately possibly taking, days, months or even years.
