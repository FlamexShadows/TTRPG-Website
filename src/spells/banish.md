The caster targets one creature it can see not native to the plane it currently inhabits. If the creature has no magical armor, the creature must succssed on a Resistance (Mind) save. On a failure, the creature is instantly banished back to its home plane. On a success, the creature becomes immune to the effects of a banish spell for the next 24 hours. When upcast the caster selects an additional target for each tier higher.
