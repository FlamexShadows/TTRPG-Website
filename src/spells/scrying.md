---
name: "Scrying"
range: "Self"
casting_time: "1 Minute"
rank: "Adept"
school: "Chronomancy"
subtype: "Divination"
requirements: "Sigils, Crystal ball worth at least 1000 GP"
duration: "1 Minute"
concentration: "No"
---
The caster uses the crystal ball to see through the past. The caster chooses a creature they know of and have a body part or item of importance to the creature they wish to scry upon. The creature must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save or have an invisible sensor appear next to them. The sensor can be seen by any creature that can see invisible creatures such as Witch Sight.
