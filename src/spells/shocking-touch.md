---
name: "Shocking Touch"
range: "Touch"
ap: "2"
rank: "Minor"
school: "Aeromancy"
subtype: "Lightning"
requirements: "Sigils"
duration: "1 Minute"
concentration: "No"
---
The caster touches a creature flooding it with electricity. The caster makes a Magic Attack, and, on a hit, the creature takes 2d6+Casting Modifier air damage. If the creature has no magical armor, it must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or become shocked for 1 minute.
