concentration: "Yes"

The caster attempts to bind the target's shadows in place. The creature must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save or become paralyzed for the duration of the spell. A creature may repeat this save at the start of each of its turns. This effect ends when the creature takes damage or succeeds on its save. This spell cannot be upcast.
