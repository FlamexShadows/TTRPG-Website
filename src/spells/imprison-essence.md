---
name: "Imprison Essence"
range: "Touch"
casting_time: "Reaction"
rank: "Major"
school: "Pneumamancy"
subtype: "Severance"
requirements: "Words of Power, A soul gem worth 1000 GP"
duration: "Permanent"
concentration: "No"
---
The caster attempts to imprison a creature's soul that was killed within the same turn that isn't severed into a soul gem. The target must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save or have its soul imprisoned in the gem. While imprisoned the creature's soul cannot be freed unless the gem is dispelled, broken, or the soul is replaced by a Genesis spell or freed by a wishing upon a star. While holding a soul gem with an imprisoned essence the wielder gains a bonus 10 true armor (to a maximum of 100). If this true armor is reduced to 0 the gem shatters and the essence is freed.

The caster who made the Soul Gem may speak to the imprisoned essence. The soul is under no obligation to tell the truth or even speak. But if the soul does lie while in the gem the caster knows.
