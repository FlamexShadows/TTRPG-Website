---
name: "Bind Creature"
range: "10 Meters"
casting_time: "1 Hour"
rank: "Adept"
school: "Arcanomancy"
subtype: "Apotropaic"
requirements: "Words of Power"
duration: "1 Week"
concentration: "No"
---
The caster spends one hour uninterrupted binding an angel, fiend, or spirit to its service. At the end of the hour, the target must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save. On a successful save the creature becomes immune to the spell from the caster permanently. On a failed save, the creature is magically compelled into the service of the caster. While bound, the creature follows all orders the caster gives to the best of it's ability.

A creature is only able to hold 1 creature bound to its service at a time.
