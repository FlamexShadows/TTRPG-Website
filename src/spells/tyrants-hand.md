duration: "10 Minute"
concentration: "Yes"
---
The caster conjures a large fist of gold that they can control as long as the spells duration is active that mimics the movements of one of the caster's free hands of choice. While conjured the hand takes up the space of a large creature. The fist can move up to 5 meters for 2 AP and make an attack dealing 1d8 bludgeoning damage on a hit.
