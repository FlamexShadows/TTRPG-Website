range: "Self"

rank: "Grand"

subtype: "Transformation"
requirements: "Sigils, Words of Power"
duration: "1 Minute"
concentration: "Yes"
---
The caster attempts to ascend one element of choice between fire, air, earth or cold. The caster's spells ignore immunity and resistance to the associated element in addition to the following effects.

White Flames: The flames of the caster burn so brightly that each creature struck by fire damage must succeed a Resistance (Body) save or become blind for 1 minute.

Violet Ice: The effected creature begins to shut down from the extreme cold. The creature must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or become slowed for 1 minute.

Red Lightning: The caster's lightning is empowered with the vile magic. A creature that is struck by red lightning must succeed a Resistance (Body) save or become cursed for 1 minute.

Petrifying Earth: A creature struck by Petrifying Earth must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or have its maximum AP reduced by 1 until the end of its next turn. If its maximum AP is reduced to 0 it becomes petrified permanently.
