---
name: "Unholy Burst"
range: "10 Meters"
ap: "4"
rank: "Minor"
school: "Demonology"
subtype: "Torment"
requirements: "Words of Power, Sigils"
duration: "Instant"
concentration: "No"
---
The caster points its finger at a creature causing it to bubble with unholy energy. The target must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save or take 2d6 unholy damage and become fearful of the caster until the end of it's next turn. The target can repeat its save at the end of each of its turns.
