name: "Electric Pulse"
range: "Self"
ap: "3"
rank: "Adept"
school: "Aeromancy"
subtype: "Wind"
requirements: "Words of Power"
duration: "Instant"
concentration: "No"
---
The caster creates a pulse of electricity from themselves that electrocutes all within range. Any creature within 5 meters of the caster must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or take 6d10 air damage or half as much on a success. Any creature that has no magical armor that takes damage becomes shocked.
