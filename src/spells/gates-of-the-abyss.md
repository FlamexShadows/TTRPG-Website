The caster summons 3 portals that are 2 meters wide and 10 meters long rifts at points of their choosing. From the portal a bright light appears before sucking in a creature's source. Each creature within the area of this effect must make a Resistance (Body) save or take 10d10 magic damage and lose 2d10 mana and 1d12 stamina.
