Amongst the most powerful spells a caster can evoke is to create a demi-plane. The caster opens a portal to a small plane of their possession called a demi-plane. The demi-plane is a 1-kilometer space that can be arranged, decorated, and customized to the caster's will. Inside the demi-plane are 100 servants that follow the caster's orders. The servants may only perform small unskilled tasks. A caster may control up to 2 demi planes at a time. If a 3rd is created the caster's first demi-plane is destroyed to make room for the 3rd.

Time: The caster may alter the flow of time inside a demiplane. This may be sped up or slowed down upon its creation and cannot be changed.<br>
Gravity: The gravity in the demiplane can be altered. This gravity can be increased or decreased<br>
Objects created in the demiplane created by its casting are incapable of leaving.
