ap: "2"

school: "Demonology"
subtype: "Possession"
requirements: "Words of Power, Sigils"
duration: "1 Minute"
concentration: "No"
---
Sacrificing 40 health the caster performs a forbidden chant allowing a fiend to possess a creature within range granting it the strength of a fiend. While possessed the fiend grants a +10 to a statistic of the caster's choice and the creature counts as evil against spells, maneuvers and unholy damage.
