---
name: "Sleep"
range: "15 Meters"
ap: "4"
rank: "Minor"
school: "Arcanomancy"
subtype: "Apotropaic"
requirements: "Words of Power"
duration: "1 Minute"
concentration: "No"
---
The caster attempts to send a creature it can see within range into a magical slumber. If the creature has no magical armor, it must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save. On a failure, the creature is rendered unconscious until the end of the spell's duration. If the creature takes any damage or a creature spends 2 AP to shake the creature awake, the spell ends and the creature wakes up.
