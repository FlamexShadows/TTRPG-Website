The caster creates a 15 meter radius 100 meter tall cylinder that devastates all it touches. If the tornado is casted at a point where it cannot fit, the spell fails and the mana and AP is wasted. This spell functions as <a href="gust.html">Gust</a> and in addition any loose objects are sucked into by the tornado and scattered. Lost items require at least 1 hour of searching for each DC 60 sense environment rolled to find the item.<br>
Creatures within 10 meters of the tornado that weigh less than 1000 kilograms must make a overpower check at the start of each of their turns. On a failure the creature is shoved an amount of meters equal to the caster's primary statistic.<br>
All structures smaller than 10x10 meters is instantly destroyed.
