---
name: "Maze of Elsewhere"
range: "15 Meters"
ap: "5"
rank: "Adept"
school: "Otherworldly"
subtype: "Dimension"
requirements: "Words of Power, Sigils"
duration: "1 Minute"
concentration: "No"
---
The caster banishes a creature within range into a shifting labyrinth folded between worlds. The target must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save or vanish from the world. While in the maze the target can do nothing on its turn except make a Resistance (Mind) save to find the way out; on a success, or when the spell ends, it reappears in the space it left or the nearest open space.
