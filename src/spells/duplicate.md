---
name: "Duplicate"
range: "Touch"
ap: "2"
rank: "Minor"
school: "Otherworldly"
subtype: "Illusion"
requirements: "Sigils"
duration: "1 Hour"
concentration: "Yes"
---
The caster touches a creature or object creating a duplicate of it. The duplicate has no mass and physical interaction such as touching the duplicate is impossible. From a glance, the object is indistinguishable from the original. To see the duplicate is an illusion a creature may roll an Inspect Object. On a successful check, the creature determines the object is illusionary. The caster can move the illusion in any way even in ways that such ways that original cannot. Physical interaction with the object automatically reveals its illusionary state such as if a creature were to try picking it up or common deduction can be used to identify it is fake such as a weight on a scale having no effect on its balance. Although the object can be determined fake by physical interaction objects like flames and ice still give off a slight heat or a chill.
