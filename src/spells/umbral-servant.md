The caster separates their shadow creating a simulacrum of themselves. The servant is able to perform simple tasks such as cooking food, washing dishes or doing laundry until the duration of the spell ends. At the end of the spell's duration the Servant is returned to the casters side potentially leaving any task it was performing unfinished.
