---
name: "Razor Talon"
range: "Self"
ap: "4"
rank: "Minor"
school: "Transmutation"
subtype: "Conjuration"
requirements: "Words of Power, Sigils"
duration: "1 Minute"
concentration: "Yes"
---
The caster conjures a bird with 2 wings as sharp as razors. The bird must stay within 10 meters of the caster or the spell ends. While summoned the caster may have the bird fly by cutting a creature using a magic attack roll. On a hit the creature takes 1d10+casting modifier slashing damage.
