The caster attempts to condense an object or creature. If targeting an object, the object must be no larger than 10 meters or weigh more than 200 kilograms. The target object halves in size. This effect does not change the weight or amount of substance present but simply makes it take up more or less space. If the target is a creature, it increases or decreases its size category by one. If used on a creature, the target must succeed a Resistance (Body) save or take 2d6 bludgeoning damage. If cast on a creature the spell lasts for 10 minutes. When upcast the caster the fourths at Major or eights the size of an object at Grand. If used on a creature, it decreases the size category of a creature by an additional size for each tier upcast.
