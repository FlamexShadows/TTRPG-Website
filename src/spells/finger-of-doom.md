---
name: "Finger of Doom"
range: "20 Meters"
ap: "4"
rank: "Major"
school: "Arcanomancy"
subtype: "Destruction"
requirements: "Words of Power"
duration: "Instant"
concentration: "No"
---
The caster points its finger at a creature within range springing a red beam that attempts to pierce the target's source. The creature targeted must succeed on an Evasion save. On a failure the creature takes 10d10+Casting Modifier True damage. If the creature is reduced to 0 health, it is instantly slain.
