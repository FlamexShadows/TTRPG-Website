name: "Mold Metal Ore"
range: "Touch"
ap: "4"
rank: "Minor"
school: "Geomancy"
subtype: "Metal"
requirements: "Words of Power"
duration: "Instant"
concentration: "No"
---
The caster takes metal ore and molds it into a more useful form or shape. The spell can alter up to 1 meter of ore per casting. The caster can make simple shapes such as bars, plates, or basic tools. More complex shapes such as weapons or armor require a successful Intelligence (Crafting) check with a DC of 15.
