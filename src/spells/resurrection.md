---
name: "Resurrection"
range: "Touch"
ap: "1"
rank: "Major"
school: "Heavenly"
subtype: "Mercy"
requirements: "Sigils, Words of Power, Herbs worth 10,000 Gold"
duration: "Instant"
concentration: "No"
---
The caster touches a creature that has been dead for no longer than a year. Contacting the soul of the dead creature in its afterlife it is offered a path back to its body. If accepted the creature returns to life rolling 5 of its health die adding its fortitude score Health. This spell fails on a creature with a body that cannot survive or whose soul is trapped or destroyed. This spell cannot be upcast
