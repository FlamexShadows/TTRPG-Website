The caster transforms a willing creature within range into a partially demonic form. The target gains a a natural melee weapon that are proficient with. The weapon has the deadly 1 and enchanted 1 properties and uses the caster's casting modifier instead of strength. While transformed, the target has resistance 5 unholy damage for the duration of the spell. On a hit, the target takes 1d12+casting modifier unholy damage. When upcast the resistance doubles for each tier and the level of deadly and enchanted each increase one level.
