---
name: "Cackling End"
range: "10 meters"
ap: "4"
rank: "Grand"
school: "Demonology"
subtype: "Curse"
requirements: "Words of Power, Sigils"
duration: "Instant"
concentration: "No"
---
The caster summons the echo of Misery in front of its intended target. If the target has no magical armor, horrors of the underworld flood the mind of the target. The target of the spell becomes cursed and must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save. If the victim fails, the target is then haunted by the demon. While haunted the target suffers a -30 penalty to its Charisma, Wits, and Intelligence. The target madly cackles speaking incoherent babbling as instead of speaking leaving it incapable of performing words of power or forming a coherent sentence. The target may repeat its save if it is affected by a Grand Purge or any other effect that performs an exorcism.
