---
name: "Sphere of Agony"
range: "Self"
ap: "4"
rank: "Minor"
school: "Demonology"
subtype: "Torment"
requirements: "Words of Power, Sigils"
duration: "1 Minute"
concentration: "Yes"
---
The caster creates a sphere around themselves that emits the cries of tormented souls that can be heard up to 50 meters away. The souls spread their agony to all creature within the 1 meter of the caster. Any creatures that starts their turn or enters its range has spectral hands lash out clawing them. The target must make a Evasion save or take 1d6 unholy damage and 1d6 slashing damage.
