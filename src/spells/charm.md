---
name: "Charm"
range: "Self"
ap: "4"
rank: "Minor"
school: "Enchantment"
subtype: "Charm"
requirements: "Words of Power, Sigils"
duration: "1 Hour"
concentration: "No"
---
The caster attempts to charm a creature within 5 meters of them. The creature must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save or be charmed for the duration. The charmed creature regards the caster as a friendly acquaintance. When the spell ends, the creature knows it was charmed by the caster. The spell ends if the caster or any of their companions do anything harmful to the charmed creature. The creature is immune to the caster's charm for 24 hours after the spell ends.
