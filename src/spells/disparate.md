ap: "4"

school: "Pneumamancy"
subtype: "Severance"
requirements: "Words of Power"
duration: "1 Minute"
concentration: "No"

The caster shoots a beam of magical energy that attempts to force a creature's soul from its vessel. The caster makes an attack roll. On a hit, the target takes 2d6 magic damage and roll a Resistance (Mind) save. On a failure the creature becomes severed for the duration of the spell.
