name: "Cloud Mind"
range: "10 Meters"
ap: "3"
rank: "Adept"
school: "Enchantment"
subtype: "Charm"
requirements: "Words of Power, Sigils"
duration: "1 Minute"
concentration: "Yes"
---
The caster targets an area creating transparent pink fog in a 5 meter radius cloud that muddles the minds of those within the area. Any creature inside the sphere must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save or take 4d6 psychic damage or half as much on a success. A creature that failed its save that has no magic armor while inside the fog, it is incapable of concentrating while inside the cloud.
