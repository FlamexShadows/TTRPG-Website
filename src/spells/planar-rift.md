<div class="spell-text-html">
<p>The caster creates a rift connecting two planes together, allowing travel between the two worlds.
The rift appears as a 1-meter-long bright orange crack in the air that emits a hymn with a sound that correlates with the plane it connects to on the other side.
The portal is a two-way portal and appears at a random place in the other world.</p>
<h3>Material Requirements</h3>
<ul>
<li>Border World: Etherstone</li>
<li>7 Heavens: Halo Crystals</li>
<li>Underworld: Hellstone</li>
<li>Spirit World: Luminite</li>
<li>Empty: Emoulitatie</li>
</ul>
