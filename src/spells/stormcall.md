---
name: "Stormcall"
range: "Self"
casting_time: "1 Minute"
rank: "Grand"
school: "Aeromancy"
subtype: "Lightning"
requirements: "Words of Power, Sigils"
duration: "1 Hour"
concentration: "No"
---
The caster calls a black storm that gathers over 1 kilometer of sky. Rain and wind impair vision, and flames in the open are snuffed out. While the storm lasts, the caster can spend 2 AP to call a bolt of lightning down on a point it can see. Each creature within 2 meters of the point must make an Evasion save, taking 8d8 air damage on a failure or half as much on a success.
