name: "Neural Shock"
range: "10 Meters"
ap: "3"
rank: "Minor"
school: "Aeromancy"
subtype: "Lightning"
requirements: "Words of Power, SIgils"
duration: "Instant"
concentration: "No"
---
The caster creates a surge of electrical energy that jolts the nervous systems of a creature within range. The creature must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save or lose concentration on any spells, maneuvers or items they are maintaining.

When upcast, the caster doubles the number of targets for each tier above minor.
