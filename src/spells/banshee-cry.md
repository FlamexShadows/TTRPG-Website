ap: "5"
rank: "Major"
school: "Necromancy"
subtype: "Death"
requirements: "Words of Power, Sigils"
duration: "Instant"
concentration: "No"
---
The caster condenses magic to mimics a banshee wail's necrotic energy in a 5 meter radius circle at a point they can see in range. All creatures within the circle that are able to hear must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save or take 18d12 necrotic damage. This damage ignores resistance and a reduces a creature's maximum hit points by the amount taken until they finish a full rest.
