The caster creates a bolt of chaotic magic that launches at a target, they choose within range. The caster makes a magic attack roll. On a hit if the creature takes 4d6 randomly determined damage and must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or suffer the condition applied for 1 minute. The creature may repeat its save at the start of each of its turns. The caster rolls 2 six sided die one to determine the damage of the spell and the condition applied from the spell.
