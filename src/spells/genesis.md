---
name: "Genesis"
range: "Self"
casting_time: "24 Hours"
rank: "Grand"
school: "Pneumamancy"
subtype: "Manipulation"
requirements: "Words of Power, Soul gem"
duration: "Instant"
concentration: "No"
---
The caster restores creatures they have met personally or frees a soul held within a soul gem they used while casting this spell. The gem can be broken by the caster with no check required freeing the soul into the cosmos allowing it to inhabit a body.
