A direct translation for 'The End' from empatic the caster attempts to tear a hole in creation allowing The Arbiters of the empty to gaze into creation in a 5 meter radius cylinder. Any creature within the radius must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save or be erased. Any objects, and non living matter aside from relics that arent worn or carried inside the radius are removed creating a 100 meter deep hole in the ground any creature that remained in the circle falls into. A creature that is erased cannot be restored.
