---
name: "Swarm of Insects"
range: "10 Meters"
ap: "6"
rank: "Major"
school: "Transmutation"
subtype: "Creation"
requirements: "Sigils"
duration: "1 Hour"
concentration: "Yes"
---
The caster designates a creature or object to become infested with insects. The insects deal 4d10 True damage to the target and the creature must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save or become fearful. A creature may spend 4 AP and roll a sleight of hand check to remove the swarm infesting them.
