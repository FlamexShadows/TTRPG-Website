---
name: "Undertow"
range: "15 Meters"
ap: "3"
rank: "Adept"
school: "Hydromancy"
subtype: "Water"
requirements: "Words of Power, Sigils"
duration: "Instant"
concentration: "No"
---
A surge of churning water rushes along the ground in a line 10 meters long from a point within range. Each creature in the line takes 3d6 bludgeoning damage and must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or be dragged 5 meters toward a point of the caster's choice along the line and knocked down.
