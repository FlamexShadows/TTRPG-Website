The caster tears a 3 meter wide breach into the underworld at a point it can see. At the start of each of the caster's turns, a lesser fiend crawls out of the breach under the caster's command, to a maximum of 3 fiends. The fiends use the stats of conjure fiend as if cast one tier lower than hellgate.
