ap: "4"

duration: "1 Minute"
concentration: "No"
---
The caster creates a flash of bright light that blinds creatures. The caster chooses a point in range creating a bright light in a 10 meter radius sphere. All creatures within range must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or become blind for 1 minute. The creature may repeat the check at the end of each of its turns.
