name: "Incarnate of Shards"
range: "20 meters"
ap: "5"
rank: "Adept"
school: "Geomancy"
subtype: "Incarnate of Shards"
requirements: "Words of Power, Shards of a glass, or gemstones"
duration: "10 Minutes"
concentration: "Yes"
---
The caster summons an incarnate made of fragments of its material that occupies a large creature's space at the origin point of the fragments. The incarnate has 5 times as much health as its caster's casting modifier. Whenever a creature within 5 meters of the caster choice is the target of an attack roll, the incarnate intercepts the attack granting them a bonus to their AC of 5. If the attack still hits the Incarnate reduces the damage by half (rounded up) and takes the damage to its own health as it absorbs part of the blast. For 3 AP or a reaction the caster can move the incarnate up to 6 meters in any direction but to a maximum of 20 meters. The Incarnate still defends targets up to 25 meters from its caster but if it moves to occupy a space 21 or further meters the spell ends. When Incarnate of Shards ends as it crumbles to dust. When upcast this spell increases the AC by 5 for every upcast level.
