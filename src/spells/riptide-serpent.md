name: "Riptide Serpent"
range: "20 Meters"
ap: "6"
rank: "Major"
school: "Hydromancy"
subtype: "Water"
requirements: "Words of Power, Sigils"
duration: "1 Minute"
concentration: "Yes"
---
The caster shapes a great serpent of rushing water that occupies a large creature's space within range. The caster can spend 2 AP to command the serpent to move 10 meters or strike a creature within 3 meters of it. On a strike the caster makes an attack roll; on a hit the target takes 4d8 bludgeoning damage and must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or be Grappling with the serpent's coils.
