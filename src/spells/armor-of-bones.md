duration: "1 Minute"
concentration: "No"

The caster creates armor from corpses from around them coating them in the bones of the dead. The caster gains physical armor equal to their casting modifier for each creature killed within the hour within range. Any armor gained from this spell disappears after the duration ends. Armor gained from armor of bones can go over the targets maximum. This spell cannot be upcast.
