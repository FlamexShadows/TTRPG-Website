Create Crystal Golem


AP Cost: 6
Spell Rank: Major
Class: Geomancy
Subtype: Crystal
Range: Touch
Requirements: Sigils, Words of Power
Duration: Permanent
Concentration: No

The caster transforms crystals into a golem. The golem has 10 hit points per kilogram of crystal used and can be used to a maximum of 10 kilograms. The golem can only make skill checks that are Dexterity, Fortitude, and Strength checks though it is still subject to Resistance (Mental) Checks. The golem uses half the casters spell casting modifier for all checks, attack and damage rolls.

The Crystal golem is vulnerable to physical and immune to psychic and earth damage. This golem still counts as a golem for the purposes of create golem spell limitations.

A golem created by Create Crystal Golem can only be healed by being submerged in liquid and having crystalize cast on it healing 2d6 health per casting.
