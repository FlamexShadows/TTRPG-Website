Understanding Martial Maneuvers

Martial Maneuvers represent skilled instances or sequences of attacks, movements, or defenses. Similar to spells, they are categorized into tiers, ranging from Basic to Master. However, unlike spells where certain branches might be inaccessible, a creature must possess the physical capability to perform a Martial Maneuver. This often requires the creature to have sufficiently high physical stats to execute or develop it.

Progression of Maneuvers

Martial Maneuvers cannot be "upcast" in the way spells can. Instead, they can be progressed. Progression is typically indicated by a maneuver having a lower-tier variant as a prerequisite.

A progressed Martial Maneuver can incorporate its previous variants as part of the evolved maneuver's execution. Importantly, evolved maneuvers take up the same maneuver slot as the prerequisite maneuver, meaning they do not require additional slots to learn.
