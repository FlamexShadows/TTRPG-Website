Trickshot

Basic
AP: 4
Requirements: Ranged Weapon
Type: Attack
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The martialist create a fancy shot. They choose one of the following options.

Close Call
The martialist attempts to intimidate(STR) a creature. You may use finesse instead of STR.

Show Off
The martialist attempts an Allure check using finesse instead of charisma.

Disarm
The martialist attempts to disarm a foe. The creature must succeed on an evasion save or drop its weapon.
