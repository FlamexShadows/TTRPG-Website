Statistics

Basic Rules, revised November 3, 2026

Every creature has seven statistics. Each score gives a modifier: modifier = (score − 8) ÷ 2, rounded down. A score of 8 gives +0, 10 gives +1, and 17 gives +4.

Strength

A character's physical ability to lift, carry and push objects and creatures. A low strength indicates a creature has little capability to overpower others while a high strength is gained through design or long and hard physical practice and training. Examples of creatures with high strength would be dragons, elephants, and giants.

Finesse

Finesse is the ability to perform quick or precise actions. A creature with high finesse is extremely fast and agile. A creature with low finesse will be slow and have poor hand eye coordination. Examples of high Finesse are flies, cheetahs, and birds.

Fortitude

Fortitude is how durable and resilient a creature is to the environment and attacks from other creatures. A creature with high fortitude will survive harsher weather and is harder to kill. Examples of creatures with high fortitude are tardigrades, hippos, wolverines.

Charisma

Charisma is a creature's ability to perform socially and its creativity. This shows in smooth talking, good looks, previous connections, and new and interesting ideas. High-Charisma creatures tend to hold high social status. Those who do not lack either the chance or the interest. Creatures with high Charisma are dogs, demons, and deities.

Wits

Wits are a creature's basic senses. A creature with high wits will make rational decisions and have the ability to perceive, navigate and track creatures in various environments. Creatures with high wits include eagles, foxes, crows.

Intelligence

Intelligence is a creature's ability to learn, remember, create, and recall information. A creature with higher Intelligence makes use of tools and solves the problems presented to it. Examples of creatures with high Intelligence are humans, octopuses, and dragons.

Memory

Memory sets how many spells, Martial Maneuvers, and extra skill ranks you learn. It has no effect on recall or Knowledge checks. Each +1 of Memory modifier grants 1 extra skill rank and 1 extra learned spell or Martial Maneuver, your choice.
