Mental Acrobatics


Range: Touch
Casting Time: 1 Minute
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Enchantment
Subtype: Charm
Requirements: Sigils
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster attempts to charm a creature into believing something they otherwise wouldnt. After 1 minute of conversation the target must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) check or rationalize a simple idea such as convicting a target 1+1=3.
