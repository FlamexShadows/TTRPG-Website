Enslave


Range: 10 meters
AP Cost: 4
Spell Rank: Grand
Class: Enchantment
Subtype: Charm
Requirements: Words of Power, Golden Chain worth 1000 Gold per hit die of the target consumed upon casting
Duration: Until Dispelled
Concentration: No

The caster targets one creature within range presenting the golden chain. The target must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) check or become Charmed by the caster. While charmed they are dominated until cured by a Purge or magic of similar effect.
