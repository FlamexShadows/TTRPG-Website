Conjure Fiend


AP Cost: 6
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Demonology
Subtype: Torment
Range: 5 Meters
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils, An obsidian skull worth at least 750 gold
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: Yes

The caster summons a fiend from the Underworld at a point it can see in range. If the fiend is not able to fit in the spot its summoned the spell fails wasting the casters mana. The fiend is hostile to all creatures including the caster and will attack the nearest creature until it is reduced to 0 hit points, the caster's concentration is broken, or the spells duration ends. The fiend is banished to the Underworld when the caster's concentration is broken. All of the fiends DC's use the caster's Magic Potency DC. When upcast the spell only modifies the stats effected by its tier. The fiend has the following stats:

  • AC: Casting Modifier+10
  • Size: Medium
  • Armor: Casting Modifier*5
  • HP: 50+Casting Modifier
  • Speed: 5 Meters
  • STR: (5*Spell Tier) +Casting Modifier
  • DEX: Casting Modifier
  • CON: (3*Spell Tier) +Casting Modifier
  • INT: Casting Modifier
  • WIS: Casting Modifier
  • CHA: Casting Modifier
  • Damage Immunities: Poison, Unholy
  • Condition Immunities: Poisoned
  • Senses: Darksight 60ft
  • Languages: Bellsybabble

    • Actions:
    Range: 1 Meter
    Attack Modifier: (5*Spell Tier)+Str Modifier
    Attack Damage: 2d10+Str Modifier+2d4 Unholy

    Abilities:
    Frightening Presence: Each creature of the fiend's choice that is within 5 meters of the fiend and aware of it must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) check or become frightened for 1 minute. A frightened target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. If a target's saving throw is successful or the effect ends for it, the target is immune to the fiend's Frightening Presence.

    Bloodbath: When the fiend reduces a creature to 0 hit points it can make an additional attack for no AP against a creature within 1 meter of itself.
