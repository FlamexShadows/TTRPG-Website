Venomous Sludge


AP Cost: 4
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Biomancy
Subtype: Evolution
Range: 10 Meters
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils, 1 Mutagen
Duration: 10 Minutes
Concentration: No

The caster coats a willing creature a thick venomous mucus. Any creature that makes a melee attack against the target must succeed on a Evasion check or take 2d6 poison damage. Any creature that swallows the target when covered must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or be forced to regurgitate the caster.
