Blinding Flash


Range: 30 Meters
AP Cost: 4
Spell Rank: Major
Class: Pyromancy
Subtype: Flame
Requirements: Sigils
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: No

The caster creates a flash of bright light that blinds creatures. The caster chooses a point in range creating a bright light in a 10 meter radius sphere. All creatures within range must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or become blinded for 1 minute. The creature may repeat the check at the end of each of its turns.
