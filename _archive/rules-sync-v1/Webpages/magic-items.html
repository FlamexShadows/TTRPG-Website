Magic Items

Basic Rules, revised November 3, 2026

A creature has a certain amount of slots it can use. This is its head, face, neck, body, wrists, waist, legs, feet, hands, 2 rings and 3 Miscellaneous slots. Magic items are classified by power of the item from weakest to strongest being faint, glimmering, exotic, legendary, mythic, and relics. While power makes an item harder to create it does not dictate the price as a powerful item can be more commonly produced than a less powerful item or its creation process can be less known.

  • Head: hats and helmets.
  • Face: anything worn on the face, such as glasses and masks.
  • Neck: anything worn on the neck, such as a necklace or scarf.
  • Body: anything worn over or under your armor, mostly large robes or cloaks.
  • Wrists: anything worn on your forearms, such as bracelets and arm sleeves.
  • Waist: mostly belts.
  • Legs: anything worn on your legs over or under pants, such as knee pads or compressions.
  • Feet: different kinds of shoes.
  • Hands: anything worn on the hands, such as gloves or wraps.
  • Rings: only ring fingers channel a ring's magic, so you wear 2 rings. Rituals in Lemara's church add more.
  • Miscellaneous: items not covered by the slots above, including weapons, armor, staves, and other objects. Miscellaneous items require attunement to function. You start with 3 attunement slots, and this number rises over time. Attuning an item requires knowledge of how it works and takes 10 minutes. Miscellaneous items you are not attuned to do not function, as their magic stays unresponsive.
