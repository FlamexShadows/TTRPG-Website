Hypnosis


AP Cost: 4
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Enchantment
Subtype: Charm
Range: 15 Meters
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: Yes

The caster attempts to influence all creatures within a 5 meter cube with mesmerizing lights. All creatures within the radius of these lights must succssed a Resistance (Mind) check or become stunned for 1 minute. This effect ends when a creature uses 3 AP to snap it out of its stupor, it takes damage or is subjected to a harmful effect.
