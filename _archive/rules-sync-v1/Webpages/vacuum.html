Vacuum


Range: 20 Meters
AP Cost: 4
Spell Rank: Major
Class: Aeromancy
Subtype: Wind
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: Yes

The caster targets a creature within range creating a vacuum devoid of air or water around it. If the creature that is targeted must breathe, it must succeed on an Resistance Body save. On a failure the air or water is pulled from the creature's lungs and or lungs and pulled outside the vacuum. A creature that failed it's save has disadvantage on all attack rolls and physical checks and saves for the duration of the spell.
