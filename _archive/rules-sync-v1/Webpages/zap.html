Zap


Range: 10 Meters
AP Cost: 3
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Aeromancy
Subtype: Lightning
Requirements: Sigils
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: No

The caster sends a bolt of lightning at a single creature. On a hit the creature takes 2d6+Casting Modifier air damage. If the creature has no magical armor, it must succeed on a Resistance (Body) check or become shocked for 1 minute.
