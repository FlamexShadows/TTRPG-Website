Projection


Range: Self
AP Cost: 1 Hour
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Pneumamancy
Subtype: Manipulation
Requirements: Words of Power, Incense
Duration: 10 Hours
Concentration: Yes

The caster performs a 1-hour long ritual that when completed ejects their soul from their vessel. For the next 10 hours, the caster's soul walks the border world. While in the border world, it cannot see further than 30 meters, interact with the material world or its body, and cannot be perceived by any creature without Supernatural or True vision.
