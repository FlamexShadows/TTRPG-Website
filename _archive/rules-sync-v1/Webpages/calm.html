Calm


Range: 10 Meters
AP Cost: 2
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Enchantment
Subtype: Charm
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: 10 Minutes
Concentration: Yes

The caster targets one creature it can see within range. The creature must succeed on a resistance (Mind) save or become calm. A creature while calm cannot be affected by the Fearful, Hopeless, Inspired, or Motivated conditions.
