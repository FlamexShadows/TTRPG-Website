Flame Wave


Range: 15 Meters
AP Cost: 5
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Pyromancy
Subtype: Flame
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster conjures a 5 meter cone of fire that all targets must succeed a Resistance Body save or take 6d4 fire damage. In addition, it begins burning. The target takes half damage on success.
