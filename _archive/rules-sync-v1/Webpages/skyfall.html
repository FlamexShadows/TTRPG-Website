Skyfall

Master
AP: 6
Requirements: Strength 30
Type: Attack
Range: 1000 meters
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The martialist takes to the air and gathers momentum, building up power as they soar or jump 1 kilometer into the sky. The creature can choose to last anywhere within 1000 meters origin. Any creature within 250 meters of the landing point must succeed on an Evasion save or take 40d12 bludgeoning damage. In addition if the martialist lands directly on a creature that fails its evasion save they take an additional 10d12 bludgeoning damage and must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or become crippled for 1 minute. A creature may repeat this save at the end of each of its turns.
