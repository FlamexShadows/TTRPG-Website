Hurl Fire


Range: 10 Meters
AP Cost: 2
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Pyromancy
Subtype: Flame
Requirements: Sulfur
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster targets one creature within range. The creature must succeed on an evasion save or take 1d6+Casting Modifier fire damage. Objects not worn or carried are lit ablaze by this spell.
