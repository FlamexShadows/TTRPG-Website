Understanding Spells

Spells are the result of a creature utilizing its source as mana, transforming that energy into magical effects. Spells follow many different trees, including: Arcanomancy, Heavenly, Otherworldly, Nature, Chronesis, Demonology, Enchantment, Necromancy, Transmutation, Hydrokinesis, Pyrokinesis, Geokinesis, and Aeromancy.

Spells are classified by the amount of mana required to cast them. These classifications are: Minor, Adept, Major, Grand, and Divine. Mortals are strictly forbidden by Lemara from casting Divine spells; only the divine are permitted to wield such power.

Access to certain magic is restricted. While anyone can cast spells from Necromancy, Transmutation, Hydrokinesis, Pyrokinesis, Geokinesis, and Aeromancy, other schools such as Heavenly, Demonology, Arcanomancy, Nature, Otherworldly, and Enchantment magic are exceptionally rare. These cannot be accessed unless the knowledge or power to cast them is granted to a creature through specific features or means.

Upcasting Spells

Magic is volatile, and spells can be empowered by channeling additional mana into them. The effects of upcasting spells are geometric; for instance, a spell upcasted from Minor to Grand is 16 times more potent. A creature must possess the ability to cast spells of the desired tier to successfully upcast a spell to that tier.

When a spell is upcast, and it doesn't have a specific upcast effect described, you can choose one of the following general enhancements (note: doubling spell effects are geometric):

  • Double Dice rolled
  • Double the range
  • Add a caster modifier (if not already included)
  • Increase Duration, as follows:
    • 1 Minute → 10 Minutes
    • 10 Minutes → 1 Hour
    • 1 Hour → 6 Hours
    • 6 Hours → 1 Day
    • 1 Day → 1 Week
    • 1 Week → 1 Month
    • 1 Month → 1 Year
    • 1 Year → Permanent
