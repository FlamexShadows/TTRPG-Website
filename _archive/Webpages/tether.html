Tether


Range: 10
AP Cost: 2
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Demonology
Subtype: Torment
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: 1 minute
Concentration: Yes

The caster links one target to itself. If the creature has no physical armor, it must succeed on a Resistance (Body) check or have its body tethered to the caster. While tethered if the target receives healing or receives armor it instead applies to the caster. The creature can repeat its check at the end of each turn. This spell cannot be upcast.
