Anagisa


AP Cost: 6

A direct translation for 'The End' from empatic the caster attempts to tear a hole in creation allowing The Arbiters of the empty to gaze into creation in a 5 meter radius cylinder. Any creature within the radius without magical armor must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) check or be erased. Any objects inside the radius such as doors, dirt, stone are removed creating a 100 meter deep hole in the ground any creature that remained in the circle falls into. A creature that is erased cannot be restored.
