Create Material


AP Cost: 4

The caster creates non exotic material worth no more than 100 Gold not exceeding a 1x1 meter cube. The material forms in the casters hand. If dangerous material such as Molten rock or a corrosive acid the caster would need adequate protection or suffer damage from the creation of such materials. Anything considered exotic is clarified by the Game Master but usually entails precious metals, gemstones, or rare ingredients.
