Ignite


AP Cost: 1

The caster chooses a point within two meters of themselves creating a 5 meter sphere generating a harmless wave of heat to all creatures but igniting all flammable objects within the sphere. Any creature wearing or carrying a flammable object takes 4d6 fire damage for armor and 1d6 for equipment or weapons worn or carried. Damage from equipment or armor may only be applied only once even if the creature is carrying multiple flammable equipment or is wearing multiple pieces of flammable armor.
