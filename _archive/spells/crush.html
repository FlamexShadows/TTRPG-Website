Crush


AP Cost: 2

The caster targets a piece of metal crushing it into a small ball. If used on a creature made of or wearing metal the creature takes 5d6 physical damage and must succeed on a Resistance (Body) check. On a failure the creature becomes crippled. The creature may reroll this save at the start of each of its turns on a success taking off its armor and no longer being crippled. Using Mend Metal to put the armor back to its original state or expand overpower removes crippled condition.
