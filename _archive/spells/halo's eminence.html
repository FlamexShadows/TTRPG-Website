Halo's Eminence


AP Cost: 4

Speaking the words of heavens, the caster gains aspects of the heavens based on their alignment.

Good
The caster grows a pair of heavenly white wings and adorns a celestial halo that strikes onlookers with awe. Charisma-based checks with good-aligned creatures gain a +20 bonus. Any evil creature within 20 meters of the caster that has no magical armor must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) check or become Hopeless against its Divine Light. A creature repeats this save at the start of each turn it takes until the spell ends. Additionally, the casted is granted a flight speed equal to double its base speed.

Evil
The caster grows a pair of decrepit raven black wings and adorn a cracked red halo that strikes onlookers with terror. Charisma based checks with evil aligned creatures gain a +20 bonus. Any non-evil creature within 20 meters of the caster that has no magical armor must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) check or become Hopeless against its Unholy Light. A creature repeats this save at the start of each turn it takes until the spell ends. Additionally, the casted is granted a flight speed equal to double its base speed
