Flame Web


AP Cost: 4

The caster shoots a web of flame centering at a point in range covering a 10 meter radius sphere. Any creature caught within the sphere must succeed on an Evasion check or take 4d6 fire damage and become Pinned. Each time a creature enters or starts a turn in the web it takes 2d6 fire damage and may make an Overpower check to remove the pinned condition. A creature that enters the area of the spell that fails its Overpower check becomes pinned.
