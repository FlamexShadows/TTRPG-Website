Ice Wall


AP Cost: 4

A solid wall of ice erupts at a point of choosing from the caster within range. The wall can reach up to 10 meters long and 5 meters high. If a creature is caught in the formation of the ice it must succeed on a resistance Physical check or take 5d6 cold damage and become frozen inside of the wall. On a successful save the creature takes half as much damage and is not freezing. The wall has 30 health, is immune to cold, poison, and psychic damage and vulnerable to fire damage. Any resistance checks the wall makes uses the spell casting modifier of the caster.
