Omnipresence


Casting Time: 24 Hours

Often used by archmagi to maintain a tower while in a council with the royal court. The caster creates 1d4+1 duplicates of themselves anywhere in the world they are aware of acting as if they were in the area of all the places at once. The caster acts as themselves through these duplicates. The caster can choose to cast spells through the duplicates and all duplicates share the caster's health and armor pools, mana, and Stamina.
