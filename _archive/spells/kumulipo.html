Kumulipo


AP Cost: 6

The caster creates a 5 meter radius origin at a point they can see in range. Each creature within 20 meters of the point must succeed on an Overpower check or be pulled to the origin of the spell. Any creature that touches, enters or starts its turn in origin point takes 20d12 magic damage. A creature reduced to 0 health dies as it merges with the Kumulipo increasing its radius by 20 and resetting its duration to 1 minute.
