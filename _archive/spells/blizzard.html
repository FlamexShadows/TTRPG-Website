Blizzard


AP Cost: 5

The caster creates a whirling storm for harsh winds and snow that impairs vision and causes creatures and objects in the area to be affected by extreme cold within 10 kilometers. At the start of its turn, any creature exposed to the blizzard takes 3d6 cold damage and if the creature has no magical armor must succeed on a Resistance (Body) check. On a failure the creature becomes freezing.
