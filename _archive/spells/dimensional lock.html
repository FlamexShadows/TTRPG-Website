Dimensional Lock


AP Cost: 2

The caster creates a translucent 100 meter cube centered at a point they choose. Any creature that attempts to teleport or cross planar boundaries from an Adept spell or advanced maneuvers or lower fails. Features from a Class or monster ability that is not either of those must roll a contested check the caster using their primary stat of their spell casting and the target's primary stat for the granted feature. For example, a slayer Hunter using Rapid Movement to escape would roll Wits against the casting modifier. When upcast to major blocks major spells and expert maneuvers and when upcast to grand blocks grand spells and master maneuvers.
