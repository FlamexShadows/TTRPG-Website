Ignore


AP Cost: 3

The caster targets a large group of creatures such as commoners, a herd of farm animals or any other target the GM deems susceptible as a target for the spell. More powerful individuals or those immune to charm remain unaffected by the spell. While the spell is active the crowd becomes unaware of the caster and up to 6 other creatures they designate within range for the duration. While walking through the crowd creatures will rationalize any interaction with creatures protected by the spell. For example, if a creature is bumped into by a protected creature, the person they came into contact with will conclude others in the area interacting with them bumped into them.
