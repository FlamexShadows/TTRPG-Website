Banish


AP Cost: 2 AP

The caster targets one creature it can see not native to the domain it currently inhabits. If the creature has no magical armor the two creatures make contested charisma checks. If the caster wins the creature is instantly banished back to its home domain. If the creature wins the spell fails and is immune to the effects of a banish spell for the next 24 hours. When upcast the caster selects an additional target for each tier higher.
