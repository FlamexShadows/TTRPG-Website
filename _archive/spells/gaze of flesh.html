Gaze of Flesh


Casting Time: 10 Minutes

The caster casts this spell and within 1 hour after casting has 1 hour to touch a creature marking it for later viewing. Once the caster touches a creature it becomes marked and any creature that inspects the target (Including Itself) may make a DC 20 Medicine check to spot the mark while viewing itself in a matter like a mirror or medical inspection. The creature may attempt to remove the mark by making a DC 15 Knowledge (Arcana). While marked the caster may attempt to scry on the marked target. The target must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) check or the caster is able to perceive the targets environment through its senses. The target is not aware the caster is crying on them. If the target succeeds on its check the mark dissipates and the spell ends.
