Name: Disparate


AP Cost: 4
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Pneumamancy
Subtype: Severance
Range: Touch
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: 1 Minute

The caster attempts to force a creature's soul from its vessel. If the target has no magical armor, it must succeed on an Evasion check or take 2d6 magic damage and roll a Resistance (Mind) check. On a failure the creature becomes severed for the duration of the spell.
