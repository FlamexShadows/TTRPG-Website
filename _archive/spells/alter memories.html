Alter Memories


AP Cost: 6

The caster targets an unconscious creature within range. The creature must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) check or become charmed. While charmed the caster whispers the memory they wish to change and what they wish to change it to. On a failure the creature's memory is altered to believe the implanted memories.

The spell automatically fails if the implanted memories would directly cause future self-harm. Memories of actions being performed in the past of such nature can still be implanted.
