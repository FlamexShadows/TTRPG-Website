Bind Creature


Casting Time: 1 Hour

The caster spends one hour binding an angel, fiend, or spirit to its service. At the end of the hour, the creature must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) check or become dominated by the caster for the duration. On a successful check the creature becomes immune to the spell from the caster permanently. A creature is only able to hold 1 creature bound to its service at a time. If the creature was bound during the casting of this spell and succeeds on its check unless from a mythic, or relic item the creature is freed. If the creature was bound by magic that is a lower tier than the most powerful spell of the bound creature it is also broken.
