Void Calling


AP Cost: 3

The caster calls upon the void to emulsify a target morphing its structure randomly for the duration of the spell. If the target is a creature it must succeed on a Resistance (Body) check or roll on the percentile die.

Creature
1-45 The creature becomes partially liquidated making them take on the form of a gelatinous body. The creature can fit through any gap that is 1 centimeter or larger and gains resistance 10 to physical damage.
46-60 The creatures bones, organs, and muscles merge making the creature immune to critical and decisive strikes.
61-100 The creatures body rejects the emulsification process causing them a great deal of pain. The creature loses 2 maximum AP, and its speed is halved.

Object 1-50 The object turns into a liquid for the duration. Its temperature, durability, and weight do not change but act as if they were liquid water.
75-90 The object remains unaffected by the spell.
91-100 The object rejects the emulsification process and instead of liquefying the object is permanently immune to the spell.
