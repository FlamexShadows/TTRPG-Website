Tornado


AP Cost: 4

The caster creates a whirlwind that spirals into the sky creating a tornado that devastates all it touches. This spell functions as Gust and in addition any loose objects are sucked into by the tornado and scattered. Lost items require at least 1 hour of searching for each DC 60 sense environment rolled to find the item. Creatures that are within meters of the tornado that weigh less than 1000 kilograms must make a contested overpower check with the tornado using the casting statistic of the caster in place of strength and is treated as size colossal. On a failure the creature is shoved. All medium size open structures are destroyed by the tornado.
