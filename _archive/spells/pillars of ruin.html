Pillars of Ruin


AP Cost: 4

The caster speaks a holy word chanting for the destruction of 4 areas of its choice it can see within range of itself. A beam of light parts the sky causing all within a 4 meter square to be smitten by the heavens. Any creature within the area must succeed a Resistance (Body) or take 15d12 holy damage. Evil aligned creatures instead take 20d12. On a failure a creature with no magical armor is blinded. A creature struck by multiple pillars is only affected by one. A creature reduced to 0 hit points is slain and is reduced to a shadow in place of where they previously stood.
