Spectral Sword


AP Cost: 2

The caster summons a spectral weapon that attacks targets of the caster's choice. For 3 AP the caster orders the sword to attack creatures within range. The caster makes two magic attacks against a target. On a hit, the creature takes 1d6+Casting Modifier. The caster may spend 1 AP to move the sword up to 5 Meters to an unoccupied space.
