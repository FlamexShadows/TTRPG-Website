Polymorph


AP Cost: 5 AP

The caster transforms a creature into another harmless creature of its choice. The caster picks a creature within range and attempts to alter its physical form into another creature. If the creature has no Magical Armor, it must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) check or be transformed for 1 minute. While transformed, the creature must spend its AP running away from all hostile creatures. A transformed creature always retains the required anatomy to survive its current environment if it previously had it in its new form. Examples of harmless creatures include but are not limited to rabbits, pigs, chickens, and parrots.
