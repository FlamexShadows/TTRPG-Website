Cloud Mind


AP Cost: 3

The caster targets an area they can see within range, The caster creates a transparent pink fog 5 meter radius cloud that alters the minds of creatures inside the sphere. Any creature inside the sphere must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) check or take 4d6 psychic damage or half as much on a success. If the creature has no magic armor, it is incapable of concentrating while inside the cloud.
