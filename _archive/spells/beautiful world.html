Beautiful World


AP Cost: 2

The caster molds a painting in which a beautiful creature, object or world of desire appears to the target in the painting who invites them to the world in their image. If the target has no magical armor, it must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) check or approach the painting of interest. If the creature gets within 1 meter of the painting before the end of the spell's duration, burning hands pull the creature inside the painting. The target is sent into a magical world they perceive as a perfect world. The creature is trapped inside the painting until freed by a purge spell or similar magic. Until freed the target is stuck in the image as they appear in the image alongside a dark entity. This spell has no effect on creatures immune to charm effects or fiends. If the painting is destroyed it reforms 24 hours later in the closest open space that would not destroy the painting.
