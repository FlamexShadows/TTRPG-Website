Flames of the Phoenix


AP Cost: 6

The spell requires a feather from a phoenix as a catalyst, which is consumed during casting. When the spell is activated, the targets body is engulfed in brilliant flames that can incinerate anything they touch. The target gains immunity to fire and heals 2d6 health whenever subject to fire damage while the spell is active. During the spell's duration the target can unleash a ray of flame. The targeted creature must succeed on an Evasion check or take 15d10 fire damage. The flames then leap to up to 7 creatures extending the effect to them. A creature struck by a ray begins burning for 10 minutes. While burning the creature takes 2d6 fire damage at the beginning of each of its turns. These magic flames are so potent they cannot be extinguished except by any means less than a Grand Hydromancy spell.
