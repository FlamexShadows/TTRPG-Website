Finger of Doom


AP Cost: 4

The caster points its finger at a creature within range springing a red beam that attempts to pierce the target's heart. The creature targeted must succeed on an Evasion check. On a failure the creature takes 8d10+Casting Modifier True damage. If the creature is reduced to 0 health, it is instantly slain. If the creature has a heart a hole is left where the target's chest is.
