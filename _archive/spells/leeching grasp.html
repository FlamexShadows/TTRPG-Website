Leeching Grasp


AP Cost: 2

One of the casters' appendages briefly turns into a spectral festering mass of flesh that strikes an opponent in range. The caster must make a spell attack roll. If the attack hits the target, the creature takes 2d6 poison plus 1d6 magic damage. The caster is healed for the amount of magic damage dealt to the creature. If the creature has no magical armor, it must make a resistance physical check. On a failure the creature becomes cursed.
