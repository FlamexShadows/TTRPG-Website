Sinkhole


AP Cost: 2

The caster chooses a point within 10 meters of themselves opening the ground beneath the target. The creature must succeed on an Evasion check or fall into the pit created by the spell. The spell creates a sinkhole that is up to 5 meters deep and has up to a 2-meter radius. The walls inside the sinkhole are loose and hard to traverse. If a creature is not large enough to climb out of the sinkhole by reaching the top it must succeed on a DC 15 Traverse (Str) check.
