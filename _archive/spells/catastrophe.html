Catastrophe


AP Cost: 6

Catastrophe is a powerful spell that unleashes a massive wave of lava in 10 points of the caster's choice they can see creating pools of bubbling lava in a 100 meter radius, engulfing everything in its path. The spell requires a lit candle as a catalyst, which is consumed in the process. The flames burn at an intense temperature incinerating most things within the area of effect. Any creature within the range of Catastrophe when cast must succeed on an Evasion check or take 30d6 fire damage or half as much on a successful save. Any creature that begins its turn in a pool of lava must succeed on a DC 40 Resistance (Body) check or take 10d6 fire damage. A creature reduced to 0 health by this spell is melted within the lava leaving behind no remains.
