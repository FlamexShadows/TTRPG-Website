Visions of the Mire


AP Cost: 2

The caster targets one creature it can see within range of itself. If the target has no magical armor, it must succeed a Resistance (Mind) check. On a failure the target becomes Blind. While blind it becomes entrapped in a vision where its sight is blocked by a black inky substance only it can see. All noises are drowned out by the screaming of tormented souls of the underworld causing the creature to be Deaf. A creature may repeat the check at the end of each of its turns. This spell cannot be upcast.
