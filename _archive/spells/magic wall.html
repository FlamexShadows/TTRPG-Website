Magic Wall


AP Cost: 5

The caster creates a protective dome that wards off area of effect and attacks from outside the dome. The caster creates a dome of magical force within range creating up to a 5 meter diameter circle. Any attack or spell made from outside the dome has no effect on creatures inside the dome. Any creature that is inside the dome cannot affect creatures outside the dome with spells or attacks. The dome is only a one way dome only allowing for creatures and objects inside the dome to magically stop the momentum of any nonliving material or magic not worn or carried by a creature that leaves the dome. Objects and creatures are either inside the dome or not. A creature or object that partially leaves the dome is shunted out fully.
