Strength

A character's physical ability to lift, carry, and push objects and creatures. A low strength indicates a creature has little capability to overpower others while a high strength is gained through design or long and hard physical practice and training. Examples of creatures with high strength would be dragons, elephants, and giants. Effects a creatures weapon attack rolls and damage and carrying capacity.

Finesse

Finesse is the ability to perform quick or precise actions. A creature with high finesse is extremely fast and agile. A creature with low finesse will be slow and have poor hand-eye coordination. Examples of high dexterity are flies, cheetahs, birds. Increasing your finesse

Fortitude

Fortitude is how durable and resilient a creature is to the environment and attacks from other creatures. A creature with high fortitude will survive harsher weather and is harder to kill. Creatures with high fortitude usually have high strength. Examples of creatures with high fortitude are tardigrades, hippopotamus, wolverines.

Charisma

Charisma is a creature's ability to perform socially and its creativity. This can be done in various ways like smooth talking, good looks, or previous connections and coming up with new and interesting ideas. A creature with high charisma will likely hold a high social status and if they do not it is either because of poor circumstances or no desire to move up the social hierarchy. Creatures with high charisma are dogs, demons, deities.

Wits

Wits are a creature's basic senses. A creature with high wits will make rational decisions and have the ability to perceive, navigate, and track creatures in various environments. Creatures with high wits include eagles, foxes, crows.

Intelligence

Intelligence is a creature's ability to learn, remember, create, and recall information. A creature with higher intelligence will make use of tools and be able to solve problems presented to them. Examples of creatures with high intelligence are humans, octopus, aliens.

Memory

Memory is a creature's ability to memorize things. Memory is not described as your ability to memorize or recall knowledge. but is simply for game purposes of how many extra spells and martial maneuvers and extra skill levels a creature can learn.

Statistic Damage

Statistic damage is a type of damage that is dealt to a creature's statistics. When damaged, the creature has a penalty to its ability treating its score as if it was lowered by however many it was drained. This damage is not healed by normal means and requires magic or a full rest to heal. Statistic damage is dealt by certain spells, poisons, and diseases. Statistic damage is healed by 1 point per full rest. If a statistic is reduced to 0 the creature dies instantly. If resurrected the statistic is restored to one.

Statistic Bonuses

Statistic bonuses are a bonus to a creature's statistics. This bonus is added to the creature's score. Statistic bonuses are gained through spells, items, and abilities. Statistic bonuses do not stack with each other but instead take the highest bonus. Temporary bonuses to statistics the fortitude, charisma, memory, intelligence do not effect skill levels, mana, stamina or armor. Temporary bonuses are those that have a duration such as from enhance body or enhance mind but bonuses from spells like upon a star or magic items such as a ring of intelligence are considered permanent.
