Anything not covered in the rules is GM discretion

All die when multiplied or divided are rounded down

Excluding rings, any effect of the same name does not stack with each other but instead takes the more potent effect or the newest if they are the same strength.

Advantage/Disadvantage does not stack. If both advantage and disadvantage are both applied on a creature they are canceled.

Flanking is a +2 to attack rolls against a flanked creature

Vulnerability is double damage

Resistance subtracts the resistance number from the damage.

Movement

Movement is representative of meters on the map that a creature can move. A creature has designated movement that is either walking, flying, burrowing, or climbing. Walking is movement that is along the ground primarily by use of legs but some creatures such as snakes may slither across the ground. Flying is avion movement by the use of magic, wings or any other form of propulsion that allows a creature to move through the air. Burrowing is speed that is used to dig underground through dirt, sand, gravel or any other material. Most creatures with a burrow speed are incapable of movement through solid rock or metal and are incapable of passing through such things unless specifically mentioned or it has a strength score higher than 40.

Armor Value

Physical Armor is 5*Fortitude Modifier
Magical Armor is 5*Intelligence Modifier

AP

AP represents a the amount of actions a creature can preform within 12 seconds which is a round. If an action costs 4 AP to perform it means that action is more time consuming than another action that represents less than 4 AP.

AP Per Round 6
Maximum AP 6
Unless stated otherwise all actions take 3 AP
AP does not carry over but actions may be continued the next turn by sacrificing the remaining AP to finish it on your next turn.
Any action that has a casting time longer than 1 round requires 6 AP every turn it is continued in combat.

General Actions

Help 2 AP
A helped creature gains a +5 to its next roll. Multiple help actions do not stack but a creature may provide the help action more than once continuing the help onto its next action. If helping a creature with a skill check the creature must have at least 1 rank in the skill and have a reasonable way to help the creature.

Attack 3 AP
You make one attack using a weapon you currently are wielding. Making non lethal attacks incurs a -5 penalty to attack rolls. Spells and Maneuvers with a save cannot be non lethal nor can nonlethal attacks become critical or decisive strikes.

Object Interaction 4 AP
You interact with an object. For exceptionally swift tasks such as pushing a light object or opening a door without requiring a lot of precision or strength the AP is reduced to 2.

Activate Equipment 3 AP
Used for activating scrolls, lighting a candle or consuming a potion, etc.

Damage Types

Physical
Physical Damage is any damage dealt to the body that isn't by the elements such as bludgeoning, piercing and slashing, and decay of all forms. Physical damage always lowers a creature's physical armor.

True
True damage is identical to physical damage but instead bypasses the physical armor and instead directly damages its health. It is still applicable to any resistance or immunity from physical damage.

Psychic
Psychic damage is an attack on the mind of a creature. Damaging a creature's ability to cognitively think psychic damage always damages a creatures magical armor.

Fire
Fire damage is raw elemental energy unleashed in a searing inferno. Appearing as dancing flames and flickering embers, fire damage scorches flesh and ignites flammable materials that deals damage to magical armor.

Cold
Cold damage is the frigid touch of elemental ice, manifested as biting frost and chilling winds. Appearing as shimmering ice crystals and swirling snowflakes, cold damage saps warmth and slows movement that deals damage to magical armor.

Earth
Earth is unlike bludgeoning or poisoning a creature but is directly the result of the elements of earth having an effect on the body. Deals damage to magical armor.

Poison
Poison damage encompasses both the insidious touch of toxins and the corrosive bite of acids. Manifesting as noxious fumes, bubbling liquids, stinging barbs, or burning ichor, poison damage weakens the body, hinders vital functions, and erodes flesh that deals damage to magical armor.

Air
Air damage is from the elements present within storms such as sound or electricity. Deals damage to magical armor.

Holy
Holy damage is damage directly from the heavens or a force of good. Most commonly as a bright light or white flames, holy damage deals damage directly to a target's health if it is evil aligned. Otherwise it deals damage to magical armor.

Unholy
Unholy damage is damage directly from the underworld or from a force of evil. Most commonly seen as bright blue flames or a deep crimson light, unholy damage deals damage to a target's health if it is good aligned. Otherwise it deals damage to magical armor.

Magic
Magic damage is pure magical energy impacting a creature. Appearing as a almost completely translucent or unperceivable force magic damage does damage directly to a target's health if it is neutrally aligned. Otherwise, it deals damage to magical armor.

Measurements

5 rounds is equal to 1 minute
Each tile on battle maps represents 1 meter

Combat DCs

Combat DCs use a class's primary stats for determining its DC. This is the higher bonus class statistic granted in a class when leveling up.

Critical/Decisive Strikes
Percentile die is only allowed on physical melee and ranged attacks. If the attacker has taken the Deadly magic feat, melee and ranged spell attacks gain Percentile die.
When a creature lands an attack with attacks that allow Critical Strikes. it rolls a Percentile die. If it lands on a creature's wits score or lower the attack becomes a Critical Strike and doubles the damage die of the attack. When a creature rolls a natural 20 on its attack roll it automatically counts as a critical strike. The attacker rolls a Percentile die. If it lands on its wits score or lower the Critical Strike becomes a Decisive Strike. On a Decisive Strike, the creature becomes crippled and the damage die is tripled instead of doubled.

Armor

Armor represents a character's resistance to certain types of damage from their past. Mages have high magical armor because their constant exposure to magic makes them more resilient than a warrior who has trained physically and becomes more resilient to physical attacks. Monsters do not have magic and physical armor and instead just have a singular armor value and possibly a true armor value. Anything that states it affects magical or physical armor interacts with this armor as if it was magical or physical armor.

Physical Armor
Physical armor is a creature’s ability to resist cuts, stabs and blunt force. The higher the armor the harder it is for a creature to succumb to such wounds.

Magical Armor
Magical armor is a creature's ability to resist lasting damage from non physical effects even those not caused by magic such as heat, electricity, poison, etc.

True Armor
True armor is rare and only gained from a few features. True armor is always damaged after magical and physical armor and protects against both. True armor mitigates true damage and other damage that directly damages health such as holy damage on an evil creature.

Resting

Resting is in between strenuous activities such as combat, traveling, or casting spells. A partial rest is completed when a creature spends 8 hours without performing strenuous activity. During this time a creature may do any light activity such as reading, writing or sleeping.

Partial Rest
When a creature completes a partial rest they gain Mana and Stamina back equal to their level. In addition to this they gain back physical armor equal to their fortitude score and magical armor equal to their intelligence score. Partial rests take 8 hours.

Full Rest
A full rest is identical to a partial rest but takes a full 24 hours to complete. Upon finishing a full rest a creature resets its armor value to its maximum and levels up if it meets the prerequisites to do so. The creature also rolls its health die and adds it to their current health up to their maximum.

Concentration

A creature may only hold concentration one one spell at a time unless otherwise stated. Losing concentration on an effect instantly ends any effects it produced. A creature automatically loses concentration on a spell when it's maximum AP is reduced to 0, it becomes severed or nauseated.

Spells and Maneuvers

Maneuvers and Spells use the Maneuver and Spell DCs for determining their DC. This is the higher bonus class statistic granted in a class when leveling up.

Opportunity Attacks

An opportunity attack is a special type of attack that a creature can make when another takes an action that leaves itself vulnerable in combat.

Whenever a uses the activate equipment action, they provoke an Opportunity Attack. If a creature uses the activate equipment action to activate an object worn or carried, or used on another creature both creatures provoke an opportunity attack.
Whenever a creature has its maximum AP reduced to 0 they are always susceptible to Opportunity Attacks which can be taken at any point.
Reloading ranged weapon or using ranged attacks in melee provoke an Opportunity Attack.
Using a non touch or melee attack spell or maneuver in melee.
Moving away from a creature that can see the target in melee range.
Making a distracting skill check or action such as picking a lock, stabilizing another creature, picking up a object or lifting/moving a heavy object.
