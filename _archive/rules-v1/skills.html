Skill Scaling Description
Overpower Strength When one creature physically attempts to overwhelm another. When used you may attempt to move while grappled, knock down, pin, or shove another creature. Knock downs and shoves may be evaded while grapples, pins, shoves and movement are always contested by overpower. A creature gains a +10 for each size category larger than its contestant. Knocking a creature down applies the Knocked Down condition. A pushed creature gains the Shoved condition.
Heavy Object Interaction Strength When a creature attempts to lift, push or pull a heavy object safely within 10 pounds of their limit. If failed this applies the Tired condition.
Traverse (Str) Strength Used to travel across terrain that requires physical strength. This includes climbing, swimming against a current, walking while encumbered.
Intimidate (Str) Strength When a creature tries to intimidate another using physical stature or force. Contested by resistance (Mental). If the user of the threat has no intention to follow its threat the target may use Intuition instead.
Endurance Fortitude When a task may be tiring to complete or continue, endurance is rolled to see if a target has the stamina to continue.
Resistance (Body) Fortitude Used to resist the application of physical effects.
Evade Finesse When a target wishes to avoid being struck by a creature or object evasion check is used against a DC set by the GM.
Balance Finesse When a creature may struggle to keep standing when not being physically moved.
Sleight of Hand Finesse When a creature desires to perform a swift or precise action using their hands or appendages.
Sneak Finesse When a creature wants to keep a target unaware of its presence. It rolls a sneak check. All creatures it intends to hide from within must roll Sense Environment checks contested by this roll to detect this creature. Any creature that fails this contest gains the Unaware condition. Sneak checks must be rerolled for every 20 meters its user moves. A creature who has direct vision on a creature need not roll to contest sneak checks. A creature uses its same stealth roll until it reveals itself or after 1 minute.
Traverse (Dex) Finesse When traveling across terrain it requires precise and light footwork.
Knowledge (Arcane) Intelligence Knowledge in arcane magic.
Knowledge (Divine) Intelligence Knowledge on divine magic and religion.
Knowledge (History) Intelligence Knowledge of past places, events, and people.
Knowledge (Demonic) Intelligence Knowledge of the Underworld and Fiends.
Knowledge (Spiritual) Intelligence Knowledge of the Spirit World and its inhabitants.
Knowledge (Medical) Intelligence Knowledge in medicinal treatments and general anatomy of creatures.
Knowledge (Nature) Intelligence General Knowledge of plants and animals, environments.
Sense Environment Wits A creature gains information about its surroundings from any senses it has. A creature with the Unaware condition that is unaware of an applicant's presence but not location may attempt to find it. Sense environment uses the same stealth roll for each check until its Unaware is ended.
Survival Wits A creature's ability to survive beyond basic survival. This includes avoiding other apex animals, extreme weather, survival resources missing or low.
Resistance (Mind) Wits Used to resist effects that are not physical.
Intuition Wits Intuition is how honed a creature's instincts are. This is used when interacting to determine danger or truth in statements when speaking with creatures based on feeling.
Inspect Object Wits When inspecting an object this is used to identify features or traits about a specific object. It is used to find traps connected with objects or determine if an object is magical.
Animal Handling Wits A creature's ability to interact with unintelligent creatures.
Deception Charisma A creature's ability to deceive others.
Persuasion Charisma A creature's ability to convince others to agree or act on its words.
Allure Charisma How interesting or attractive a creature is to others.
Intimidate (Cha) Charisma How well a creature can intimidate another using its words. Contested by resistance (Mental). If the user of the threat has no intention to follow its threat the target may use Intuition instead.
Teamwork Charisma Each creature must roll a teamwork check. The DC is set by the Game Master.
