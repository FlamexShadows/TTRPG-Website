Common terms are terms used by tabletop players to abbreviate certain common or long names of certain abilities and mechanics in games. Here is a list of a few common tabletop abbreviations that will be used in Sparks to help new players understand the terms used by fellow game masters and players alike.

Statistics (Stats)
Armor Class (AC)
Threat Rating (TR)
Advantage (ADV)
Disadvantage (DIS)
Action Points (AP)
Opportunity Attack (OA)
Crowd Control (CC)
Difficulty Class (DC)
Damage Resistance (DR)
