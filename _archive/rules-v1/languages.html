Universal
Universal is a common language between traders, nobility, and travelers developed as a universal language for trade and the ability to travel to foreign locations and be able to speak with those who you meet and be able to buy most goods. While everyone is not fluent in universal, anyone not living under a rock knows enough to hold a simple conversation if it's not part of their occupation.

Elvish
Elvish is the language of the elves. Its words are odd and consist of things that occur in nature such as animals, plants, bugs, and tree sounds to form its speech.

Dwarvish
Dwarvish is the language of dwarves when spoken it sounds Nordic.

Novan (Nova Strados)
The official language of Nova Strados. Sounds East Asian.

Solvian (Sol Inos)
The official language of Sol Inos. Sounds Greek.

Brenchisch (Brenchisca)
Brenchisch is a sub-dialect of Dwarvish formed to help others new to Brenchisca to be able to communicate. Still sounds Nordic in tone and anyone who doesn’t know either language has a hard time differentiating them.

Desertic (Black Desert)
Desertic is the official language of the black desert. It sounds middle eastern when spoken.

Arendellian (Arendell)
Arendellian is the official language of Arendell used everywhere aside from the desert. Sounds Shakespearean English.

Enochian
The language of the angels in the seven heavens. When spoken it sounds as if the person is singing a song. The more beautiful a speaker's voice is, the more fluent and native-speaking they sound.

Bellsybabble
The language of fiends that reside in the underworld. Even those who are native speakers may have a hard time understanding its speech as it is inconsistent and it consists mostly of gurgles and low-pitch rhythmic hymns.

Draconic
Draconic is the language of dragons. Sounds very similar to elder scrolls draconic speech.

Spirit
Spirit is the language of the spirit world. The language has no written form instead relying on hieroglyphics. When spoken it sounds as if the speaker is whispering and fades away as echoes into the air. When spirit is spoken both speakers speak at the same time and there is no speaking turn order.

Kull
The language of the Naga within the desert. Sounds serpentine in nature with hisses and dragged-out sounds.

Orcish
The language of the orcs. There is no written dialect of orcish and when spoken the speaker is always very proud and sounds angry often making the tone of the speaker impossible to tell. This is the main reason orcs are considered unpredictable to non-speakers.

Druidic
Language of the Grove Keepers. Sounds similar to elvish but is only known to Grove Keepers. It is a closely guarded secret to help organize without speaking to others.

Aquaurian
The language of the Aquarii. Can only be spoken while submerged in liquid such as water. Sounds muffled and drowned out while underwater.

Gnome
Gnomish is the language of Gnomes. It is very high pitched in native speakers and spoken very fast. It is renowned as the hardest mortal language to learn.

Kender
Kender is the language of the Kenderkai. Its language is Chitinous in speech consisting of clacking of mandibles. It can be spoken by other races replacing the mandible clicking with clapping in place.

Cryptic
Cryptic is the language of Cryptids. It’s supernatural in nature and sounds as if it’s attempting to mix all of the languages together.

Empatic
The language of the empty. Spoken by outsiders, the language drives those who read or hear it that aren’t an outsider mad.
