Cryptid
Cryptids are always known to the local people through stories passed down from generation to generation. Information about Cryptids can be gathered from locals in the area. A common way adventurers determine if creatures are cryptids is simply asking local legends and seeing if the creature they are hunting fits the description.

Cryptids require a Knowledge (History) check to learn information about if a person is from the local area cryptids are from. The base DC always equals 10+TR and upon a success the person learns one thing about the creature's nature. This can range from what it hunts to why it exists or even how to remove it. For every time the check is divisible by the DC the person passed they learn an extra bit of Knowledge.

Example
Kelly rolls a 25 on a DC 12 Knowledge check. Kelly learns 2 pieces of relevant information about the cryptid.

Angel
Angels are the servants of the deities of the seven heavens. Angels always manifest as extensions of their deities' powers. Angels of Lemara will have spectral wings made of pure mana while an angel of Marissa will have scaled wings and gills along the sides of their ribs. Angels always serve to further their deities goals and uphold the tenants they set. Angels that become disobedient fall severing their grace destroying their connection to the heavens. Fallen angels appear as dark, pale and hollow aspects of their former selves. Fallen angels are amongst the most evil in the cosmos and often strive for power. Angels are common knowledge to the people yet their existence would be heavily debated amongst the people as they are never seen if it wasn’t for the demiangels the offspring between mortals and angels.

Fiend
The origins of fiends is heavily debated as the stories of their creation or subjugation are referenced in multiple stories pertaining to the gods. Fiends are known to serve the deities of the underworld. Fiends are organized into several ranks ranging from demons to half fiends. Fiends are portrayed as evil though stories of heroes amongst them saving mortals from torment is prominent in their stories. Fiends are weary of holy symbols. The weaker the fiend the more prominent of an effect they procure over them. Anytime a holy symbol is presented to an exposed fiend the creature must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) check equal to 10+ TR or become fearful of the symbol. Fiends are known for their ability to corrupt and possess mortals and seek to spread corruption amongst them, damming souls to the underworld to raise their ranks.

Beast
Beasts are creatures that appear in the wild. They hold little to no intelligence and are seen everywhere in the world. They include all animals and insects except those created or modified by magic. Many hold an affinity to these creatures and most can form a bond with other creatures feeling complex emotions such as hatred or love often resulting in some species coexisting with other creatures.

Magical Beast
Magical beasts are exactly like their non magical counterparts except they are created or modified by magic to be more adept in certain skills or tasks. This can include hunting or even being used as war beasts. They are often the tales of great triumphs in legends such as a hero slaying the great sea serpent or the hunting of an animal terrorizing a kingdom. Magical beasts are almost exclusively created by accident or by a deity as a trial to test a hero.

Elemental
Elementals represent the primal forces of nature such as air, earth, fire, etc. They reside in the planes of substance and are the living embodiment of the elements they represent. Elementals vary in power drastically, a famous example of them being the phoenix compared to their base fire embers. Elementals hold no strong emotions resulting in them not swaying strongly towards good or evil.

Golem
Golems are almost identical to Elementals but are created by a master using magic to serve them. They hold just enough intelligence to follow the orders of their masters but are considered not sentient. Powered by a core they animate themselves and are highly resistant to physical attacks and certain elements but are always weak to another, usually the opposite or natural counterpart of the material they are constructed from.

Humanoid
Humanoids are bipeds that are intelligent. The most prevalent of which being humans, elves, and dwarves. Mostly a term for any human-like creature.

Plant
Plants are the fauna and trees in nature. Most are non sentient, and immobile. Few plants exist that evolve from this basic form becoming close to humanoids or beasts. They gain intelligence and like humanoids they are not strongly aligned with any alignment. Sentient plants often reside in elven society as they share a close bond and are seen as equals.

Spirit
Spirits are a mystical force that reside in a realm that lies just below the border world. Spirits are viewed mostly negatively having a reputation as tricksters and bringers of ill omen. Spirits can commonly be mistaken as Cryptids or Magical beasts due to the similarity between them. Spirits include races such as faeries and yokai. They are renowned for a vast majority of them possessing the ability to almost perfectly mimic humans and beasts. Most spirits devolve from Cecylla and are said to be her first angels before they were dismissed from the heavens and sent to the spirit world. Due to their nature upon creation, spirits it's not unheard of for them to be worshippers of hers and are protective of nature where they reside.

Slime
Slimes are composed of a soft gelatinous semi-fluid that is highly acidic or basic resulting in objects and creatures that come into contact with them to disintegrate. Vulnerable to water they rapidly absorb it into their body causing them to explode and die. Slime reproduction is usually a result of them splitting in two. Slimes grow in size as they consume and most are blind beyond certain radii.

Undead
Undead are creatures that have been raised from the dead incomplete resulting in a hollow semblance to life or are transformed through a curse, disease, or some other magical method. Whatever raises an undead into death is permanently etched into their soul unless killed, therefore allowing it to be removed. Undead are feared, the most famous of them being the Queen of Undeath and her bride The Witch King who wished to usher in an era of undeath raising all to undead. Undead are animated at the state they are at in decomposition making some into nothing but its skeleton and others have most if not all their flesh such as zombies.

Outsider
Outsiders are not native to any plane but hail from the empty. Outsiders are a primordial evil and are incarnates of entropy always searching for ways into one of the worlds to make it into an extension of the empty. Outsiders influence creatures inside the planes to create portals for them to enter. Outsiders are defeated at all costs as they exude a presence of nondescript malice causing even the worst of enemies such as fallen angels and angels to create temporary alliances to rid the world of them.

Draconid
Draconids are seen as the peak of reptilians but only share a similar appearance causing them to be classified differently from even magical beasts. Among the top of draconids are true dragons known for their powerful environmental effects and their ability to breathe a force that causes massive damage to anything touched by it. Most draconids are proud creatures who look down upon any creature they believe to be weaker than themselves. Among the draconids are the Lung. Lung are dragons of Cecylla’s creation that represent a force of nature. Lung are revered by her followers often leading to many places where they visit in legend having shrines dedicated to them for protection against spirits.
