A creature has a certain amount of slots it can use. This is its head, face, neck, body, wrists, waist, legs, feet, hands, 2 rings and 3 Miscellaneous slots. Magic items are classified by power of the item from weakest to strongest being faint, glimmering, exotic, legendary, mythic, and relics. While power makes an item harder to create it does not dictate the price as a powerful item can be more commonly produced than a less powerful item or its creation process can be less known.

Head
This includes hats and helmets.

Face
Face is anything worn on the face such as glasses and masks

Neck
This includes anything worn on the neck such as a necklace or scarf.

Body
Body is defined as anything worn over or under your armor mostly composed of large robes or cloaks.

Wrists
Wrists are anything worn on your forearms such as bracelets and arm sleeves.

Waist
Mostly composed of belts.

Legs
Anything worn on your legs over or under pants such as knee pads or compressions.

Feet
Different kinds of shoes.

Hands
It’s for anything worn on the hands such as gloves or wraps.

Rings
Innately only a creature's ring finger is able to harness the magical power of rings granting them only 2 slots. This can be expanded through various rituals in the church of Lemara.

Miscellaneous
Misc items are those that are not defined by the above slots. This includes weapons, armor, staves and any other objects. Misc items require attunement to function. You only have 3 Attunement slots to start off with though this may increase with time. Attuning an item requires knowledge of how it works and takes 10 minutes. Miscellaneous items you are not attuned to do not function as the magic is unresponsive.
