Leveling

Feats and Free Bonus statistics are calculated by cumulative level no matter the classes or path chosen. A level 8 mage/4 pactkeeper will have 20 bonus statistics. Bonus statistics are separate from primary and secondary statistics and can be put into any statistic of choice. When leveling up you must have enough experience listed beside the level a character with under 500 experience is level 1 and a character is level 2 until they reach 1000 experience.

Progression

Martial/Caster progression means when a character level up in a class they are advancing towards higher tier magic. A level 7 Priest/ 2 Warrior only has access to adept spells and basic combat maneuvers. You must be a high enough level in one class to gain progression to a new tier of magic. A level 4 Priest/4 Mage will still only have access to minor spells.

Bonus Statistics

Class Bonus Statistics are total not cumulative. When it says +2 Primary +1 secondary and the next level says +2 Primary +2 Secondary you only add +1 to the secondary stat not add an additional +2 to the primary and and +2 to the secondary Class Bonus Statistics are in addition to Bonus statistics when leveling up in general. It is meant to demonstrate training in the statistic what one would gain from being in that class and progressing
