Veils of Authority

Simple Veil

Upon reaching 3rd level you the ability to on a reaction gain a simple domain of authority. When activated, you gain a saving throw and the the caster of the veil must make attack rolls on you. against a higher ranking Veil.

Awakened Veil

Upon reaching 4th level, once per day you gain a minor domain of authority. Maintaining a veil requires concentration and lasts for 1 minute. If two veils are active at the same time, the effects of both are nullified. Pick one of the following;

Absolute Might

When first obtained, the veil caster selects a saving throw of Evasion, Body, or Mind. All creatures inside or that enter the veil are subject to its effects. When a creature subject to a veil that does not possess a veil automatically fail its save. On a failure the caster chooses one of the following to apply to the veil.

Status Effects: Slowed, Fearful, Tired, Cursed

Absolute Reinforcement

When first obtained, the veil caster selects a one of the following bonuses to apply to themselves. While inside the veil’s the caster gains this benefit always.

Status Effect: Hastened, Motivated,

Regenerating: The caster gains 10 health at the start of each of their turns.

Sturdy: The caster gains 30 Magic and Physical Armor at the start of each of their turns.

Absolute Attack

The veil caster deals 8d8 damage of a type of their choice that is selected when they first obtain this ability. Attack rolls with absolute attack use the veil casters highest statistic + double their level.

Thief of Tricks

The veil caster gains the ability to automatically copy a singular monster ability they have previously seen. This DC is the same DC as the original creature.

Conduit

While active, the veil caster can hold concentration on multiple spells, maneuvers or abilities.
