Brand of Lethargy


AP Cost: 3
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Demonology
Subtype: Curse
Range: 15 Meters
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: No

The caster brand a creature within 10 meters of themselves with a rune of exhaustion. The target must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save become tired for its duration. The target can repeat its save at the end of each of its turns. Recasting of Brand of Lethargy can be stacked onto the same target.
