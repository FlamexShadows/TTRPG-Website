Languages

Basic Rules, updated November 26, 2026

Universal

Universal is a common language between traders, nobility, and travelers, developed for trade and for travel to foreign lands. Not everyone is fluent. Most people speak enough Universal for a simple conversation, even outside trade work.

Elvish

Elvish is the language of the elves. Its words are odd and consist of things that occur in nature such as animals, plants, bugs and tree sounds to form its speech.

Dwarvish

Dwarvish is the language of dwarves. When spoken, it sounds Nordic.

Novan (Nova Strados)

The official language of Nova Strados. It sounds East Asian.

Solvian (Sol Inos)

The official language of Sol Inos. It sounds Greek.

Brenchisch (Brenchisca)

Brenchisch is a sub dialect of Dwarvish formed to help others new to Brenchisca to be able to communicate. Still sounds Nordic in tone and anyone who doesn't know either language has a hard time differentiating them.

Desertic (Black Desert)

Desertic is the official language of the Black Desert. It sounds Middle Eastern when spoken.

Arendellian (Arendell)

Arendellian is the official language of Arendell used everywhere aside from the desert. Sounds Shakespearean English.

Enochian

The language of the angels in the seven heavens. When spoken it sounds as if the person is singing a song. The more beautiful a speaker's voice is, the more fluent and native speaking they sound.

Bellsybabble

The language of fiends residing in the underworld. Even native speakers struggle with Bellsybabble. Its speech shifts constantly, mostly gurgles and low, rhythmic hymns.

Draconic

Draconic is the language of dragons. It is deep and booming, and every word lands like a shout.

Spirit

Spirit is the language of the spirit world. Spirit has no alphabet. Its speakers write in pictographs. When spoken, it sounds as if the speaker is whispering, fading away as echoes into the air. When Spirit is spoken, both speakers speak at the same time and there is no speaking turn order.

Kull

The language of the Naga within the desert. Sounds serpentine in nature with hisses and dragged out sounds.

Orcish

The language of the orcs. Orcish has no written form. Speakers always sound proud and angry, which makes their tone impossible to read. This is the main reason non-speakers consider orcs unpredictable.

Druidic

Language of the Grove Keepers. Sounds similar to elvish but is only known to Grove Keepers. It is a closely guarded secret to help organize without speaking to others.

Aquarian

The language of the Aquarii. Speakers use it only while submerged in a liquid such as water. It sounds muffled and drowned out under water.

Kendar

Kendar is the language of the Kendarkai. Its speech is chitinous, made of clacking mandibles. Other races speak it by clapping in place of the mandible clicks.

Cryptic

Cryptic is the language of Cryptids. It is supernatural in nature and sounds as if it is trying to mix all languages together.

Emptic

The language of the Empty. Outsiders speak it. Any creature other than an outsider who reads or hears it goes mad.
