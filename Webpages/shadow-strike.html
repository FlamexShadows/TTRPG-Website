Shadow Strike

Master
AP: 6
Requirements: Finesse 30
Type: Attack
Range: Touch
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The martialist disappears into the shadows, becoming invisible. They then reappear behind the target and deliver a swift and powerful melee strike, dealing 20d12+Maneuver Modifier piercing damage. The martialist gains a +20 to the attack roll if they use this in darkness.
