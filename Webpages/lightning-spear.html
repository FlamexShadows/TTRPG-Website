Lightning Spear


Range: 5 meters
AP Cost: 3
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Aeromancy
Subtype: Lightning
Requirements: Sigils, Words of Power
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster conjures a spear of lightning and hurls it at a target within range. The target must succeed on a Evasion save or take 3d4 air damage and become stunned until the end of their next turn.
