Hurl Earth


Range: 20 Meters
AP Cost: 2
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Geomancy
Subtype: Rock
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster magically tears a ball of earth from the soil hurling it at a target within the range of the spell. The caster rolls an attack against the target. On a hit, the creature takes 3d6 earth damage. If the target has no magical armor it must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or become slowed until the end of its next turn.
