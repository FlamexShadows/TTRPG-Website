Slow


Range: 10 Meters
AP Cost: 3
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Chronomancy
Subtype: Time
Requirements: Words of Power, An Hourglass worth 100 GP
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: Yes

The caster distorts time on a target within range. If the creature has no magical armor must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or become slowed.
