Grasp of The Grave


Range: Touch
AP Cost: 3
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Necromancy
Subtype: Decay
Requirements: Sigils, Words of Power
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster touches a creature filling them with negative energy. The creature must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or take 2d6+Casting Modifier Necrotic damage.
