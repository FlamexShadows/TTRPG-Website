Tsunami


Range: Sight
Casting Time: 10 Minutes
Spell Rank: Grand
Class: Hydromancy
Subtype: Water
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster creates a Tsunami that is 1 Kilometer wide and 100 Meters tall. Any creature that is struck by this Tsunami takes 20d12 physical damage and if the creature has no physical armor must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save. On a failure any creature hit by the Tsunami is knocked unconscious for 1 hour.
