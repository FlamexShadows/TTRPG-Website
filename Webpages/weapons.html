Weapons

Basic Rules, updated November 26, 2026

Every weapon lists a damage die, a damage type, and its traits. Attack rolls add your Strength modifier. A Light or Ranged (Finesse) weapon uses your Finesse modifier instead when it is higher. Add your level if you are proficient with the weapon, plus its magical bonus and any class features, minus penalties. Damage adds the same stat modifier you attacked with.

  • Deadly: each level widens the decisive range by 1. A normal weapon is decisive on a 20. Deadly I is decisive on 19 to 20. Deadly II on 18 to 20.
  • Enchanted: each level ignores 5 resistance.
  • Chosen weapon: a feature that names your chosen weapon applies to one weapon at a time. Pick it when you take the feature. Picking a new one ends the old choice.

Weapon Traits

TraitEffect
HiddenThis weapon is easily concealable. When hidden in clothing or armor, any creature without the observant feature is incapable of spotting the weapon.
PunchthroughSpending an extra 2 AP when you take the attack action, you can choose to make the weapon's damage dice deal true damage.
LightAny weapon with the light property uses finesse instead of strength for attack and damage rolls
RangedThis weapon is used from range and can reach any creature within the amount of tiles designated to the weapon. While within the melee range of any hostile creature, the user takes a -5 to all attack rolls.
ReloadWeapons with Reload (N) have an ammunition counter of N shots and must reload after N uses. Reloading takes 1 AP (plus 1 AP for Firearms) and requires a free hand.
ThrownUsually a melee weapon this weapon has the option to be thrown to any tile within the designated range.
ReachThis weapon reaches an extra tile per level of reach.
FirearmThis weapon's ammunition is bullets instead of arrows or bolts Costs an extra 1 AP to reload
HeavyWhile wearing or holding a object with the heavy property its movement is lowered by 2. Weapons with this property require 2 hands to wield.
DefensiveA weapon with the defensive trait adds +1 to a target's AC for each weapon thue actively wield with the defensive trait.
DeadlyAny weapon with the deadly property has its decisive range increased by 1 for each level.
CleaveWeapons with this property don't attack a single target but instead hit all adjacent tiles in a single direction within the targets melee range of the wielder's choice.
Two HandedA weapon with this property requires 2 hands to wield.
HookedWeapons with the hooked property allow for an overpower check to be made after landing a successful attack as part of the attack.
BrittleWeapons with the brittle property break after a certain amount of uses. It is GM discretion if a weapon with this property is allowed to be repaired before or after it has broken.
EnchantedA weapon with the enchanted property ignores 5 resistance per level of enchantment
MagicalA weapon with the magical property can have its damage type changed to magical for 1 AP. A weapon must have the enchanted property to have the magical property.
SecuredA weapon with the secured property is hard to disarm gaining a +5 to all checks to avoid being disarmed.
Ranged (Finesse)This functions as ranged but uses finesse instead of strength
ExoticExotic weapons are rare and are rare or sold in very few places.
AttachmentA weapon with this property is attacked onto a two handed weapon with the reach property
SkilledRequires proficiency in skilled weapons to use
SimpleAny creature can use this weapon without being proficient.
