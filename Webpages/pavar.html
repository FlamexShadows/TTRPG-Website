Information

Amongst the gods only Lemara does not fear the name Pavar. The brother of Lemara, Pavar is the eldest deity alongside Lemara. The two's arguments about what was right shaking the heavens creating Marissa from the resulting storms. The two were locked into eternal conflict as Pavar destroyed Lemara would create and repair. Eventually Lemara became pregnant and birthed Bhera. From this Lemara's order, law, and justice portfolios were passed to Bhera. Pavar after a long conflict with Bhera he is said to have had his head priestess slain by her followers enraged her demanded reoperations for the assassination. After her refusal he swore vengeance upon his niece. Pavar's greatest story is how the tricked Narolith into handing him the portfolio of murder. Narolith and Elesnia had a disagreement in the underworld and it is said one of them spoke his name summoning the wicked trickster. Narolith insulted him prompting Pavar to play a game. Narolith obliged weary of the trickster. During the game he insulted Narolith prompting him to leave the table enraged. As Narolith left too occupied by his anger Pavar used the opportunity to steal from his power.

Appearance

Pavar appears as a small hunched over person wearing a red cloak that covers all features except his legs and hands. His pale wrinkled, cold shriveled hand is covered in jewelry and his feet are webbed and monstrous each toe from pinky to big growing disproportionally larger than the last.
