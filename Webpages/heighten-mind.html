Heighten Mind


Range: 15 meters
AP Cost: 2
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Enchantment
Subtype: Enhancement
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils, Owl Feather
Duration: 10 Minutes
Concentration: No

The caster bestows a target with enchanting magic that allows a creature to surpass its limits. The caster targets a creature within range granting it a +1 to intelligence, wits, or charisma.
