Bull Rush

Advanced
AP: 3
Requirements: -
Type: Attack
Range: 5 Meters
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The martialist rushes towards a target moving up to 5 meters. Any creature they pass while rushing must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or take 2d6 Piercing damage. If the creature is smaller than the martialist and has no physical armor it is automatically knocked down. If it is their size or larger they must roll a contested overpower check counting as one size larger if the creature has no physical armor.
