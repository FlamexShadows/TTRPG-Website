Poison Cloud


Range: 10 Meters
AP Cost: 4
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Nature
Subtype: Primal
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: Yes

The caster creates a cloud of poison that damages creatures within its radius. Each creature that starts, enters, and ends its turn in the cloud must make a Resistance (Body) save. On a failure the creature takes 8d6 poison damage and if the creature has no magic armor becomes poisoned. On a success, the creature takes half as much damage and suffers no additional effects.
