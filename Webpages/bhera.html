Information

Bhera resides in the Seventh Heaven. She long holds resentment to Pavar the god of lies, thievery, and murder. Bhera often takes in those who have a strong sense of justice as her people strive to achieve a lawful land. Because of this many of her extremists kill those who do wrong in any way. These extremists are hunted down by her order believes they have fallen under the control of Pavar twisting their ideals to drive her most loyal followers to sin. Bhera’s holy symbol is that of a shield behind a sword. It is there to symbolize they protect those who are in need. Offenders who break this protection are then slain by the sword that is behind it.

Bhera once fell in love with Oarin as his meeting in the seven heavens. They became close and eventually joined in union. Pavar thought this an opportunity to seed doubt into the heavens and eventually convinced Oarin that she was cheating on him with Polith. He used her bandaged eyes as proof as she has seen his naked body blinding her and no longer able to see she bandaged them to hide their scorched eyes. After this Oarin declared he was done with Bhera leaving her behind alone and blind. Bhera begged for her love to stay but he left in anger. She claimed her eyes were burned out by another and didn’t see who. Before he left he turned around to see her crying tears of blood as her bandages were removed as he saw her burned eyes. Bhera grew resentment towards Pavar and Oarin for this act of betrayal and deceit. Because of this all of her followers are to act just. Those who leave another behind are outcast from the church. Those who deceive another are sentenced to have their tongue removed to have their lies silenced.

Appearance

Bhera, a figure of ethereal beauty, presents herself as a youthful woman in her early twenties with fair skin. Her eyes, once a vibrant yellow, has been replaced by a white blindfold, echoing the choice of her mother, Lemara. Adorning her head, four halos float gracefully, creating a celestial aura above her. Her hair, a cascade of shimmering silver, flows down her back, contrasting with the purity of her white and gold attire. On her back is a set of angelic wings that emerge from her back, each adorned with a delicate blend of white and gold feathers. Bhera's presence exudes an air of calm and serenity, a stark contrast to the fiery passion that burns within her heart. Her voice, soft and melodic, carries the weight of her divine authority, commanding respect and reverence from all who hear it.
