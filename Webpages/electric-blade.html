Electric Weapon

Expert
AP: 2
Requirements: Weapon
Type: Enhancement
Range: Touch
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: Yes

The martialist magically imbues a weapon they are holding with a static charge causing its edge to crackle and pop with electricity. For the next minute, the weapon deals an extra 2d10 air damage on a hit and any creature hit by this weapon must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or be shocked until the end of its next turn.
