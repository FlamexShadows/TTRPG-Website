The Great Purge

In an era long past, before Lemara's reign, power surged through the veins of creatures, unrestrained and chaotic. The world existed in a state of perpetual flux, a tapestry of untamed forces where order was elusive and stability a distant dream. Creatures, endowed with immense powers, reveled in their unchecked abilities, drawing upon the boundless energy that coursed through their beings. However, such unbridled power came at a price. The world tottered on the brink of anarchy, conflicts raged unchecked, and the very fabric of existence trembled under the weight of unrestrained magic.

Recognizing the need for harmony and balance, the divine beings, in their infinite wisdom, sought to intervene. Among them, Lemara, the goddess of order and equilibrium, emerged as the catalyst for change. With a stroke of her celestial hand, she enacted a profound transformation that would forever reshape the magical landscape. Lemara severely reduced the overall power that each creature possessed, ensuring that none, save for the divine themselves, could transcend the boundaries of fourth-tier magic.

This act, though seemingly restrictive, was born out of necessity. By curtailing the unrestrained flow of power, Lemara restored a semblance of order to the chaotic realm. The rampant use of powerful spells, capable of reshaping reality or unleashing cataclysmic forces, became a thing of the past. With the limitations imposed, creatures were forced to explore the depths of their ingenuity, relying on strategy, cunning, and a refined understanding of the arcane arts rather than sheer overwhelming might.

Thus, the world entered a new age, where order and chaos found a delicate equilibrium. The untamed forces of the past were harnessed, channeled, and refined, giving rise to a tapestry of magic that no longer focused on sheer magical might but on refined efficiency and sophisticated mastery.
