Overhead Cleave

Advanced
AP: 4
Requirements: Strength 20
Type: Attack
Range: 10 meters
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The martialist cleaves their sword into the ground sundering all in its path. They create a 1 meter wide and 10 meter long line wave of force. Any creature within the line must succeed on a Evasion slashing or take 8d6 slashing damage.
