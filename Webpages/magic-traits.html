Magical Item Traits

Magic item traits can be added to a weapon or armor such as plate, leather, daggers, clubs, or tachis. A magical weapon can have a maximum magical bonus of +10. Each trait adds a specified magical bonus to a weapon. Any damage die gained from a magic trait is not multiplied on critical or decisive strikes. In order to use magic item traits, a creature must be attuned to the magic item otherwise it is unresponsive.

Weapon Traits

Accuracy

Magical Bonus +1/level
A weapon of accuracy adds an additional +1 to the weapons attack rolls for each level of accuracy.

Sharing

Magical Bonus +1
A sharing weapon allows a creature wielding it to share it’s magical bonus to a creature within 10 meters of it. A sharing weapon that shares its some or all of it’s bonus to the ally so long as they are within range until the end of their turn up to a maximum of +10.

Slaying

Magical Bonus +2
A slaying weapon is designed to kill a specific creature type. When attacking the chosen creature type, a slaying weapon deals an additional 1d10 true damage and gains an additional level of deadly to the creature.

Merciful

Magical Bonus +1
A merciful weapon can easily be used to not kill a creature. When using a merciful weapon, you do not incur a nonlethal attack penalty.

Holy

Magical Bonus +2
A holy weapon gains a level of enchanted and deals an additional 4d4 damage to evil aligned creatures.

Unholy

Magical Bonus +2
A holy weapon gains a level of enchanted and deals an additional 4d4 damage to good aligned creatures.

Duplicating

Magical Bonus +5
A duplicating weapon can make one additional attack when fired and is treated as an offhand attack when the attack action is taken using it. Duplicating can only be applied to ranged weapons.

Blinding

Magical Bonus +2
A blinding weapon on a critical or decisive strike emits a burst of light be blinding the target until the end of its next turn. This effect can only occur once per turn.

Flaming

Magical Bonus +1
A flaming weapon is wreathed in magical fire. When you hit with a flaming weapon, it deals an additional 1d8 fire damage. Additionally, the weapon sheds bright light in a 5-meter radius and dim light for an additional 5 meters.

Freezing

Magical Bonus +2
A freezing weapon is imbued with the power of ice. When you hit with a freezing weapon, it deals an additional 1d8 cold damage and reduces the target's movement speed by 1 meter until the end of its next turn.

Vampiric

Magical Bonus +4
A vampiric weapon drains the life force of those it strikes. When you deal damage with a vampiric weapon, you regain health equal to half the additional magical bonus of the weapon.

Thunderous

Magical Bonus +2
A thunderous weapon reverberates with a booming sound. When you hit with a thunderous weapon, it deals an additional 1d8 air damage. Additionally on a critical or decisive strike the target is pushed back 5 meters.

Venomous

Magical Bonus +1
A venomous weapon injects poison into the target. When you hit with a venomous weapon, the target take 1d8 poison damage.

Guiding

Magical Bonus +1
A guiding weapon aids in precision. When you hit with a guiding weapon, the next attack roll made against the target by an attacker other than you has a +1 bonus if the attack is made before the end of your next turn.

Stormcaller

Magical Bonus +3
A stormcaller weapon crackles with electrical energy. When you hit with a stormcaller weapon, it deals an additional 1d8 air damage. Additionally, on a critical or decisive strike, the target is deaf until the end of its next turn.

Arcane

Magical Bonus +2
An arcane weapon enhances spellcasting abilities. While wielding an arcane weapon, you gain a +1 bonus to spell attack rolls and spell save DCs.

Prowess

Magical Bonus +2
A prowess weapon enhances martial abilities. While wielding an prowess weapon, you gain a +1 bonus to maneuver attack rolls and maneuver save DCs.

Shadow

Magical Bonus +1
A shadow weapon is cloaked in darkness. When you land a critical or decisive strike the target becomes fearful of the sword until the end of its next turn.

Enchanted

Magical Bonus +1/level
A weapon with the enchanted property ignores 5 resistance per level of enchantment

Transmuting

A weapon with the magical property can have its damage type changed to magical for 1 AP. A weapon must have the enchanted property to have the magical property.

Homing

Magical Bonus +3
A homing weapon seeks out its target. When you make an attack with a homing weapon, you can ignore cover, concealment and finesse modifiers added to AC.

Phasing

Magical Bonus +3
A phasing weapon can bypass physical barriers. When you make an attack with a phasing weapon, you can ignore fortitude modifiers, armor and shield bonuses to AC, treating the target as if they have no armor or shield. This trait can only be applied to melee weapons.

Vortex

Magical Bonus +2
A vortex weapon creates a gravitational pull. When you hit with a vortex weapon, you can pull a you can see target up to 5 meters closer to you. If the target collides with another creature or obstacle, both take 1d6 physical damage.

Resonating

Magical Bonus +2
A resonating weapon hums with energy. When you hit with a resonating weapon, you and allies within 5 meters gain a +1 bonus to their next attack roll before the end of their next turn.

Leeching

Magical Bonus +3
A leeching weapon drains energy from the target. When you hit with a leeching weapon, you regain 2d4 mana or 1d6 stamina This effect can only occur once per turn.

Warping

Magical Bonus +3
A warping weapon distorts space around it. Once per day, when you hit with a warping weapon, you can teleport up to 5 meters to an unoccupied space you can see. This teleportation does not provoke opportunity attacks.

Elemental

Magical Bonus +2
An elemental weapon can channel different types of energy. When you make an attack with an elemental weapon, you can choose to deal fire, cold, air, or poison damage instead of the weapon's normal damage type.

Illuminating

Magical Bonus +1
An illuminating weapon glows with a bright light. When you hit with an illuminating weapon, the target and all creatures within 5 meters are bathed in light.

Gravity

Magical Bonus +2
A gravity weapon can alter the weight of its strikes. When you hit with a gravity weapon, you can choose to increase or decrease the target's weight. Increasing the weight reduces the target's movement speed by 5 meters until the end of its next turn. Decreasing the weight allows the target to jump twice as high until the end of its next turn.

Phantom

Magical Bonus +3
A phantom weapon exists partially out of phase with reality. When you make an attack with a phantom weapon, you can choose to ignore cover and concealment, treating the target as if it were in plain sight.

Recalling

Magical Bonus +1
A recalling weapon can be called to it’s wielder’s hand magically teleporting to their hand for 1 AP if it is within 10 kilometers of them. Any effect such as dimensional lock that is in the area of the sword or the wielder causes this trait to fail.

Dispelling

Magical Bonus +3
A dispelling weapon vibrates with an innate hunger for source. When landing a critical or decisive strike on a creature or object under the effects of a maneuver or spell, the sword dispels one current effect on them.

Storing

Magic Bonus +2
A lesser storing weapon can store up to 20 mana or 10 stamina worth of magic. Using a spell or maneuver from a storing weapon does not use it’s wielder's mana or stamina. Upon attuning to a storing item, it’s wielder learns of any spell or maneuver stored within it. The wielder may choose to restore any spells into a storing sword in a full or partial rest.

Lesser Storing

Magic Bonus +1
A lesser storing weapon functions as a storing weapon but stores up to 10 mana or 5 stamina.

Greater Storing

Magic Bonus +3
A greater storing weapon functions as a storing weapon but stores up to 30 mana or 15 stamina.

Vorpal

Magic Bonus +5
A vorpal weapon ignores any resistance and immunity to physical damage a creature has. This trait can only be applied to melee weapons.

Piercing

Magic Bonus +5
A piercing weapon ignores any resistance or immunity to physical damage a creature has. This trait can only be applied to ranged weapons.

Tempo

Magic Bonus +5
A tempo weapon has the attack action reduced to 2 AP.

Shattering

Magical Bonus +2
A shattering weapon can cause devastating fractures. When you hit with a shattering weapon, the target has it’s its AC, movement and attack rolls reduced by 1 until the end of its next turn.

Ensnaring

Magical Bonus +3
An ensnaring weapon entangles its targets. When you land a critical strike with an ensnaring weapon, the target becomes pinned until the end of its next turn.

Repelling

Magical Bonus +1
A repelling weapon creates a forceful push. When you hit with a repelling weapon, you can push the target up to 1 meter directly away from you.

Entropic

Magical Bonus +1
An entropic weapon accelerates the decay of its target. When you land a critical or decisive strike with an entropic weapon, the target suffers from a weakened state, having it’s next save reduced by 1d4.

Corrosive

Magical Bonus +2
A corrosive weapon is coated in a powerful acid. When you hit with a corrosive weapon, it deals an additional 1d8 poison damage and reduces the target's armor effectiveness, lowering their AC by 1 until the end of its next turn.

Harmony

Magical Bonus +3
A harmony weapon resonates with positive energy. When you hit with a harmony weapon, you and allies within 5 meters regain health equal to half the additional magical bonus (rounded down).

Chilling

Magical Bonus +3
A chilling weapon emanates a frigid aura. When you hit with a chilling weapon, the target's attacks are slowed, causing them to spend 1 additional action point on their next action.

Timewarp

Magical Bonus +3
A timewarp weapon bends the fabric of time. When you hit with a timewarp weapon, you can choose to gain 1 additional action point immediately, usable before the end of your turn. This effect can only occur once per combat.

Life Draining

Magical Bonus +2
A life-draining weapon siphons vitality from its targets. When you hit with a life-draining weapon, the target's maximum health is reduced by an amount equal to the additional magical bonus until the end of its next full rest.

Forceful

Magical Bonus +3
A forceful weapon strikes with incredible power. When you land a critical strike the target is knocked back 5 meters. If you land a decisive strike, the target is knocked down.

Mind Piercing

Magical Bonus +4
A mind-piercing weapon disrupts mental defenses. When you you land a decisive strike the target is stunned until the end of its next turn. This is a charm effect.

Wild Magic

Magical Bonus +1
A wild magic weapon is infused with chaotic energy. When you hit with a wild magic weapon, roll a d6. On a roll of 5 or 6, the weapon deals an additional 2d6 damage of a random type (fire, cold, air, or poison).

Teleporting

Magical Bonus +5
A teleporting weapon allows for rapid repositioning. When you hit with a teleporting weapon, you can teleport up to 5 meters to an unoccupied space you can see. This teleportation does not provoke opportunity attacks.

Armor Traits

Sharing

Magical Bonus +1
Sharing armor allows a creature wielding it to share it’s magical bonus to a creature within 10 meters of it. Sharing armor that shares its some or all of it’s bonus to the ally so long as they are within range until the end of their turn up to a maximum of +10.

Reflective

Magical Bonus +2
Reflective armor can reflect ranged attacks back at their caster. When you are targeted by a ranged attack, you can use your reaction to reflect the projectile or ray back at the attacker. If the attacker’s attack roll is high enough to hit their AC they take half the damage they would’ve dealt. This effect can be used once per full rest.

Healing

Magical Bonus +1
Healing armor continuously restores its wearer's health. At the start of your turn, you regain 1 health. You are also immune to the bleeding condition while wearing healing armor.

Phasing

Magical Bonus +3
Phasing armor allows the wearer to become ethereal. For 2 AP, you can phase out of the material plane, becoming ethereal until the start of your next turn. While ethereal, you cannot be targeted or affected by non-ethereal creatures and effects. This ability can be used once per full rest.

Thorned

Magical Bonus +1
Thorned armor is covered in sharp spikes. When a creature hits you with a melee attack, it takes 1d6 piercing damage.

Elemental Resistance

Magical Bonus +2
Elemental resistance armor grants protection against specific elements. Choose one damage type: fire, cold, air, or poison. You gain resistance 10 to the chosen damage type while wearing this armor.

Displacement

Magical Bonus +3
Displacement armor creates an illusionary duplicate of you. Attackers have a 10% miss chance on attack against you while you are wearing this armor. This effect ends if you take damage, but reactivates at the start of your next turn.

Sturdy

Magical Bonus +2
Sturdy armor grants the wearer a +3 bonus checks and saves to avoid becoming knocked down or shoved.

Mist

Magical Bonus +1
Mist armor coats its wearer in a thick mist for 2 AP that displaces environmental effects such as gasses like cloud mind, plague cloud, or weak liquid droplets such as rain granting a +2 bonus to checks and saves to avoid effects from them.

Guardian

Magical Bonus +3
Guardian armor can protect allies. As a reaction, when an ally within 5 meters is targeted by an attack, you can transfer the attack to yourself.

Temporal

Magical Bonus +2
Temporal armor can manipulate time around you allowing for a second chance. Once per partial rest, you can use a reaction to reroll a failed check, save or attack roll.

Anchoring

Magical Bonus +2
Anchoring armor prevents teleportation. Any creature within 5 meters of you cannot teleport or use abilities that involve teleportation.

Chameleon

Magical Bonus +1
Chameleon armor blends into the surroundings. You gain a +5 bonus to Sneak checks while wearing this armor.

Shining

Magical Bonus +1
Shining armor glows with a glimmer as if it was always bathed in light. Once per full rest, the wearer can create bright sunlight in a 5 meter radius around them and 5 meters of dim light.

Aegis

Magical Bonus +3
Aegis armor projects a protective shield. As a reaction, you can create a shield that provides a +2 bonus to AC for all allies within 5 meters for one round. This ability can be used once per partial rest.

Absorptive

Magical Bonus +2
Absorptive armor absorbs a portion of incoming damage. When you take damage, you can reduce the damage taken by 1d10.

Fleetfoot

Magical Bonus +1
Fleetfoot armor increases your speed. While wearing this armor, your movement speed increases by 2 meters.

Fortifying

Magical Bonus +2
Fortifying armor enhances your resilience. While wearing this armor, you gain an additional 10 health points.

Warding

Magical Bonus +3
Warding armor provides a magical barrier. While wearing warding armor, you gain a +5 bonus to Resistance (Mind) saves.

Evasive

Magical Bonus +3
Evasive armor provides ais nimble and grants its wearer small bursts of speed . While wearing warding armor, you gain a +5 bonus to Evasion saves.

Fortfied

Magical Bonus +3
Fortified armor provides a protection from physical effects. While wearing warding armor, you gain a +5 bonus to Resistance (Body) saves.

Silent

Magical Bonus +1
Silent armor muffles sound. While wearing this armor, you make no noise, removing the sneak penalty from a piece of armor if it had it.

Stalwart

Magical Bonus +2
Stalwart armor grants unwavering resolve. When subject to a negative effect with a duration that is longer than instant, the duration of a status effect on you is reduced by 1 round.

Radiant

Magical Bonus +3
Radiant armor shines with holy light. Radiant armor functions as shining but any creature that isn't the wearer that begins its turn in the light takes 3d6 holy damage.

Adaptive

Magical Bonus +2
Adaptive armor changes to suit the environment. While wearing this armor, you gain resistance 5 to the first type of damage you take in a combat encounter until the end of that encounter.

Aquatic

Magical Bonus +2
Blessed by Marissa, Aquatic armor enhances underwater capabilities. You can breathe underwater and gain a swim speed equal to your walking speed while wearing this armor.

Glacial

Magical Bonus +2
Glacial armor emanates cold. Any creature that hits you with a melee attack takes 1d4 cold damage. Additionally, you are immune to cold environmental weather effects.

Stormbound

Magical Bonus +3
Stormbound armor is charged with electrical energy. While wearing this armor, you can use a reaction to deal 2d6 air damage to a creature that hits you with a melee attack.

Beastial

Magical Bonus +2
Beastial armor enhances your senses and agility. You gain a +2 bonus on Sense Environment checks and Balance checks while wearing this armor.

Volcanic

Magical Bonus +3
Volcanic armor is infused with the power of a volcano. Once per partial rest, you can erupt in flames, dealing 2d6 fire damage to all creatures within 5 meters and creating a Smoke Screen

Vigilant

Magical Bonus +2
Vigilant armor enhances your alertness. You cannot be surprised while wearing this armor, and you gain a +5 bonus on initiative rolls.

Featherlight

Magical Bonus +1
Featherlight armor reduces weight making you light as a feather. As a reaction the armor can be activated, causing you take no damage from falling while wearing this armor.

Spellguard

Magical Bonus +3
Spellguard armor provides protection against magic. While wearing this armor, you have a +2 bonus on saves against spells.

Sentinel

Magical Bonus +3
Spellguard armor provides protection against magic. While wearing this armor, you have a +2 bonus on saves against martial maneuvers.

Spiritbound

Magical Bonus +3
Spiritbound armor connects you to the spirit realm. You gain a +2 bonus to the charmed condition and you gain resistance 10 to psychic damage.

Sanctified

Magical Bonus +3
Sanctified armor is blessed with holy power. You gain resistance 5 to unholy damage and a +2 bonus on saves against being cursed while wearing this armor.

Windborne

Magical Bonus +2
Windborne armor grants the agility of the wind. When activated, you gain a flying speed of 5 meters for the next minute. This ability can be used once per partial rest.
