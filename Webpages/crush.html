Crush


Range: 10 Meters
AP Cost: 2
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Geomancy
Subtype: Metal
Requirements: Sigils, Words of Power
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster targets a piece of metal crushing it temporarily into a small ball. If used on a creature made of or wearing metal the creature takes 5d6 physical damage and must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save. On a failure the creature becomes crippled. Creatures made of metal become paralyzed. The creature may reroll this save at the start of each of its turns on a success taking off its uncrushing it's body or armor and no longer being crippled. Using Mend Metal to put the armor back to its original state or expand overpower removes crippled condition. Crush has no effect on adamantite.
