Blood to Ichor


Range: Touch
AP Cost: 3
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Heavenly
Subtype: Protection
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: 10 minutes
Concentration: Yes

The caster turns a creature's blood into Ichor, blessing it with the magic that protects its angels from harmful effects. The creature gains +10 Unholy resistance and immunity to holy damage.
