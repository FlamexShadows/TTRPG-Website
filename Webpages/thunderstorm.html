Thunderstorm


Range: 15 Meters
AP Cost: 4 AP
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Aeromancy
Subtype: Lightning
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: Yes

The caster creates a storm cloud 10 meters above the ground centered on an area within range. The 5 meter diameter thundercloud strikes all creatures within its range with lightning when it's first cast. The lightning strikes again at the start of each of the caster's turns. Any creature under the cloud must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or take 6d12 air damage or half as much on a success.
