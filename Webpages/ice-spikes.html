Ice Spikes


Range: 15
AP Cost: 2
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Hydromancy
Subtype: Ice
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: 1 Hour
Concentration: No

The caster designates a 20 meter radius cube creating sharp icicles that damage creatures that walk its radius. Any creature that walks in the spikes takes 1d4 physical and 1d4 cold damage for each tile moved.
