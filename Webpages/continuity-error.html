Continuity Error


Range: 15 Meters
AP Cost: 3
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Chronomancy
Subtype: Time
Requirements: Words of Power, A Silver Mirror worth 50 GP
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster shows the targeted creature a vision of the future that is slightly incorrect. At any point in the target's turn, the caster can reposition them 1 meter to reflect their reaction to the false insight. The target may react after the repositioning has occurred. This might make an action the creature was about to take invalid, such as repositioning them when they are about to make an attack, in which case the action points are wasted. You cannot reposition the creature into a square that they could not occupy or where there is no ground (such as over a cliff, into rapids, or a flow of lava). Repositions using this spell do not provoke opportunity attacks.
