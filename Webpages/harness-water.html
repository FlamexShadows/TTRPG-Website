Harness Water


Range: 5 Meters
AP Cost: 3
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Transmutation
Subtype: Creation
Requirements: Words of Power, A Pinch of Salt
Duration: Permanent
Concentration: No

The caster pulls water from the surrounding area creating liquid water. Water can be pulled from plants and the air creating drinkable water. The spell creates 1 liter of drinking water unless the area is significantly dry or moist it may yield an amount greater or less than that. If used on a creature they must succeed a Resistance (Body) save or take 4d6 necrotic damage.
