Impale

Tier: Basic
Action Cost: 3 AP
Requirements: -
Type: Attack
Range: Weapon
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The martialist attempts to impale a creature with its weapon. The martialist makes an attack roll. On a hit, the creature becomes impaled. While impaled the creature is crippled and grappled by the martialist until the start of its next turn.
