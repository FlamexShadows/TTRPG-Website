Still Mind


Range: 10 Meters
AP Cost: 2
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Arcanomancy
Subtype: Apotropaic
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster chooses one target within range of themselves. The spell automatically ends ongoing Silenced, Blind, Deaf, Fearful, Hopeless, Cursed effects on the target.
