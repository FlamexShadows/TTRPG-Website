Name: Find Plants


AP Cost: 1
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Nature
Subtype: Primal
Range: Self
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

Locate Plant
The caster emits a wave of magic searching for a specific type of plant. If the plant is within 1 kilometer of the caster the closest one's location is revealed to the caster.

Detect Sentience
The caster searches for sentient plant life within 100 meters of themselves. If there is a sentient plant, they become aware of it and its location. A sentient plant that wishes to hide its location may roll a stealth check.
