Luminous Armament

Advanced
AP: 2
Requirements: Luminous Object
Type: Enhancement
Range: Touch
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: Yes

The martialist channels source into a weapon causing it to shed bright light for 5 meters for the duration of the maneuver. In addition to this when the martialist strikes a creature with a Luminous Armament it takes an additional 2d6 holy damage.
