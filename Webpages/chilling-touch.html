Chilling Touch


Range: Touch
AP Cost: 2
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Hydromancy
Subtype: Ice
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster touches one creature within their melee range sending a burst of cold through the target's body. The target takes 2d6 + Spell modifier cold damage. If the target has no magical armor it must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or have its movement lowered by 1.
