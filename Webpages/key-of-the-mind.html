Key of the Mind


Range: 30 Meters
AP Cost: 3
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Otherworldly
Subtype: Entropy
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: 8 Hours
Concentration: No

The caster chants a ritual calling upon the void to bestow them with eldritch sight. An extra eye appears along the casters forehead as they gain Supernatural Vision until the end of the spell.

Supernatural Vision
The caster can see through true darkness and can identify shape changers although they cannot see their true form the creature appears twisted and unnatural when seen.
