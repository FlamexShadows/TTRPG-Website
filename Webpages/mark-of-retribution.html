Mark of Retribution

Master
AP: 2
Requirements: N/A
Type: Enhancement
Range: 15 Meters
Concentration: Yes

The martialist marks their enemy with a floating visible symptoms, cursing them with a powerful doom magic. Each time a creature is struck by an attack from its user the creature must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or lose 5 in finesse, fortitude and strength or a Resistance (Mind) save or subtract 5 from its intelligence, wits, and charisma. The mark ends if the marked creature and the martialist are ever more than 15 meters apart. Statistics lost from Mark of Retribution may only be restored by a purge or similar effect.
