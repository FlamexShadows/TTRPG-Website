Catastrophe


Range: Sight
AP Cost: 6
Spell Rank: Grand
Class: Pyromancy
Subtype: Magma
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: No

Catastrophe is a powerful spell that unleashes a 15 meter explosion of lava. Any creature within the explosion must succeed on an Evasion save or take 30d8 fire damage or half as much on a successful save. Within the radius, in 5 one meter points of the caster's choice are filled with lava that lasts for the duration. Any creature that begins its turn or enters in a pool of lava has its speed halved until the end of its turn and must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or take 10d6 fire damage. A creature reduced to 0 health by this spell is vaporized leaving behind no remains.
