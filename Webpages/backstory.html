When creating a backstory most game masters are not looking for a specifically long backstory but rather something they can work within games to create plot hooks relevant to your character. One idea to help create an intriguing backstory is to include details such as the following.

Origins: Where were you born? What kind of family or community did you come from?

Formative Experiences: What pivotal events or experiences shaped your early life?

Class Selection: How did you become your chosen class? Was it a family tradition, a personal calling, or born out of necessity?

Adventurous Spirit: Why did you choose the life of an adventurer? Are you running from something, seeking glory, or searching for something or someone?

Connections: Do you have any significant relationships, allies, or enemies? How did these connections come about?

Personal Growth: Have you made any past mistakes that you regret? Or achievements you’re particularly proud of?

Beliefs: What are your character's strongest beliefs? Are they religious, moral, or personal codes they live by?

Fears and Desires: What is your greatest fear? What is the one thing you desire more than anything?

Keepsakes: Do you carry anything that holds sentimental value? What's the story behind it?

Appearance: Do you have any scars, tattoos, or unique features? How did you get them?

Dreams and Ambitions: Where do you see yourself in the future? What is your ultimate goal?
