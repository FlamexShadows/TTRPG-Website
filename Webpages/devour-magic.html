Devour Magic


AP Cost: 6
Spell Rank: Major
Class: Otherworldly
Subtype: Entropy
Range: 5 Meters
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster targets one ongoing magical effect attempting to consume its magic.

Spell

If the target is a spell of the same level or lower than devour magic it is dispelled. The caster regains half the mana of the cost to cast the spell.

Magic Item

The caster targets a magic item. The caster must make a check using its casting modifier to dispel the magic.
