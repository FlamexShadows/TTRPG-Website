Mind Edge

Expert
AP: 2
Requirements: Weapon
Type: Enhancement
Range: Touch
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: Yes

The martialist magically imbues a weapon they are holding with magic to turn incorporeal causing its edge to glow a translucent dim pink. For the next minute their weapon deals psychic damage. In addition the weapon deals an extra 2d10 psychic damage on a hit and they ignore any AC bonus provided by shields and armor.
