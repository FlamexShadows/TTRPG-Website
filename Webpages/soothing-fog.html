Soothing Fog


Range: 15 meters
AP Cost: 5
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Hydromancy
Subtype: Restoration
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: Yes

The caster creates an area of blue fog that takes up a 5 meter sphere. Any creature that starts its turn in the area heals 3d10 health.
