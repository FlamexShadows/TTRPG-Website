Sunder


Range: 10 Meters
AP Cost: 3
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Pneumamancy
Subtype: Severance
Requirements: Sigils, Words of Power
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster targets one creature within range sending a translucent wave of magical force to the target creature. The creature must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save or take 3d6 magic damage.
