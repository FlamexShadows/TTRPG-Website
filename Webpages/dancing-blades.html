Dancing Blades

Expert
AP: 2
Requirements: -
Type: Enhancement
Range: Touch
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: Yes

The martialist imbues a melee weapon not worn or carried by another creature with the light property with animating magic allowing it to act on its own. The weapon connected to the martialist by magic for the next minute. Once per turn the martialist may spend 1 AP to attack with the weapon. Maneuvers and spells used on the sword before its animation still apply.
