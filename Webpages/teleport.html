Teleport


Casting Time: 1 Minute
Spell Rank: Major
Class: Chronomancy
Subtype: Space
Range: Unlimited
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster utters a chant folding the space of their current location and connecting it to another allowing for instantaneous travel to anywhere on the current plane they reside. The caster chooses an area they are aware of and chooses up to five willing creatures and rolls a percentile die. The die determines how successful the teleportation is.
