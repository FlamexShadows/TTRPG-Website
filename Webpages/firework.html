Firework


Range: 1 Kilometer
AP Cost: 1
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Pyromancy
Subtype: Flame
Requirements: Word of Power
Duration: 10 Minutes
Concentration: No

The caster creates a flare that shoots into the air creating a bright flash of light that can be seen from up to 1 Kilometer away in bright sunlight and 10 Kilometers away during night time. The fireworks can appear in various colors and create intricate patterns. The flare lasts up to 10 minutes, but its duration must be chosen at the time of casting and cannot be altered once cast. Cannot be upcast.
