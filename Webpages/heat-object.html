Heat Object


Range: Touch
AP Cost: 3
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Pyromancy
Subtype: Flame
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: Yes

The caster heats an object to a warm or scorching temperature. If the object is warm, it is heated but is still comfortable to the touch. If the object is scorching it becomes red hot dealing 2d6 fire damage at the start of each of the creature's turns. A creature may remove the item from physical contact if possible, causing the creature to no longer take damage.
