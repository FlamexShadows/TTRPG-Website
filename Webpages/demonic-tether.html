Demonic Tether


AP Cost: 2
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Demonology
Subtype: Torment
Range: 10 Meters
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: 1 minute
Concentration: No

The caster links one target to itself. The target must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or have its body tethered to the caster. While tethered if the target receives healing or receives armor it instead applies to the caster. The creature can repeat its check at the end of each of its turns. Cannot be upcasted
