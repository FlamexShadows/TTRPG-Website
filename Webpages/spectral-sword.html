Spectral Weapon


Range: 5 Meters
AP Cost: 3
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Arcanomancy
Subtype: Destruction
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: No

The caster summons a spectral weapon that attacks targets of the caster's choice. For 3 AP the caster orders the sword to attack creatures within range. The caster makes two magic attacks against a target. On a hit, the creature takes 1d6+Casting Modifier magic damage. Once per turn as a free action, the caster may move the weapon up to 5 Meters to an unoccupied space.
