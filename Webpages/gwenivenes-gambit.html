Gwenivene's Gambit


Range: Touch
AP Cost: 6
Spell Rank: Grand
Class: Arcanomancy
Subtype: Destruction
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster gambits their magical armor in a duel of source against the target. The caster's magic armor is compared to the creature they are currently gambling against. The creature with the lower magical armor has its armor reduced to 0 and is Cursed for 1 minute.
