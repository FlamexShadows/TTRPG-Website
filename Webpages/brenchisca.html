Brenchisca

Fleecast

Fleecast, a mountain city nestled deep within the frigid peaks of Brenchisca, stands as a solitary beacon of civilization in a landscape dominated by relentless cold. Inhabited predominantly by of dwarves, Fleecast serves as a sanctuary, a refuge from the harsh elements that envelop the continent.

The dwarves of Fleecast, renowned for their resilience and ingenuity, forged an unbreakable pact to establish their city as a haven from the unforgiving outside environment. Brenchisca's merciless cold would swiftly claim the lives of any creature venturing into its icy embrace, granting them mere hours before succumbing to hypothermia even with proper protection.

Fleecast became the one true "safe haven" in Brenchisca, attracting countless souls desperate to escape the deadly chill. Many perished in their treacherous journey to reach the city, but those who succeeded were welcomed with open arms by the dwarves, who shared their warmth and compassion with all who sought shelter within their mountain stronghold.

In the depths of Fleecast, the dwarves, skilled artisans and master craftsmen, tirelessly worked their forges, creating and enchanting weapons of extraordinary power. These weapons were essential for fending off the monstrous creatures that roamed the frozen wastes, as many had adapted to a carnivorous diet due to the scarcity of vegetation.
