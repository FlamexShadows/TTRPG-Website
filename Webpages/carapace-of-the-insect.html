Carapace of the Insect


AP Cost: 4
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Biomancy
Subtype: Evolution
Range: Touch
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils, 1 Mutagen
Duration: 1 Hour
Concentration: Yes

The caster gains a thick exoskeleton that provides a +2 bonus to AC. The caster also gains resistance 5 to slashing damage.

When upcast the caster can choose to double the AC or resistance granted by this spell.
