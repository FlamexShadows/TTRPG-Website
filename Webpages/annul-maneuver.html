Annul Maneuver

Advanced
Casting Time: Reaction
Requirements: N/A
Type: Attack
Range: 1 Meter
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The martialist attempts to counter a martial maneuver made by another martialist within 1 meter of themselves. Both the martialist and target roll a contested maneuver stat check. If the martialist wins the maneuver fails and if the target wins they perform the maneuver normally.
