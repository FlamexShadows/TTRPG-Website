Acrobatic Assault

Master
AP: 4
Requirements: Finesse 30
Type: Attack
Range: Touch
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The martialist flips, twists, and somersaults through the air with incredible speed and precision, launching a barrage of magical projectiles at the enemy from every angle. The martialist makes a Martial Attack. On a success the creature takes 18d10 piercing damage and must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or become paralyzed from the rapid strikes for 1 minute.
