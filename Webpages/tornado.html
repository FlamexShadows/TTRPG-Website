Tornado


AP Cost: 4
Spell Rank: Grand
Class: Aeromancy
Subtype: Wind
Range: 10 Kilometers
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: 10 Minutes

The caster creates a 15 meter radius 100 meter tall cylinder that devastates all it touches. If the tornado is casted at a point where it cannot fit, the spell fails and the mana and AP is wasted. This spell functions as Gust and in addition any loose objects are sucked into by the tornado and scattered. Lost items require at least 1 hour of searching for each DC 60 sense environment rolled to find the item.
Creatures within 10 meters of the tornado that weigh less than 1000 kilograms must make a overpower check at the start of each of their turns. On a failure the creature is shoved an amount of meters equal to the caster's primary statistic.
All structures smaller than 10x10 meters is instantly destroyed.
