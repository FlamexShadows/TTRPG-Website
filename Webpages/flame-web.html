Flame Web


Range: 15 meters
AP Cost: 4
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Pyromancy
Subtype: Flame
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: Yes

The caster shoots a web of flame centering at a point in range covering a 5 meter radius. Any creature caught within the sphere must succeed on an Evasion save or take 4d6 fire damage and become Pinned. Each time a creature enters or starts a turn in the web it takes 2d6 fire damage and may make an Overpower check to remove the pinned condition. A creature that enters the area of the spell that fails its Overpower check becomes pinned.
