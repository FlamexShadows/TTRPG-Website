Crystal Spikes


Range: 15 Meters
AP Cost: 3
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Geomancy
Subtype: Crystal
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster creates 3 spikes of fragile crystal that targets up to 3 creatures within range. On a successful hit the crystals are launched at the targets dealing 2d6+Casting Modifier earth damage.
