Nature Lore


AP Cost: 1
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Nature
Subtype: Protection
Range: Touch
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster touches a piece of earth or a creature they wish to gain information about as part of the casting of the spell getting the result of the following depending what they choose.

Touch Creature
The caster touches a sentient plant, beast or non-magical beast. They learn 1 piece of vital information about the creature. This can include a resistance, immunity, vulnerability or a particular ability it possesses that they touch.

Touch Earth
The caster touches the earth. They learn 3 pieces of information about the area's history.

Touch Plant
The caster touches the surrounding fauna. They learn basic information about it such as its lifespan, current age, if it is poisonous, ETC.
