Heatwave


Range: 10 Kilometers
AP Cost: 2
Spell Rank: Grand
Class: Pyromancy
Subtype: Ash
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: 1 Day
Concentration: Yes

The caster creates a wave of scorching heat that exhausts creatures within range exposed to its effects. Any creature that spends 10 minutes unprotected from the heat must succeed on an Resistance (Body) save or become exhausted.
