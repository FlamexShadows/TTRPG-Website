Coup De Grace

Tier: Basic
AP: 6
Requirements: N/A
Type: Attack
Range: 1 Meter
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The martialist attempts to execute a creature within 1 meter or melee range that has its maximum AP reduced to 0. The creature must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or die. A creature with physical armor automatically succeeds the save but instead takes 10d6 true damage. If this damage reduces a creature to 0 health it dies.
