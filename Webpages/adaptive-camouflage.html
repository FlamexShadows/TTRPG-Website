Adaptive Camouflage


AP Cost: 4
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Biomancy
Subtype: Evolution
Range: Touch
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: 1 Hour
Concentration: Yes

The caster becomes invisible to the naked eye. The caster can move and take actions without breaking the spell. The spell ends if the caster attacks, casts a spell or uses a maneuver.
