World Tree


Casting Time: 1 Hour
Spell Rank: Grand
Class: Nature
Subtype: Nurture
Range: Touch
Requirements: Sigils, A seed of a tree
Duration: Permanent
Concentration: No

The caster inscribes ancient sigils into a seed before planting it into the ground. The seed instantly sprouts into a seedling and grows at 1 meter per day. All plants within 10 kilometers mimic the color of the leaves of the World Tree and give it nutrients to grow. Refer to the table below for effects.

Seedling
50 Meters
The animal life within the area flourishes. The caster gains a 10-meter cube private quarter inside the base of the World Tree. The room is accommodating and made of wood but can be customized to the casters desire.

Sapling
100 Meters
The World tree gains further control over the seasons in the area surrounding it. The caster may choose a season. Within 100 Kilometers of the tree the area is always treated as that season.

Mature
250+ Meters
The caster's soul becomes linked to the World Tree. The caster no longer ages and becomes immune to aging effects. In addition, the caster's life force becomes tethered to the tree. Whenever the caster dies, it may choose to sacrifice the life of the tree. Instead of dying its body is transported to the base of the tree where they are resurrected at its roots as the tree dies.
