Serpent's Fangs


AP Cost: 1
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Biomancy
Subtype: Evolution
Range: Self
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils, 1 Mutagen
Duration: 1 Hour
Concentration: Yes

The caster grows fangs as a natural weapon they are proficient with that use their casting modifier instead of strength that deal 1d6 Piercing damage. Any creature bit by the caster must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or become poisoned for 1 minute. A creature poisoned by this spell may repeat this save at the end of each of its turns ending the effect on a successful save and becoming immune to the poison for 24 hours.
