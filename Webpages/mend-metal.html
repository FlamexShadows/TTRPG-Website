Mend Metal


Range: Touch
AP Cost: 2
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Geomancy
Subtype: Metal
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster takes two pieces of a broken metal object and mends them back into one. Alternatively, the spell may be used on a creature wearing metal armor such as platemail, restoring 2d10 physical armor.
