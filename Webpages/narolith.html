Information

The two rugs are woven from the essence of a person's sins and good deeds, embodied by contrasting elements of light and darkness. Upon death, these elements unfurl like carpets, revealing the moral tapestry of the deceased's life. The length of each rug determines the individual's fate, with the longer one tipping the balance of their destiny. Narolith presides over the judgment of souls upon death, a process marked by the dramatic removal and bifurcation of the heart, transforming it into two distinct rugs.

Before his transformation into a Tenant, Narolith was once the most feared of all deities. His dominion over death endowed the Abyssal Brotherhood alliance with immortality, enabling them to rival even the most powerful divine coalitions. His followers, driven by a fervent zeal, sought to spread death to all who opposed him. However, Kelria, the goddess of night's cleric, wielded a relic of immense power unknown to the new gods. This artifact, the "Sword of Desolation," was potent enough to dismantle Narolith's control over death, leading to the catastrophic downfall of his church. The clerics of Kelria used this sword to decimate Narolith's followers, causing a schism among his devotees. Fleeing into the desert from whence they came, the surviving members of the Triarchy began to fear death, feeling abandoned by their god. Only the most devout retained their faith, while the mass slaughter paradoxically enabled Narolith to remain a greater deity within the pantheon.

Appearance

Narolith is an entity composed entirely of shadows, his form perpetually shifting and changing like smoke or mist. His body is slender and tall, with long, spindly limbs that stretch and twist in unnatural ways. The darkness constituting his form absorbs all light and sound around him, making the air grow colder and quieter in his presence. Despite lacking discernible facial features, the weight of his gaze is palpable, settling upon one's shoulders like a heavy cloak. When Narolith speaks, his voice is barely more than a whisper, yet it echoes within one's mind with a chilling, otherworldly resonance.
