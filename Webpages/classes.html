Grove Keeper


Grove Keepers are the sworn guardian protectors of nature and the wild. Grove Keepers have dedicated themselves to the plants and animals around them and are given powers from spirits and natural forces to protect their existence. These sources are what grant power to Grove Keepers over natural forces they use to strike down their foes. Grove Keepers use any material that is unprocessed past its natural state. Grove Keepers may use copper and tin in weapons, armor, and other equipment but refuse to use bronze and other alloys. Naturally occurring mixtures of elements such as obsidian are still viable and common among Grove Keepers.

The most powerful source of a Grove Keeper's power is his grove. A grove is an area of dense natural energy that grants a Grove Keeper powers and is usually protected by one or more spirit and the various Grove Keepers it empowers. Starting a Grove is no easy task as one must be an accomplished Grove Keeper to create their own.

The most common groves orders include The Order of the Emerald Arrow, The Order of the Amethyst Wing, and The Order of the Amber Leaf. The members of these groves are aligned with Cescylla or one of the four spirit courts. A typical Grove Keeper will begin by offering aid to their local area or protecting a large tree or plot of land from human encroachment. As the Grove Keeper gains power they may find the natural energy in their grove increasing and be able to start a new order under their control.

Hunter


Hunters prefer ranged assaults on their adversaries taking away escape before landing a fatal strike on opponents. Hunters are often contracted as bounty hunters or assassins to kill targets. Marking creatures with a magic escape from a Hunter is nearly impossible. Hunters usually have formal military training and have a great concept of tactics, Their incredible wits and Finesse allow them to easily identify and strike the vitals of foes they face in combat.

Mage


Many are often born without magical prowess in their blood. As a result, they spend much time practicing the arcane arts to learn magic. Those who learn magic in this way are known as mages. Mages are scholars first and magic casters second. They gain the ability to tap the arcane arts spending after years studying the weave and the world to gain control over it. This usually leads to them having knowledge in various subjects through their upbringing. Mages are often held in high regard by nobles and royalty and will often serve as advisors for their knowledge.

Pact Keeper


Pact Keepers gain power from a foreign source usually a malevolent entity to complete a certain objective or task. The benefactor grants the Pact Keeper power to uphold the benefactors' goal. The more powerful a pact keeper becomes the more power and influences their patron gains on them. A weak pact keeper may lose their sanity for breaking a pact, while a powerful pact keeper may be reduced to a pile of gore with no soul for breaking its pact if its benefactor so desires. To ensure their future loyalty or as payment the pact keeper gives their soul or something of equivalent value to the pact keeper to the benefactor. If the object of value loses its power or significance to the keeper it must sacrifice a new piece of itself or be left to the benefactor's mercy. Pact Keepers must honor the pact they make to a benefactor but may rebel against a benefactor in ways that don't slow or stop the goals of the pact or face the reality of their choices.

Most Benefactors are magically bound to fulfil a pact to the letter by cosmic law even the most powerful fiend, angel, or spirit cannot break. Benefactors have many reasons for granting mortals pacts from pure sadistic curiosity to revenge upon another entity. Benefactors may grant power beyond the scope of the pact to further their own ends or reward a particularly loyal pact keeper for fulfilling its task. Benefactors can grant magical powers, knowledge, objects for mortal souls.

The Pact Keeper is always bound to one benefactor at a time and will have a unique mark upon them to signify the contract between the two beings. Most marks are magical tattoos but others are unique to the benefactor such as a token of agreement, a enchanted soul jar or simply a contract written on paper signed in blood. Pact Keepers receive their first powers and mark immediately after signing the pact but others may not receive it until after fulfilling their part of the bargain. Some pact keepers may receive more than one mark signifying more than one benefactor. These are usually small or subtle marks but a Pact Keeper is bound by cosmic law to never be able to offer more the same payment to multiple benefactors.

Phantom

Phantoms are operatives of criminal organizations or spies for kingdoms or noblemen. They specialize in stealth and information gathering. Phantoms don't operate in the open often resorting to disguises or remaining unseen while working. The best phantoms in history are forgotten over time as only a failure would have their name outed and recorded in the books. Phantoms have a dedicated secret language only others in the business understand. These are symbols that mark things for people to know such as information on if a shop is an easy place to rob and the last time someone hit it. Hidden symbols that mark gang territory or a hidden drug trafficking ring in a tavern.

Priest


Worshippers are mortal emissaries of the gods allowing them to exert their will and spread their name through the world without the use of angels or direct involvement. The exceptionally devout in their worship performing rituals and gaining followers are granted power by the deity giving them abilities similar to their angels. Priests are loyal to their deities and place their will above all else. Leading the temples of the gods they help others to follow their ways helping the sick and wounded to the best of their abilities.

Warrior


Warriors spent their lives training in the ways of the blade. After years of dedication, they became soldiers in many battles fighting to defend or conquer for their cause. As a result, warriors are the most known class as a blade can be picked up by anyone to become a warrior after training enough.

Saves

Average saves add half level to the save
