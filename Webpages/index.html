Sparks of Dynasty is a fantasy role-playing game centered on magic, swordsmanship, and archery, inspired by role-playing games, folklore, and fantasy staples from around the world.

The rules are designed to keep the game fair and balanced while leaving room for every table to find its own kind of fun. Some groups love vicious combat where each character scrapes by on good rolls and battle-hardened tactics; others prefer exploring new lands, roleplaying with NPCs, and light-hearted adventure. Sparks is built to support both.
