Grace


Range: Touch
AP Cost: 2
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Heavenly
Subtype: Mercy
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: 6 Hours
Concentration: No

The caster touches a creature granting it a +1 bonus to the targets Resistance (Mind), Resistance (Body), or Evasion saves. If the target fails a save with the bonus, it can end the spell early allowing it to reroll the save.
