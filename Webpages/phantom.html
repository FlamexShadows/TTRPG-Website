Class Features
Shadow Sigil
Stating at 1st level a Phantom gains a sigil as a sign of loyalty to an organization or faction that they have connections with. When presented to a faction agent the creature can gain one of the following benefits.

Criminal Underworld
% off all black market purchases equal to double the Phantoms Level

Seal of Order
The Phantom is given a safe environment for sleep by its organization. In addition, the phantom is granted a % bonus of gold equal to equal to their level from their faction.

Freelancer/Diplomat
The Phantom gains a bonus to all charisma skills equal to half their Phantom level rounded down (Minimum +1)

In addition to the shadow sigil, Phantoms learn a special language that makes it easier for them to learn information about a certain area. Language is called "Code" and only phantoms know it.

Deathly Blades
Starting at 5th level all attacks made by a Phantom ignore 10 resistance.

Cloak of Shadow
Starting at 10th level a Phantom gains the ability to spend 6 AP and become invisible for 1 minute.

Precise Puncture
Starting at 15th level a Phantom gains the ability to puncture a target with deadly accuracy. For 10 stamina the phantom strikes a target with weapon that deals physical damage. On a hit the target takes 5d6 true damage and the target takes 10 points of true damage for every meter it walks, burrows or flies until the end of its next turn.

Trained Instincts
Starting at 20th level a Phantom gains the ability to spot traps and lies easier. The Phantom gains a +5 to all Intuition contests against Deception and a +5 to Sense Environment to find hidden objects or traps.

Creeping Death
Starting at 25th level a Phantom gains an additional 5 damage die-on attacks made with light or ranged weapons.



Subclasses

Thief


Practiced Hand
Starting at 1st level a Thief phantom gains levels in the sleight of hand and sneak skills equal to their thief level.

Magic Adept
Starting at 3rd level a Thief Phantom gains the ability to use all magic items in their possession that require the mana for stamina instead.

Fast Footwork
Starting at 8th level the movement speed of a Thief Phantom increases by 1 meter. This bonus increases by +1 at levels 12, 16, 20, and 24.

Adept Handiwork
Starting at 13th level the Thief Phantom gains the ability to use consumables, and activate equipment and magic items for 1 AP less (Minimum of 1).

Acute Senses
Starting at 18th level the Thief Phantom gains the ability to dodge effects with ease. When subjected to an evasion or overpower checks the phantom is only displaced or damaged half as much.

Elusive
Staring at 23rd level a Thief Phantom is Elusive physically and vocally. The Phantom gains levels in Deception and Evasion saves equal to their level. These skills stack with the freelancer/diplomat levels.

Architect


Inquisitive
Starting at 1st level the Architect gains a +5 bonus to intuition checks.

Tactician
Starting at 3rd level an Architect Phantom gains the ability to plan ahead. When initiative is rolled the Architect may rearrange the placement of allies in the initiative with each others scores.

Leadership
Starting at 8th level the architect grants a bonus to attack rolls and skill checks to themselves and allies they help or when helped equal to half their level. In addition, the architect may reroll any teamwork check they make twice and take the higher roll.

Connections
Starting at 13th level an Architect Phantom may select another Shadow Sigil. In addition when the Phantom rolls initiative the help action now costs 1 AP.

Multitasker
Starting at 18th level once per turn when the Architect Phantom uses the help Action they may attack once themselves. This feature is not subjected to effects or features that grant extra attacks.

Formation Expert
Starting at 23rd level when the Architect Phantom spends 20 stamina coordinating a deadly assault on a creature. Until the start of the phantom's next turn the Phantom and its allies' attacks deal an additional 5d4 true damage to the selected target.

Assassin


Ambush
Starting at 1st level an Assassin's critical range increases by 20 when unseen by a creature they attack.

Silent Shadow
Starting at 3rd level an Assassin learns to hide their movement. When the Assassin moves their movement makes no sound.

Disguise Master
Starting at 8th level an Assassin gains the ability to change their appearance. The Assassin may change their face to an alternate one they have made. Doing so can be done so swiftly it costs no AP. An Assassin only has one alternate face they can use.

Killing Intent
Starting at 13th level an Assassin gains the ability to exude a killing intent. When the Assassin uses this ability it chooses a creature within 15 meters it can see. The target must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save or become fearful of the Assassin.

Silent Death
Starting at 18th level an Assassin learns to prevent a creature from making noise. When the Assassin makes a successful attack the target must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or become silenced for 1 minute.

Vital Sense
Starting at 23rd level an Assassin gains the ability to tell when a creature is to die. When the assassin hits a creature they learn the current health of the creature as it is not an outsider, undead, or golem.

Pirate


Pirate's Proficiency
Starting at 1st level a Pirate gains proficiency in firearms and seaships.

Swashbuckler
Starting at 3rd level a Pirate no longer suffers penalties with ranged attacks when a creature is within melee range.

Sailor's Balance
Starting at 8th level a Pirate gains levels in Balance and Traverse Finesse equal to their level.

Iron Liver
Starting at 13th level a Pirate gains the ability to drink any liquid without being poisoned. In addition, the Pirate gains a +5 bonus to all resistance checks against poison.

Drunkard's Luck
Starting at 18th level a Pirate gains the ability to reroll any attack roll or check that they make. This feature can only be used once per combat.

Dirty Fighter
The Pirate gains the Dirty Tactics Maneuver and can use it without spending stamina.

Charlatan


Confidence
Starting at 1st level a Charlatan gains a +5 bonus to all Deception checks.

Distraction
Starting at 3rd level a Charlatan gains the ability to create a distraction to create a better opportunity to strike. When the Charlatan makes an attack on a creature that can understand them, they can roll deception against the targets intuition. On a successful check the Charlatan gains a bonus on the attack roll equal to the difference in the results. The Charlatan may use this ability once per turn.

Snake Oil Salesman
Starting at 8th level a Charlatan gains the ability to sell items for 50% more than their value.

Con Artist
Starting at 13th level a Charlatan gains the ability to create an illusion of a small object or creature. Any creature that interacts with the illusion must succeed on a Intuition check or fall under the effects of the Mental Acrobatics spell.

Vanishing Act
Starting at 18th level a Charlatan gains the ability to disappear from sight. A number of times equal to their charisma modifier, the charlatan can teleport to a space within 10 meters.

Rewrite Incident
Starting at 23rd level a Charlatan gains the ability to rewrite an event that has happened in another creature's mind. Once per day the Charlatan can force a creature to succeed a Resistance (Mind) save or fall under the effects of the Alter Memory spell.

Honeypot


Alluring Words
Starting at 1st level a Honeypot gains a +5 bonus to all Allure checks.

Siren's Tongue
Starting at 3rd level a Honeypot gains the ability to charm a creature thats not hostile and can understand them. The Honeypot can use this ability a number of times equal to their charisma modifier. The target must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save or be charmed for 1 hour.

Master Ambusher
Starting at 8th level a Honeypot gains the ability to set up an ambush on a creature. A creature that is charmed by the Honeypot is stunned when the honeypot or any creatures they consider an ally attacks the creature. This ability does not function on creatures hostile to the Honeypot.

Master of Speech
Starting at 13th level a Honeypot gains the ability to speak any language. In addition the Honeypot can attempt to temporarily dominate a creature it has charmed. The target must succeed on Resistance (Mind) save or become dominated by the Honeypot for 1 minute. After this minute ends the creature knows it was charmed.

Disarming Beauty
Starting at 18th level a Honeypot any creature that attempts the harm the only the Honeypot that can be attracted to them must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save or be forced to select a new target or waste the attack, maneuver, spell or ability.

Web of Lies
Starting at 23rd level a Honeypot can choose to succeed on a failed Charisma check, force a creature to reroll against its siren's tongue or disarming beauty. A Honeypot can only use this ability once per roll. The Honeypot can use this ability a number of times equal to their charisma modifier.

Ninja


Shinobi
Starting at 1st level a Ninja deals an additional 1d6 true damage to creatures who are unaware of it. This damage increases by 1d6 for every 3 levels they possess.

Shadow Stealth
Starting at 3rd level a Ninja gains the ability to hide in plain sight when in Dim Light or Darkness.

Ninja's Agility
Starting at 8th level when a Ninja makes an attack they can attempt a sneak check once per turn.

Ninja's Reflexes
Starting at 13th level a Ninja gains the ability to dodge attacks with ease. When a Ninja is hit by an attack roll they can use 5 stamina to take half damage instead.

Ninja's Precision
Starting at 18th level all weapons a Ninja uses gain a level of deadly and the hidden property.

Afterimages
Starting at 23rd level a Ninja gains the ability to create an afterimage of itself. When the ninja lands a critical or decisive strike they can roll an extra attack for no AP.
