Tomb of Ice


Range: Touch
AP Cost: 4
Spell Rank: Grand
Class: Hydromancy
Subtype: Ice
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: Permanent
Concentration: No

The caster targets a creature they can touch, encapsulating it in ice. The creature if it has no magical armor must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or becomes frozen solid. While frozen the creature has 0 AP. The tomb has 500 health, must remain in an area below the freezing point of water or begin melting, taking 10 damage at the start of each of its turns. This damage can be raised or lowered depending on the temperature difference from the freezing point by the Game Master.
