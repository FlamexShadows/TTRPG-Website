Oil Field


Range: 20 Meters
AP Cost: 2
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Geomancy
Subtype: Rock
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: Yes

The caster creates a field of oil at a point within range causing a 10 meter diameter circle of ground to become hard to traverse. Any creature that walks across the afflicted area must succeed on a Traverse (Finesse) check or be knocked down and reducing it's movement to 0 until the end of its turn. The oil field may also be lit ablaze creating a field of fire. Any creature within the radius while burning loses 20 resistance to fire and takes 1 damage for each die rolled when taking fire damage.
