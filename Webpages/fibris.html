Information

In a world permeated with ancient myths and mystical creatures, Marissa, a revered water deity, found herself pregnant by one of her brides. The thought of being eternally bound to the depths of the sea filled him with a yearning for the surface above. Unable to bear the confinement any longer, he broke free, swimming swiftly towards the sunlit realm. Marissa, enraged by his betrayal, pursued him relentlessly. She churned the waters, creating treacherous rip currents and a swirling vortex known as the Eye of Marissa. Despite her tempestuous fury, he managed to reach the surface. In a fit of anger and desperation, she hurled herself out of the water, trident in hand, ready to exact revenge on the treacherous traitor. However, as she lunged towards him, a miracle occurred. Marissa gave birth to a radiant child named Fibris, cradled in the palm of her hand. In a moment of profound mercy, she granted her former bride a reprieve, allowing their child to float gracefully towards the heavens. With a touch of her divine power, Marissa created an ethereal barrier, separating the water from the land. A magnificent, pearlescent blue dome stretched across the sky, mirroring the shimmering surface of her aquatic domain. Fibris, blessed with her mother's cerulean hue, ascended to her celestial abode, bringing forth enchanting wonders.

Each day, Fibris engages in a playful celestial dance with Kelria, the luminous spirit of the stars. Upon her playful defeat, Fibris orchestrated a cosmic spectacle, exchanging the moon's place with the sun. However, this celestial ballet was not permanent; every ten hours, she diligently returned the moon to its rightful position. Before every significant competition in the mortal realm, Fibris's avatar, a majestic avian creature, soared across the sky, visible only to those who held unwavering faith in her divine presence. Believers who doubted their chances of success sought her favor, offering heartfelt prayers and sacrifices to invoke her benevolent influence and secure a favorable outcome.

Appearance

To the denizens of the open skies, Fibris reveals herself as a majestic eagle, her wings outstretched in a graceful arc that spans the heavens. Each feather shimmers with the ethereal hues of dawn and dusk, casting a radiant glow that imparts wisdom to those who seek her counsel. Her piercing gaze mirrors the boundless expanse of the firmament, offering clarity and insight to those who dare to soar to greater heights. For those who tread upon the earthly realm, Fibris assumes the guise of a magnificent Avatol, her form radiating an otherworldly beauty that captivates the senses. Adorned with vibrant plumage and adorned with celestial jewels, she embodies the harmonious balance between earth and sky. Her presence brings solace and inspiration to those who walk the land, guiding them with a gentle hand towards the path of enlightenment. In the depths of the ocean's embrace, Fibris takes on the form of a winged Aquarii, her translucent figure weaving through the azure depths with an ethereal grace. Her skin, like the shimmering surface of the sea, is transparent and illuminated from within, revealing a celestial vista of clear skies and billowing clouds. Here, amidst the tranquil depths, she imparts her wisdom to those who seek solace beneath the waves, offering guidance and enlightenment to those who traverse the watery realms.
