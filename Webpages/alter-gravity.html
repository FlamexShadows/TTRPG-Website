Alter Gravity


AP Cost: 4 AP
Spell Rank: Major
Class: Chronomancy
Subtype: Space
Range: 15 Meters
Requirements: Words of Power, Iron weights worth 200 GP
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: Yes

The caster alters gravity in a 5 meter cube at a point of their choice within range. Any creature within range of the spell falls into the direction of the new gravitational pull. The pull can be directed anyway horizontally, diagonally or vertically. While concentrating on the spell, for 2 AP the caster can spend an additional 10 Mana to change the direction of the gravity to a different orientation in the cube.
