Soul Bargain


Range: Touch
AP Cost: 2
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Demonology
Subtype: Tether
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: 1 Hour
Concentration: No

THe target gains 10 true armor for the duration. When the spell ends, the caster regains health equal to any of those temporary health that remain. When upcast the temporary health increases by 5 for each tier upcast.
