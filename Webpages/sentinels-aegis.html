Sentinel's Aegis


Range: Self
Action Cost: Reaction
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Geomancy
Subtype: Rock
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

As a reaction, the caster shifts the earth to mitigate an attack that would hit them. The caster gains a +2 bonus to AC against one melee or ranged attack that would hit them. If the attack misses, the caster can choose to move their speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
