Bhaalnuva's Gambit

Master
AP: 6
Requirements: Fortitude 30
Type: Attack
Range: Touch
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The martialist gambits their physical armor in a duel of endurance against the target. The martialist's physical armor is compared to the creature they are currently gambling against. The creature with the lower physical armor has its armor reduced to 0 and is Stunned for 1 minute. The stunned creature may roll a Resistance (Mind) save at the beginning of each their turns to end the stunned effect.
