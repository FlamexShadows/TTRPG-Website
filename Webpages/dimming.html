Dimming


Range: 15 meters
AP Cost: 1
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Tenemancy
Subtype: Penumbra
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: 10 Minutes
Concentration: No

The caster dims the light of the targeted area of the spell. This blot of darkness appears as a 10 meter radius sphere centered at the point the caster chooses and any creature that looks into or tries to see past the area is affected by darkness.
