Shadow Champion


Range: 20 meters
AP Cost: 3
Spell Rank: Major
Class: Tenemancy
Subtype: Umbral
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster animates the shadow of a creature they can see within range. The shadow attacks its creator using the caster's spell attack modifier. On a hit the creature takes 5d8 psychic damage and must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save or begin draining until the end of their next turn.
