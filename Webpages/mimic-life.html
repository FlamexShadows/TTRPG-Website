Mimic Life


Casting Time: 1 Hour
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Necromancy
Subtype: Puppetry
Range: 5 Meters
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: Permanent
Concentration: No

The caster targets the corpse of a creature reanimating it with a semblance of life. The creature is reanimated as a zombie if it has flesh or a skeleton if it remains as bones. This spell fails to animate a body missing a head.
