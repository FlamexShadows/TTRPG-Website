Transport through Decay


Range: Touch
AP Cost: 1 Minute
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Necromancy
Subtype: Decay
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster touches remains of any dead creature, mushrooms or other decomposers attached to it. The caster can then transport themselves to any corpse within 1.6 kilometers of the casting point.
