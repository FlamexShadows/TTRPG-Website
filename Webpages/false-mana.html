False Mana


Range: Self
AP Cost: 1
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Otherworldly
Subtype: Illusion
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: 8 Hours
Concentration: No

The caster creates an aura of false mana presenting themselves as a caster of a tier higher than what they are capable of casting. Casters who can cast minor spells appear as Adept, appear as major, major appear as grand, and grand appear as divine for the purpose of identification. While the spell is active if the caster runs out of mana, the caster may substitute their health for mana when casting spells. If a spell would reduce the caster to 0 health, it fails to cast.
