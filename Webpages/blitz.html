Blitz

Advanced
AP: 5
Requirements: -
Type: Attack
Range: 15 Meters
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The martialist dashes between two targets within 15 meters of themselves and 5 meters of each other appearing adjacent to the second target. They may make an attack roll against each target dealing 5d6 slashing damage on a successful hit. The martialist ends the maneuver adjacent to the second target. If the second target has no unoccupied adjacent tiles they are not targeted and if the first target has no open adjacent tiles the maneuvers fails.
