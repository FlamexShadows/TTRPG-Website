Gaze of Flesh


Range: Touch
Casting Time: 10 Minutes
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Necromancy
Subtype: Tether
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: 1 Hour / 1 Week
Concentration: No

The caster casts this spell and within 1 hour after casting has 1 hour to touch a creature marking it for later viewing. Once the caster touches a creature it becomes marked and any creature that inspects the target (Including Itself) may make a DC 20 Medicine check to spot the mark while viewing itself in a matter like a mirror or medical inspection. The creature may attempt to remove the mark by making a DC 15 Knowledge (Arcana). While marked the caster may attempt to scry on the marked target. The target must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save or the caster is able to perceive the targets environment through its senses. The target is not aware the caster is scrying on them. If the target succeeds on its check the mark dissipates and the spell ends.
