Time Loop


AP Cost: 4 AP
Spell Rank: Grand
Class: Chronomancy
Subtype: Time
Range: 20 Meters
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: 10 Minutes
Concentration: No

The caster targets one creature it can see within 10 meters of itself. That creature if it has no magic armor must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save. On a failure the target becomes trapped in an instance of time until the spell ends. The target vanishes into a pocket in time disappearing from its reality until the loop is ended. The target then becomes stuck in a never ending looping series of events. The spell can be dispelled on its casters origin point when the spell was casted. The target of the spell may realize it is stuck in a loop of reoccurring events. At the start of each of its turns the creature must attempt on a DC 40 Wits check. If a creature successfully realizes it is in a time loop it may attempt to break out. Doing so requires a DC 10 Intelligence check.
