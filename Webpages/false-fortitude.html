False Fortitude


Range: Self
AP Cost: 1
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Otherworldly
Subtype: Illusion
Requirements: Sigils
Duration: 8 Hours
Concentration: No

The caster invigorates themselves with an aura of false fortitude appearing as an extremely durable and healthy creature. When the caster is reduced to 0 health, they instead remain at 1 health. Any damage that would have reduced them below that 1 health is instead subtracted from their mana.
