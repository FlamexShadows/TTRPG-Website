Omnipresence


Range: Unlimited
Casting Time: 24 Hours
Spell Rank: Grand
Class: Chronomancy
Subtype: Dimensions
Requirements: Sigils, Words of Power, Incense worth 1000 GP, Soul Gems worth 100,000 GP per duplicate
Duration: 1 Year
Concentration: No

Often used by archmagi to maintain a tower while in a council with the royal court, the caster creates 1d4+1 duplicates of themselves anywhere in the world they are aware of acting as if they were in the area of all the places at once. The caster acts as themselves through these duplicates. The caster can choose to cast spells through the duplicates and all duplicates share the caster's health and armor, mana, and stamina pools.
