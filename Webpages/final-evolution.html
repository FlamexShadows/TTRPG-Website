Final Evolution


Casting Time: 1 Hour
Spell Rank: Grand
Class: Biomancy
Subtype: Evolution
Range: Self
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils, 10 Mutagen
Duration: 1 Day
Concentration: Yes

The most pinnacle of Biomancy, the caster surpasses their natural limits modifying their body to become the perfect specimen. The caster may choose up to 5 Biomancy spells of Major or lower gaining their effects as if the spells were upcast to Grand for the duration of the spell. The caster may choose to replicate the same spell on themselves so long as they choose a different effect from the spell for each time its chosen.
