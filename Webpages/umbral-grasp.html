Umbral Grasp


Range: 5 Meters
AP Cost: 3
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Tenemancy
Subtype: Penumbra
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster targets a creature in range as 1d4+1 bolts of darkness form shooting at the target. The caster makes a single attack roll. On a hit, the creature takes 1d6 cold damage for each bolt rolled.
