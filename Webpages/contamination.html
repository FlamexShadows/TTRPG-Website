Contamination


Range: Touch
AP Cost: 2 AP
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Transmutation
Subtype: Transformation
Requirements: Words of Power, A vial of poison
Duration: Permanent
Concentration: No

The caster touches a piece of food or a drink contaminating it with pollutants. The food or drink becomes inedible, spoiling the drink. Any food or drink affected by this spell is clearly unfit for consumption to an observer able to smell or see the food appearing rotten and smelling of vinegar. Any creature that consumes the contaminated food becomes Poisoned for 1 hour.
