Lance of Tar


Range: 5 Meters
AP Cost: 3 AP
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Geomancy
Subtype: Rock
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster creates a 1 meter wide and 10 meter long. Each creature caught in this line must succeed on Evasion save or take 1d4 earth damage and become pinned until the end of its next turn.
