Character Creation

Basic Rules, updated November 26, 2026

To create a level 1 character, choose a Race, Class, and Subclass, generate your seven statistics, spend your starting gold, and pick your starting spells or Martial Maneuvers, skills, and feat.

Stat Generation

  • Selective Array: assign the seven preset scores (12, 12, 10, 8, 7, 6, 4) to any statistics you choose.
  • Rolled Stats (4d4 drop lowest): roll 4d4 and keep the highest 3 dice seven times, then assign each total to one statistic.
  • Apply your race and size bonuses, then spend 2 leveling points from the Leveling table.

Starting Equipment & Abilities

  • Starting Gold: level 1 characters start with 1,000 GP to purchase weapons, armor, shields, and adventuring gear.
  • Level 1 Unlocks: you start with Minor spell or Tier 1 maneuver slots from your class (plus 1 extra per +1 Memory modifier) and 1 feat.
