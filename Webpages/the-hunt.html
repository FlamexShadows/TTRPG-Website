The Hunt


Range: Self
AP Cost: 4
Spell Rank: Major
Class: Nature
Subtype: Primal
Requirements: Sigils
Duration: 10 Minutes
Concentration: Yes

The caster becomes the embodiment of a predator. Until the spell ends, they gain an additional 2 movement, and their teeth and nails elongate into weapons that deal 5d6 physical damage. A thick hide forms over them granting 10d10 physical armor and 10d10 magical armor.
