Debilitation

Advanced
AP: 4
Requirements: Strength 20
Type: Attack
Range: Weapon
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: Yes

The martialist delivers a powerful blow to a target. The martialist makes a maneuver attack roll. On a hit the target becomes slowed and takes 8d4 bludgeoning damage. At the start of each of the creatures turns it may make a Resistance (Body) save to end the slowed condition.
