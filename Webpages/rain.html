Rain


Range: Self
AP Cost: 1
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Hydromancy
Subtype: Water
Requirements: Sigils, Words of Power
Duration: 1 Hour
Concentration: No

The caster creates precipitation around themselves in a 100 meter radius. While raining the area becomes lightly obscured and the ground becomes covered in water on the start of the casters next turn. When upcast to Major the radius increases to 500 Meters and at Grand the radius increases to 1 Kilometer.
