Flaming Weapon


Range: Touch
AP Cost: 2
Spell Rank: Major
Class: Pyromancy
Subtype: Flame
Requirements: Words of Power, Sulfur
Duration: 10 Minutes
Concentration: Yes

The caster targets one weapon within range, coating it in sulfur and lightning it ablaze. The weapon deals an additional 2d6 fire damage. Any creature struck must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or begin burning. Any creature that is burning stops burning at the beginning of its next turn.
