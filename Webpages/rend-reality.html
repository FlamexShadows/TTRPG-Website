Rend Reality


Range: 100 Meters
AP Cost: 4
Spell Rank: Grand
Class: Otherworldly
Subtype: Chaos
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: 1 Hour
Concentration: No

The caster harnesses the power of the void partially manifesting it into reality in a 15 meter sphere. All creatures that are not outsiders besides the caster within range must make a Resistance (Mind) save. On a failure the creature takes 30d10 magic damage becomes cursed and silenced as the area becomes part of the empty. On a successful save the creature takes half as much damage and suffers no additional effects.
