Watch History


Casting Time: 10 Minutes
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Chronomancy
Subtype: Time
Range: Self
Requirements: Words of Power, Crystal ball worth 1000 GP
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: No

The caster uses the crystal ball to see through the past. The caster chooses an object in its possession gaining information about its past. The object can be a mundane item, magic item or a body part from a creature. As a result the caster gains 3 pieces of information about the item's past.
