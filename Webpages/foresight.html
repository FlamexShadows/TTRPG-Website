Foresight


Range: Touch
Casting Time: 1 Minute
Spell Rank: Grand
Class: Chronomancy
Subtype: Time
Requirements: Words of Power, Hourglass worth 3000 GP consumed on casting
Duration: 24 Hours
Concentration: No

This spell functions as deja vu but instead lasts for 24 hours and the caster gains 10 bonus on Resistance (Body) and Evasion save.
