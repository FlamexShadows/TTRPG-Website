Repurpose Vitality


AP Cost: 3
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Demonology
Subtype: Damnation
Range: Touch
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster drains the life essence of the target. The caster makes a magic attack roll on a hit dealing 1d6 unholy. The stolen energy can be absorbed to heal the caster for half the damage dealt or empower the next demonology spell they cast to be counted as upcast one tier higher.
