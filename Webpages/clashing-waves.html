Dueling Waves


Range: Sight
AP Cost: 3
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Hydromancy
Subtype: Water
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: Yes

The caster creates 2 identical parallel waves of violently upward flowing water 2 meters apart that span 10 meters high that are 15 meters long. Any ranged attacks made through this wave takes a -10 penalty and fire and gasses such as those produced by smoke screen or soothing fog cannot pass through the waves. At the end of the spell's duration the waves fall into the ground. Each creature in between the waves takes 10d8 physical damage. A successful Resistance (Body) save halves this damage.
