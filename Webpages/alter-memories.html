Alter Memories


Range: 1 Meter
AP Cost: 6
Spell Rank: Major
Class: Enchantment
Subtype: Charm
Requirements: Verbal, Words of Power, an object connected to the creature.
Duration: 30 days
Concentration: No

The caster targets an unconscious creature within range. The target must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save or become charmed. While charmed the caster whispers the memory they wish to change and what they wish to change it to and the creature believes these memories as true.

The spell automatically fails if the implanted memories would directly cause future self-harm. Memories of actions being performed in the past of such nature can still be implanted.
