Trembling Rage

Expert
AP: 5
Requirements: Charisma 25
Type: Enhancement
Range: 10 Meters
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: No

The martialist unleashes a rage fueled battle scream that fills enemy hearts with fear. All hostile creatures within 10 meters of them must succeed on a (Resistance) Mind save or become fearful of them. A creature that fails this save must repeat its save at the start of its next turn. On a failure it becomes hopeless. In addition the martialist gains an additional 2d10 true damage on each of their attacks against fearful and hopeless targets from this maneuver for its duration.
