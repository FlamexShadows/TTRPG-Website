Gut of Lead


AP Cost: 4
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Demonology
Subtype: Torment
Range: 10 Meters
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: Yes

The caster attempts to create a spiked ball of sharp metal in a target's stomach. The target must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or become nauseated and crippled for the duration.
