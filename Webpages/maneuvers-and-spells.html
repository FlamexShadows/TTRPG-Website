Maneuvers and Spells

Basic Rules, updated November 26, 2026

Spells and Martial Maneuvers are skills learned through training. Spells require Mana to cast, and maneuvers require Stamina. Stamina and Mana are two forms of a creature's source. Both are magical. A creature needs a soul to have source. Spells are channeled through words of power and arcane sigils drawn in the air, while Martial Maneuvers are skilled techniques requiring great physical ability.

Spells

Spells turn a creature's source into mana and shape it into effects. The schools of magic are Arcanomancy, Heavenly, Otherworldly, Nature, Chronomancy, Demonology, Enchantment, Necromancy, Transmutation, Hydromancy, Pyromancy, Geomancy, Aeromancy, Biomancy, Legimancy, Pneumamancy, and Tenemancy. Spells are ranked by the mana they cost: Minor, Adept, Major, Grand, and Divine. Mortals do not cast Divine spells. Lemara enforces this law. Mana costs by tier: Minor 5 Mana, Adept 10 Mana, Major 20 Mana, Grand 40 Mana. Words of Power are spoken arcane words that imbue magic into the world. Arcane Sigils are floating magical circuits created by a caster to direct and focus magic into a potent effect.

Anyone learns spells from Necromancy, Transmutation, Hydromancy, Pyromancy, Geomancy, and Aeromancy. Heavenly, Demonology, Arcanomancy, Nature, Otherworldly, Enchantment, Chronomancy, Biomancy, Legimancy, Pneumamancy, and Tenemancy are rare. You learn them only through a feature granting access.

Your casting stat is your class's primary stat.

Upcasting

Magic is volatile. Adding mana to a spell upcasts it and greatly empowers its effects. You upcast only to a tier you are able to cast: a level 5 character casts Minor spells only, so no upcasting to Adept or Major.

Upcast effects are geometric: a spell upcast from Minor to Grand is 8 times as potent. Each step, choose one effect:

  • Double the dice rolled.
  • Double the range.
  • Add your casting modifier if not already included.
  • Increase the duration one step: 1 round → 1 minute → 10 minutes → 1 hour → 6 hours → 12 hours → 1 day → 1 week → 1 month → 1 year → permanent. Instant spells gain no duration.

Concentration

Some spells and maneuvers list Concentration: Yes. You hold one concentration effect at a time. Using another concentration spell or maneuver ends the first one at once. Its effects stop, and you do not choose which one to keep.

Whenever a creature concentrating on an effect takes damage, it must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save. The DC is 10 or half the damage taken, whichever is higher. On a failure, the effect ends.

Concentration also ends when:

  • The duration runs out.
  • You choose to end it. This costs no AP.
  • You are stunned, paralyzed, unconscious, dying, or reduced to 0 maximum AP.
  • An effect says you cannot concentrate. Cloud Mind already does this.

Spells and maneuvers without Concentration: Yes do not count against your one effect.

Martial Maneuvers

Martial Maneuvers are a skilled move or sequence of attacks, movement, or defenses. They come in tiers like spells, ranging from Basic to Master. Stamina costs by tier: Basic 5 Stamina, Advanced 10 Stamina, Expert 15 Stamina, Master 20 Stamina. Unlike spells, no branch is closed to anyone, but a creature needs stats high enough to perform a maneuver.

Martial Maneuvers are not upcast but progressed. A progressed maneuver lists a lower-tier version as its prerequisite. After you progress a maneuver, you keep using its earlier versions. The progressed maneuver takes the earlier one's slot, so it needs no extra slot.
