Dance with Death


Range: Touch
Casting Time: 1 Minute
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Necromancy
Subtype: Death
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: 12 Hours
Concentration: No

While active a creature gains a +5 bonus to saves to avoid death effcts. While active if the target is to receive the dying or dies, condition, they can immediately end the spell and remain at 1 health.
