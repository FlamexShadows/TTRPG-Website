Envelop


Range: 10 Meters
AP Cost: 4
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Nature
Subtype: Primal
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: No

The caster envelops one creature or object within range in a swarm of insects. At the start of each of the creature's turns or when it comes into contact with a covered surface, it must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or take 5d6 physical damage and 2d6 poison damage or half as much on a success. If the creature takes fire, is teleported, or spends 2 AP the creature removes the swarm of insects from itself. After its removal or at the end of the spell's duration, the swarm disperses harmlessly.
