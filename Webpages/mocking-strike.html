Mocking Strike

Advanced
AP: 4
Requirements: Weapon
Type: Attack
Range: Weapon
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The martialist preforms an attack aimed to show clear combat superiority over the creature they are fighting. If the attack lands the creature must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save or become fearful of the martialist for 1 minute. The creature may repeat the save at the end of each of its turns or if it lands an attack against the martialist.
