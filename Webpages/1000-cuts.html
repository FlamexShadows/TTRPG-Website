Thousand Cuts

Basic
AP: 3
Requirements:
Type: Attack
Range: Weapon
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The martialist targets a creature within melee unleashing a flurry of precise cuts attempting to weaken the target of the attack. The martialist makes 5 attacks. On a hit the creature takes 1d4 slashing damage. If the creature is successfully hit by 3 or more attacks it becomes slowed until the end of its next turn. If the attacker lands all 5 attacks the creature takes an additional 5d6+Maneuver Score slashing damage.
