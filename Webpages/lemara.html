Information

Lemara, known as Lady Equilibrium, is the goddess dedicated to preserving balance in the world by any means necessary. Her influence is profound, particularly in the realm of magic, which she places under strict regulations. Her unyielding control over magic ensures that this powerful force remains balanced and prevents chaos. Lemara's authority is so absolute that even the other gods dare not challenge her, having witnessed her displays of overwhelming power against those who have tried in the past. Shrouded in mystery, much about Lemara remains unknown. Her true goals are enigmatic, and she prefers to keep her intentions concealed. One of her primary tenets is the prohibition of any depiction of her appearance, preventing any artwork from portraying her. This decree ensures that her image remains an enigma, adding to her aura of invincibility and supreme power.

Despite the lack of visual representations, Lemara's presence is undeniable and deeply felt. She is revered as the ultimate judge and protector of equilibrium, and her followers strictly adhere to her laws. They understand the crucial role she plays in maintaining the balance that sustains the world. Lemara is considered the most important deity because souls, which are essential for all living creatures, are created from magic. Without magic, and by extension without Lemara’s regulations, there would be no souls and thus no life.

Appearance

The only thing known about Lemara is that she wears a blindfold much like her daughter Bhera. Nothing else about the goddesses appearance is known never appearing in visage to even her most dedicated followers.
