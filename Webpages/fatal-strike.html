Fatal Strike
Tier: Expert
AP: 5
Requirements: Coup De Grace
Type: Attack
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The martialist attempts to land a vital strike on a creature within range of their weapon. The martialist makes a maneuver attack on a hit dealing 10d6 true damage. If the creature is left with less than 150 health the creature gains the dying condition but does not have its AP reduced or fall unconscious.
