Infinity


Range: Self
AP Cost: 6 AP
Spell Rank: Grand
Class: Chronomancy
Subtype: TIme
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: Yes

The caster creates a 10 meter radius void where time pauses. Any creature caught within the void must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save or have its AP reduced to 0. Any projectile or ray that extends into the void has is halted as it stops. Any spell or maneuver effects such as corrupt weapon or magnetize has its duration haulted for the duration of infinity. Instant effects produce no effect until the spell ends. All halted effects happen simultaneously as the spell ends.
