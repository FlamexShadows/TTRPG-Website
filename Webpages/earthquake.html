Earthquake


Range: Self
AP Cost: 4
Spell Rank: Grand
Class: Geomancy
Subtype: Earth
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: Yes

The caster rumbles the earth around themselves causing great damage to buildings and shaking the feet beneath creatures around themselves. The earthquake spans 10 kilometers destroying all small structures. Any creature that is underground in the radius of an earthquake spell takes 10d10 earth damage at the start of each of its turns. In addition, all creatures within range of an earthquake spell must succeed on a Balance check or be knocked down for the duration of the spell.
