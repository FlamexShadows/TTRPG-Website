Dirty Tactics

Advanced
AP: 3
Requirements:
Type: Attack
Range: Self
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The martialist breaks the conventional terms of what is traditionally accepted in combat. Doing so they catch their opponent off guard. If the creature is not chaotic the martialist gains advantage on the maneuver attack. On a successful hit the creature takes 4d6+maneuver modifier Piercing damage.
