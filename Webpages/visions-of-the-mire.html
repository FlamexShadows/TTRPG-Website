Visions of the Mire


Range: 10 Meters
AP Cost: 2
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Demonology
Subtype: Torment
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: No

The caster targets one creature it can see within range of itself. The targetmust succeed a Resistance (Mind) save. On a failure the target becomes Blind. While blind it becomes entrapped in a vision where its sight is blocked by a black inky substance only it can see. All noises are drowned out by the screaming of tormented souls of the underworld causing the creature to be Deaf. A creature may repeat the check at the end of each of its turns.
