Lacerating Rush

Advanced
AP: 4
Requirements: -
Type: Movement
Range: 5 meters
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The martialist dashes towards a creature magically enhancing their weapons and becoming temporarily incorporeal passing through all targets and objects. They move up to 5 meters and any target they pass through with this movement must succeed a Resistance (Body) save or take 8d6 slashing damage.
