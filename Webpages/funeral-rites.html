Funeral Rites


Range: Touch
AP Cost: 6
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Necromancy
Subtype: Death
Requirements: Words of Power, Incense, Sigils
Duration: Permanent
Concentration: No

The caster provides funeral rites for a dead creature marking the creature's body with various sigils and lightning incense. While warded by funeral rites any effect that raises a creature to undead that is cast at a lower tier spell than the funeral rites fail.
