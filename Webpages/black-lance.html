Black Lance


Range: 10 Meters
AP Cost: 4
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Arcanomancy
Subtype: Destruction
Requirements: Sigils
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster creates a 1 meter wide, 10 meter long beam of energy directed in a line. Any creature in it's area must succeed an Evasion save or take 5d6 magic damage and lose 5 resistance to magic damage.
