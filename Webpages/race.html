Dwarf

Stats: Fortitude +2
Size: Medium
Speed: Walk 4
Creature Type: Humanoid
Typical Alignment: Any Neutral
Racial Resistance: None
Lifespan: Dwarves age at the same rate as humans, reaching maturity at 18, but live upward of 150 years.
Racial Languages: Universal, Dwarvish

Racial Ability

Natural Resilience: dwarves are sturdy. Add double your level to your maximum Physical and Magical Armor.

Description

With their short stature and sturdy build, dwarves possess a muscular density that sets them apart from humans. This density, while granting them resilience to damage, hinders their natural buoyancy in water.

However, with diligent practice and determination, dwarves can overcome this challenge, becoming skilled swimmers. Their high muscle mass density and thick body hair contribute to their remarkable resilience and durability. As a result, dwarves have adapted to environments that pose various physical hazards.

Dwarves are renowned for their elaborate hairstyles, intricate braids, and well-groomed beards. Their abundant body hair holds cultural and societal significance. A dwarf with less hair is considered less authentic and may face exclusion from certain jobs and positions within their community.

Dwarves often exhibit a condescending attitude towards other humanoids due to their lack of hair. They perceive elves as particularly inferior, criticizing their technological ineptitude and delicate physiques. This perspective has led to many dwarves taking in races they deem inferior, offering shelter and protection when needed.

In their communities, dwarves maintain a strong sense of unity. They value hard work, craftsmanship, and loyalty. Dwarven strongholds are marvels of engineering, showcasing their exceptional skills in mining, metalworking, and architecture. They gather for grand feasts, enjoying hearty meals, music, and storytelling. Their festivities are a testament to their appreciation for life and camaraderie.

Avatol

Stats: Intelligence +2, Memory +1
Size: Medium
Speed: Walk 5, Fly 5
Creature Type: Humanoid
Typical Alignment: Any
Racial Resistance: None
Lifespan: Avatols age at the same rate as humans.
Racial Languages: Universal, 1 language of choice

Racial Ability

Skyborn: Fibris, goddess of the sky, blesses you. Choose 2 Minor Aeromancy spells. Cast each once per full rest without spending Mana.

Description

Avatol, the winged beings who share an uncanny resemblance to humans, possess a distinct feature that sets them apart: a majestic pair of wings, identical to those of a hawk or eagle. These wings are not mere appendages but symbols of their unique connection to the element of air. Blessed by Fibris, the Avatol are endowed with unparalleled control over the air. Their mastery allows them to manipulate air currents with precision, granting them the ability to soar through the skies with grace and agility.

Avatol communities, seeking to harness the benefits of their aerial prowess, often establish their settlements in places of high elevation. Mountain peaks, with their towering heights, provide the ideal setting for these winged beings to take advantage of the updrafts and soar effortlessly above the clouds. The tranquility of mountaintops also offers a sanctuary where the Avatol can easily defend from invaders.

In the verdant heart of Vonge Grove, nestled among the towering sequoia trees, the most famous Avatol community, Mulkitan, thrives. These colossal trees, reaching towards the heavens, serve as magnificent platforms for the Avatol to build their homes. From these lofty heights, they can survey the sprawling forest below, keeping a watchful eye over the delicate ecosystem they inhabit.

The Avatol way of life is deeply intertwined with the element of air. Their homes, constructed with lightweight materials, are designed to withstand the strong winds that buffet the mountaintops. They adorn themselves with feathers and other air-themed accessories, paying homage to their connection with the sky. Their language is filled with words and phrases that evoke the beauty and power of the wind, creating a rich tapestry of oral tradition that celebrates their unique heritage.

Within Avatol communities, individuals with exceptional air-bending are leaders of important roles such as governor or military general as they are seen as having a deeper connection to Fibris. These gifted beings serve as guardians of the skies, using their powers to protect their people and maintain the balance of the air around them. They are skilled in creating powerful gusts of wind, forming protective barriers, and even summoning storms when necessary.

Elf

Stats: Finesse +2
Size: Medium
Speed: Walk 5
Creature Type: Humanoid
Typical Alignment: Any Good
Racial Resistance: None
Lifespan: Elves mature at 30 and live upward of 250 years.
Racial Languages: Universal, Elvish

Racial Abilities

Nature's Embrace: add half your level to all Knowledge (Nature), Sense Environment, and Survival checks. When a natural environment causes difficult terrain, your movement penalty is halved.

Shared Memories: a number of times per full rest equal to your level, taste a creature's fresh flesh to read its memories. Choose one: add 1d4 to your next check with a skill the creature had ranks in, or learn one fact from its memories, chosen by the GM.

Description

Elves, with their towering stature surpassing even the tallest of humans, possess a slender and lanky build that sets them apart. Their elongated limbs, much like the branches of ancient trees, grant them remarkable agility and grace. The most striking features of an elf are their pointed ears, a defining characteristic that distinguishes them from other beings. Unlike humans, elves lack any hair on their bodies, save for their eyebrows and the graceful tresses that adorn their heads.

Deeply connected to the natural world, elves find harmony and solace in the embrace of nature. Many among them cultivate a profound knowledge of the plants and flowers that thrive in their verdant surroundings. Through meticulous study and experimentation, they create potent remedies that possess the ability to heal even the most afflicting diseases. Their expertise in natural medicine and their innate understanding of the environment make them sought-after guides for those seeking to navigate the dense and mystical woodlands of the world.

One of the most peculiar and intriguing traits of elves is their ability to perceive memories when they consume fresh, unblemished flesh. This extraordinary gift allows them to glimpse fragments of the life belonging to the creature from which the flesh originated. While some elves utilize this ability as a party trick to entertain non-elves, others find lucrative employment as investigators, using their unique talent to unravel mysteries and solve murders.

The elven culture is deeply intertwined with the natural world, and they hold a profound respect for the delicate balance of life. They view themselves as guardians of the environment, protectors of the ancient woodlands and the creatures that dwell within often leading to many becoming Grove Keepers. Their graceful movements, akin to the swaying of trees in the gentle breeze, embody their connection to nature. Elves often form close bonds with animals, communicating with them in ways that most other beings cannot comprehend. Their songs, a harmonious blend of mellifluous voices and the rustling of leaves, carry tales of ancient times and the timeless beauty of the natural world.

Human

Stats: Intelligence +2, Memory +2
Size: Medium
Speed: Walk 5
Creature Type: Humanoid
Typical Alignment: Any
Racial Resistance: None
Lifespan: Humans reach maturity at 18 and live up to 100 years.
Racial Languages: Universal, 1 language of choice

Racial Ability

Skilled: you gain extra skill ranks equal to your level.

Description

Humans are the most widespread race in the known world. They lack the long lives of elves and dwarves, reaching maturity at 18 and rarely living past 100, and no single deity blesses their blood. What humans have instead is drive. A human life is short, so humans learn fast, take up many trades, and push to leave their mark before their time runs out. This hunger shows in the wide range of skills a human masters.

Humans live in the cities, farms, and trade roads of every land. They live throughout Sol Inos, where the God King freed the land from the tyranny of the dragon lords, and they hold the walls of strongholds such as Wolren beside dwarves and beastmen. Human merchants carry Universal to every port and market, and human pilgrims fill the churches of Lemara.

Other races see humans in many ways. Dwarves look down on them for their lack of body hair. Elves find them restless and short-lived, yet the two races still produce half elves despite the danger of Root Rot. Half orcs, demi angels, and demi fiends often have a human parent. To the long-lived races, humans are a flood. They are rarely the strongest or the oldest, but they are everywhere, and they change the world faster than anyone else.

Demi Angel

Stats: Charisma +2, Wits +1
Size: Medium
Speed: Walk 5, Fly 5
Creature Type: Humanoid
Typical Alignment: Any Good
Racial Resistance: Holy 10
Lifespan: Demi angels mature at the same rate as humans and live upward of 300 years.
Racial Languages: Universal, Enochian

Racial Abilities

Healing Ichor (2 AP, touch): your celestial blood saves others from grievous wounds. Lose any amount of Health. One creature you touch regains the same amount.
Grace: when a Heavenly spell outside the Retribution subschool targets you, add your level ÷ 5 to its healing.

Description

Demi angels, the ethereal descendants of an angelic union between a celestial being and a devoted mortal follower, stand as a testament to the harmony that can exist between the divine and the earthly realms. Often manifesting as mesmerizing reflections of their mortal parent, demi angels embody a captivating blend of mortal beauty and ethereal grace. Their appearances are adorned with aspects that mirror the angel associated with their deity. For instance, a demi angel blessed by the deity Polith might possess fiery scarlet red hair, reminiscent of the deity's vibrant essence. Their golden eyes, akin to molten gold, would radiate a warmth that extends beyond their gaze. A gentle touch of their wings would evoke a comforting sensation, as if cradled by the warmth of a radiant sun. In contrast, a demi angel hailing from the lineage of the sea goddess Marissa might have skin composed of glimmering scales that shimmers with a soft bioluminescent blue hue. This luminescence, a reflection of the goddess's oceanic embrace, would create an enchanting aura around them.

Angel blood, also known as ichor, is a vital essence that flows through the veins of both angels and demi angels. This precious fluid holds remarkable healing properties, far surpassing the capabilities of mortal remedies. As thin as water, ichor possesses a vibrant golden glow that illuminates wounds like a beacon of hope. When applied to an injury, it initiates a miraculous regenerative process, mending shattered bones, closing gaping wounds, and restoring vitality with astonishing speed and efficiency. It is said that even the most severe ailments can be soothed by the touch of ichor, a testament to its divine origins.

Demi Fiend

Stats: Charisma +2, Strength +1
Size: Medium
Speed: Walk 5, Fly 5
Creature Type: Humanoid
Typical Alignment: Any Evil
Racial Resistance: Unholy 10
Lifespan: Demi fiends mature at the same rate as humans but live upward of 300 years.
Racial Languages: Universal, Bellsybabble

Racial Abilities

Cursed Bloodline: your corrupted blood lets you inflict grievous wounds on others and protects you from the spells of your cursed bloodline. You count as a Fiend for all effects, magical or not.

Destruction: whenever you deal damage to another creature, add half your level to the damage.

Description

Demi Fiends are the offspring of an unholy union between a mortal and a fiend. Originating from the underworld, Demi Fiends resemble their mortal parent but with notable abnormalities such as horns, a tail, or a forked tongue.

Demi Fiends are born with muted emotions, making it challenging for them to form lasting bonds and experience empathy towards others. This emotional detachment often leads them toward evil, as they find it easier to embrace immoral choices. Also known as Nephilim, Demi Fiends frequently face ostracism from society, leaving many of them indifferent.

Due to their isolation, many Demi Fiends procreate with fiends, resulting in subsequent generations of Demi Fiends who possess even more hellish traits and are more closely aligned with their demonic heritage. The further a Demi Fiend is removed from their mortal lineage, the more pronounced their fiendish characteristics become.

Demi Fiends often possess extraordinary abilities inherited from both their mortal and fiendish ancestry. They can command and control dark forces, manipulate shadows, and wield powerful magic. However, their inherent emotional detachment can make them unpredictable and dangerous, as they may lack the moral compass to use their powers responsibly.

Throughout history, Demi Fiends have been portrayed in various mythologies and cultures. In some tales, they are depicted as monstrous creatures capable of great evil, while in others, they are seen as tragic figures struggling to find their place in the world as heroes.

Deadspawn

Stats: Same as your previous race
Size: Medium
Speed: Same as your previous race
Creature Type: Undead
Typical Alignment: Usually Neutral
Racial Resistance: None
Lifespan: Deadspawn age as their previous race does but stop aging at their breaking point.
Racial Languages: 2 languages of choice

Racial Ability

Grave Affinity: when you gain Dying, you stay Dying for 5 + your level rounds and keep acting in initiative as normal.

Description

Deadspawn come about in two ways. Some are born undead. Others are created through reanimation.

Deadspawn are born with a unique set of characteristics that closely resemble the dead such as pale skin or cataract eyes. However, as they mature, they encounter a critical point known as the "breaking point." This phenomenon caused by great stress or a traumatic physical injury halts their physical development, perpetually locking them in their body as is.

After reaching their breaking point, the deadspawn cease to undergo natural changes in appearance, barring any magical intervention. Wounds and injuries sustained during their life remain forever etched upon their bodies, as their regenerative abilities are rendered ineffective. Some deadspawn may reach their breaking point while still exhibiting adolescent traits, effectively trapping them in an eternal state of juvenile appearance. Deadspawn that break before maturity usually go insane.

The breaking point is an intrinsic part of the deadspawn existence, occurring naturally as a consequence of their struggle against undeath. It may manifest at the moment of reanimation or gradually develop as the deadspawn body fights against the relentless forces of decay. This pivotal event marks a permanent shift in their physical state, leaving an indelible imprint on their eternal existence.

Dragonkin

Stats: Strength +2
Size: Medium
Speed: Walk 5
Creature Type: Humanoid
Typical Alignment: Any Evil
Racial Resistance: 10 to one of Fire, Cold, Earth, Poison, or Air
Lifespan: Dragonkin reach maturity at 50 and live upward of 500 years.
Racial Languages: Universal, Draconic

Racial Abilities

Breath of the Ancients (3 AP): exhale the breath of your ancestors in a 3 m cone. It deals 1d6 damage per 2 levels, minimum 1d6, of the type you chose for your Racial Resistance. Each target makes an Evasion save against your primary DC and takes half damage on a success. The breath recharges after 1d4 rounds.

Draconic Heritage: you gain +2 to your AC.

Description

In the realm of ancient legends, where the lines between reality and myth intertwine, a tale unfolds of an extraordinary bond between a magnificent dragon and a human woman. This union marked the beginning of a lineage that would forever change the course of history.

The dragon, a majestic creature of unparalleled power and grace, sought solace in the companionship of a human woman. Over time, their connection deepened, leading to the conception of a child who would become the progenitor of the Dragonkin. This offspring, a being of remarkable lineage, possessed the combined essence of dragon and human, embodying the pinnacle of the Dragonkin bloodline.

As the lineage was passed down through generations, the bloodline underwent gradual dilution, shaping the characteristics of subsequent Dragonkin. Over time, these descendants manifested a spectrum of physical traits, each revealing the extent of their dragon heritage.

Dragonkin of relatively pureblood retained a striking resemblance to bipedal dragons, with scales adorning their bodies, horns protruding from their heads, and piercing eyes that reflected their ancestral power. Their presence inspired awe and commanded respect among mortals, evoking tales of legendary battles and feats of valor.

As the bloodline diluted, a broader range of physical variations emerged within the Dragonkin population. Some Dragonkin bore subtle hints of their lineage, such as iridescent scales on their skin or a hint of reptilian features in their eyes. Others displayed more pronounced characteristics, including retractable claws, elongated fangs, or a forked tongue.

Regardless of their degree of blood purity, all Dragonkin shared a deep connection to their draconic roots. Their heightened senses, exceptional strength, and innate affinity for magic set them apart from ordinary humans. They possessed an unyielding spirit, a fierce determination, and a loyalty that knew no bounds.

Dragonkin still under the rule of dragons in Sol Inos are still at war trying to reclaim their homeland from the empire they view as thieves.

Demi Spirit

Stats: Wits +2, Charisma +1
Size: Medium
Speed: Walk 5
Creature Type: Humanoid
Typical Alignment: Any Chaotic
Racial Resistance: None
Lifespan: Demi spirits are erratic, reaching maturity between 15 and 30, and live upward of 200 years.
Racial Languages: Universal, Spirit

Racial Ability

Spiritual Form (2 AP): your body is not tied to one form. Take the form of any humanoid-like creature you have seen. The new form must share your size category. You keep your own speeds and features. A Demi Angel form gives you no flight and no Demi Angel traits.

Description

Demi Spirits, the intriguing offspring of a mortal and a spirit. Their appearances defy any rigid description, for they embody a captivating blend of their parents' distinct characteristics. A child born from the union of a fox spirit and a human may possess a singular, graceful fox tail that sways with every movement. In contrast, the child of an oni, and a human might resemble a demi-fiend, with striking features that hint at their dual heritage.

One of the defining traits of Demi Spirits is their innate struggle to conform to societal norms. Much like their non-mortal parent, they yearn for freedom and individuality. Society's expectations and constraints often feel stifling to a Demi Spirit, who longs to embrace their unique nature without judgment.

Growing up in a world designed for mortals, Demi Spirits face a myriad of challenges. They may possess extraordinary abilities or physical attributes that set them apart from their peers. This difference can lead to feelings of isolation and alienation, as they grapple with their dual identities. Moreover, the clash between societal expectations and their inherent freedom-loving spirit can create internal conflict and a sense of restlessness.

Demi Spirits often find solace in the company of others who understand their unique experiences. They may form tight-knit communities, seeking acceptance and a sense of belonging. Through these bonds, they learn to navigate the complexities of their dual heritage and discover ways to embrace their true selves while finding a place in the mortal world.

Kull

Stats: Intelligence +2
Size: Medium
Speed: Walk 5
Creature Type: Humanoid
Typical Alignment: Any Evil
Racial Resistance: Poison 10
Lifespan: Kull reach maturity at 10 and live upward of 50 years.
Racial Languages: Universal, Kull

Racial Ability

Venomous Fangs: the blood of the Nagas flows in your body. You have a natural weapon with the light and hidden properties, dealing 1d4 piercing damage. When your bite hits, the target makes a Resistance (Body) save, DC 10 + your level. On a failure, it is Poisoned, and the venom Paralyzes it for a number of hours equal to your level. The paralysis is a poison effect. The target repeats the save at the end of each of its turns and ends the effect on a success.

Description

Kull, a fascinating species, emerges from the union of a Naga mother and a humanoid father. These serpentine-like humanoids possess unique physical characteristics and behaviors. Kull are cold-blooded creatures, making them largely unaware of subtle temperature changes. Their skin, resembling that of a snake, is rough and scaly, offering them a protective covering. One of their most striking features is their ability to extend their jaws, revealing a toothless mouth except for two prominent fangs. These fangs serve as their primary weapons for capturing and consuming prey.

Kull are known for their voracious appetites, often devouring small animals such as cats and even small dogs whole. Their diet consists mainly of meat, and they are skilled hunters, using their keen senses and stealth to track and ambush their prey.

In Naga society, Kull generally occupy a lower status, just above slaves. They are rarely regarded as full citizens unless they are born into high-ranking families within the hierarchy. This distinction creates a social divide between Kull and pure-blooded Nagas, leading to prejudice and discrimination.

Gnome

Stats: Finesse +1, Intelligence +1
Size: Small
Speed: Walk 4
Creature Type: Humanoid
Typical Alignment: Any
Racial Resistance: None
Lifespan: Gnomes have the same lifespan as humans.
Racial Languages: Universal, Elvish

Racial Ability

Earthborn: the ancient magic of Cecylla's blessing runs in your bloodline. Choose 2 Minor Geomancy or Nature spells. Swap them every 5 levels. Cast each once per full rest without spending Mana.

Description

Gnomes are small humanoids similar to dwarves in height but weigh substantially less than any other race, usually no more than 40 kilograms. Gnomes are magically inclined living in small huts made of dirt and other natural substances. This connection to nature led to elves and gnomes naturally inhabiting similar terrain and sharing a culture.

Orc

Stats: Strength +2
Size: Large (+4 Strength)
Speed: Walk 5
Creature Type: Humanoid
Typical Alignment: Any
Racial Resistance: None
Lifespan: Orcs reach maturity at 15 and live upward of 80 years.
Racial Languages: Universal, Orcish

Racial Ability

Unyielding Might: the fury of the old warlords flows through your blood. A number of times per full rest equal to your level, reroll a check which applied a harmful condition or dealt damage to you. This works alongside other reroll abilities on the same check, but you use it only once per check.

Description

These towering humanoids stand approximately 3 meters tall, with their distinctive green skin and large, protruding teeth that jut out menacingly from their lower jaws. Orcs embody the epitome of brute strength and dominance, adhering to a harsh philosophy that only the strongest survive.

From the moment of birth, an Orc's fate is determined by the judgment of the elders. If deemed weak, the infant is mercilessly abandoned in the unforgiving woods, left to perish without mercy. This cruel practice ensures that only the most robust and resilient Orcs survive, perpetuating the relentless cycle of strength and domination.

Orcs are notorious for their violent and aggressive nature. They frequently launch raids and pillages the lands of Brenchisca, leaving no captives in their wake. Each clan of Orcs possesses a unique and distinctive warcry, which serves as a rallying call and a warning to their enemies. One such warcry became so renowned that it was adopted by the God King of Sol Inos, a testament to the fear and respect that the Orcish warcries instilled.

As an Orcish soldier reaches the threshold of adulthood, a significant ritual takes place. The father of the young warrior solemnly presents them with a shield, accompanied by a chilling declaration: "Come back with it or on it!" This phrase carries immense weight, symbolizing the expectation that the soldier will return either victorious or fallen in battle, their shield serving as a testament to their courage and strength.

The life of an Orc is defined by unwavering loyalty to their clan, unyielding ferocity in battle, and a profound respect for the strength of the individual. Their society is built upon a foundation of might, where only the fittest survive, and weakness is mercilessly cast aside. Orcs are a force to be reckoned with, inspiring both awe and fear in the hearts of those who cross their path.

Half Elf

Stats: Finesse +1, +1 to a stat of choice
Size: Medium
Speed: Walk 5
Creature Type: Humanoid
Typical Alignment: Any
Racial Resistance: None
Lifespan: Half elves mature at the same rate as humans and live upward of 200 years.
Racial Languages: Universal, Elvish

Racial Ability

Best of Both: you are a rare child of an elf and a human. You gain extra skill ranks equal to half your level, and you ignore movement penalties from difficult terrain in natural areas.

Description

Half elves, with their striking features, are a unique blend of elven and human traits. Their ears, while not as elongated as those of their elven kin, are still pointed and elegant. They have the lithe frame of elves and the sturdier bones of humans, and their senses are heightened, much like those of elves.

The creation of half elves requires the union between an elf and a human, as their reproductive systems hold a certain compatibility not found in other species. However, this union is not without risks. A nefarious disease known as "Root Rot" can afflict the elven parent after fornication. This disease starts as a minor infection in the nether regions but quickly spreads, causing tissue decay and eventually leading to death if left untreated. The only hope for survival lies in timely magical intervention.

The rarity of half elves with elven mothers is a testament to the severity of Root Rot. Only the wealthy can afford the costly magical treatment required to combat the disease. As a result, elves and humans romantically involved, despite their longing for children, are forced to face the harsh reality of potential tragedy.

Kendarkai

Stats: Wits +1
Size: Medium
Speed: Walk 5
Creature Type: Magical Beast
Typical Alignment: Any
Racial Resistance: Magic 10
Lifespan: Kendarkai reach maturity at 1 year old and live upward of 10 years.
Racial Languages: Universal, Kendar

Racial Ability

Perfection: as a Kendarkai who has left the swarm, you keep your racial adaptations. Choose two different adaptations below. No adaptation twice.

  • Winged: you gain a fly speed of 5.
  • Thick Carapace: you gain +2 to your AC.
  • Antennae: you gain +2 to Sense Environment checks.
  • Hardened Exoskeleton: attackers have −10 Critical Chance against you.

Description

In the depths of the labyrinthine caverns beneath an ancient forest, the Kendarkai dwell, a race of insectoid creatures with adaptations honed by their roles within the hive. The hive is ruled by the colossal and venerable broodmother.

The Kendar, still bound to the hivemind, toil tirelessly, their actions driven by the collective consciousness. They emit pheromones that serve as their identity within the hive, identifying them as part of the group. But among the Kendar, there are those who break free from the hivemind's control. These are the Kendarkai, who have severed their bonds with the hive, no longer emitting the pheromones necessary for recognition.

The separation from the hive can occur in two ways. Some are exiled, their pheromone sacks severed, and they are banished from the hive, condemned to a solitary existence outside the collective. Others, in an act of defiance, sever their own pheromone sacks, choosing to leave the hive and forge their own path.

The Kendar, bound by the hivemind, hold no relations with other races, save for a deep and abiding hatred for the Beastmen. The Beastmen, with their savage nature and predatory instincts, represent a threat to the hive, and the Kendar view them as enemies to be eradicated.

Half Orc

Stats: Strength +1, +1 to a stat of choice
Size: Medium
Speed: Walk 5
Creature Type: Humanoid
Typical Alignment: Any
Racial Resistance: None
Lifespan: Half orcs share the lifespan of orcs.
Racial Languages: Universal, 1 language of choice

Racial Ability

Bloodlust: your ancestry fuels a primal lust for blood in brutal strikes of glory. When you roll for a Critical or Decisive Strike, your Critical Chance increases by 10.

Description

Half-orcs are a unique breed born from the rare union of an orc and a non-orc humanoid. They share the imposing stature of their orc parent, often towering over other humanoids. Their appearance is a blend of both races, with features that may lean more towards either side. Some half-orcs may inherit the prominent brow and tusks of their orcish parent, while others may have a more human-like appearance.

Half-orcs are known for their fierce temperament, inheriting the ferocity of their orcish blood. This can often come as a surprise to their non-orc parent, who may not be prepared for such a spirited child. Half-orcs raised in non-orcish communities may struggle to fit in, as their natural inclinations can lead to misunderstandings and conflicts. They may find themselves drawn to physical activities, combat, and situations that test their strength and courage.

Growing up in a world that often holds prejudices against orcs, half-orcs face unique challenges. They may encounter discrimination and mistrust from both human and orcish societies, leading to a sense of isolation and a desire to prove themselves. This can result in half-orcs getting into trouble, as they may resort to violence or risky behavior in an attempt to assert their worth.

Despite the hardships they face, half-orcs are resilient and resourceful individuals. They possess a deep sense of loyalty and protectiveness towards those they care about. They may form close bonds with others who understand their dual nature, finding acceptance and a sense of belonging in unexpected places.

In some cultures, half-orcs are seen as fearsome warriors and respected for their strength and courage. They may find a place in mercenary companies, adventuring parties, or even as protectors of their communities. Others, however, may choose a more solitary path, seeking to find their own place in a world that often seems hostile to their existence.

Qeptol

Stats: Fortitude +2, Strength +1
Size: Medium
Speed: Walk 5
Creature Type: Plant
Typical Alignment: Any
Racial Resistance: Earth 10
Lifespan: Qeptol are created mature and live upward of 300 years.
Racial Languages: Universal

Racial Ability

Cecylla's Blessing: you gain +5 to Resistance (Body) saves. You need no food or sleep, but you must spend at least 6 hours in sunlight each day.

Description

Once ordinary plants, Qeptol were imbued with consciousness through the touch of ancient magic. In their humanoid form, they embody the essence of the plant from which they originated, showcasing skin composed of bark or leaves.

At the core of every Qeptol lies a vital organ known as the core, which functions as their heart. Removal of this core results in the irreversible demise of the Qeptol. The significance of this heart extends beyond its biological purpose. It possesses potent properties that make it highly coveted in the realm of potions and herbal remedies.

Lesser Qeptol are often bred by elves, who recognize the immense value of their cores in creating powerful potions. These potions are renowned for their healing and restorative abilities, granting unparalleled benefits to those who consume them.

However, not all Qeptol are harvested, as they form a symbiotic relationship with the Grove Keepers, protectors of the natural realm. The Grove Keepers recognize the Qeptol's invaluable contributions and extend their protection, ensuring their well-being and preservation.

Beastman

Stats: Finesse or Strength +2
Size: Medium
Speed: Walk 6
Creature Type: Beast
Typical Alignment: Any
Racial Resistance: None
Lifespan: Beastmen mature at 20 and live upward of 60 years.
Racial Languages: Universal, Cryptic

Racial Ability

The Hunt: once per full rest, channel The Hunt for 1 minute. You gain a bonus to attack rolls and Resistance (Body) saves equal to your level, +5 to tracking creatures, and +2 to your Speed. When it ends, you gain Exhausted until you complete a partial rest.

Description

Beastmen, majestic creatures that walk upright, embody the feral strength of various great beasts, such as lions, jaguars, wolves, bears, and more. These formidable beings possess extraordinary tracking and hunting abilities. They can temporarily surpass the physical limitations of a mortal creature, granting them bursts of incredible speed, agility, and strength. However, this exertion takes a toll on the beastman, leading to fatigue, so they reserve it for the most critical situations.

Beastmen society revolves around the pursuit of slaying creatures of immense power. Their tribes are highly skilled mercenaries, often contracted to fell formidable magical beasts when kingdoms or settlements face threats they cannot handle alone. Beastmen take pride in their ability to conquer such dangerous creatures, viewing it as a testament to their strength and prowess. Tribes compete to secure the most challenging contracts, as it brings not only wealth but also prestige and recognition.

Within beastmen society, there is a strong sense of hierarchy. The alpha, usually the strongest and most experienced beastman, leads the tribe. The alpha's role is to make critical decisions, ensure the tribe's survival, and maintain order. Shamans, attuned to the spirits of nature and skilled in ancient rituals, hold a respected position. They guide the tribe spiritually, providing wisdom and guidance through visions and prophecies.

Beastmen's relationship with nature is profound. They view themselves as an integral part of the natural world, acknowledging the interconnectedness of all living things. Beastmen revere the spirits of the land and believe in maintaining a balance with the environment. This respect for nature influences their hunting practices, where they take only what they need and honor the creatures they slay.

The unique nature of the beastmen makes them both revered and feared by other races. Their raw power, tracking abilities, and prowess in slaying magical beasts inspire awe and caution. Beastmen have formed alliances with certain factions, valuing strength and mutual respect. However, their unpredictable nature and occasional territorial disputes can lead to conflicts with other races.

Goblin

Stats: Finesse +1
Size: Small
Speed: Walk 4
Creature Type: Humanoid
Typical Alignment: Any
Racial Resistance: None
Lifespan: Goblins reach maturity at 10 and live upward of 30 years.
Racial Languages: Universal, Orcish

Racial Abilities

Nightstalker: you are nimble. You gain +2 to Evasion saves and 10 m Dark Sight.
Ambush Expert: Sneak and Sleight of Hand cost you 1 AP less. You gain +5 to all checks involving traps.

Description

Goblins, with their gray skin and piercing red or orange eyes, lurk in the underbelly of the world. Their fearsome reputation precedes them, leading travelers to dread encountering these cunning humanoids in the darkness of the night.

Goblins often make their abodes in intricate cave systems, driving out miners and causing havoc in the underground world. Their natural habitat provides them with the perfect terrain for their ambush attacks and skillfully placed traps. With their expert timing, they strike at unsuspecting travelers on the road, taking hostages back to their dark caves.

The goblins' reputation for cruelty is well-known. Hostages captured by goblins are subjected to inhumane conditions, deprived of food and water, and often returned in a state of utter despair. Their gaunt and dehydrated figures bear witness to the horrors they have endured.

Adventurers are frequently sought after to exterminate goblin tribes, but seasoned adventurers often decline such quests. Their past experiences in goblin-infested caves have left them with unpleasant memories. The claustrophobic caves, the constant threat of traps, and the sheer number of goblins can be overwhelming. As a result, these quests are often undertaken by inexperienced adventurers, who may not be fully aware of the dangers they face.

However, there are rare cases of goblins who have left their tribes and seek redemption. These goblins, often with a history of being outcasts or having experienced kindness from humans, can become valuable allies on adventures. Their intimate knowledge of goblin tactics and traps proves invaluable, and they can help navigate the perilous cave systems with greater ease.

Goblins, despite their fearsome reputation, are complex creatures with a rich history and culture. Some goblins have managed to break free from their savage ways and find a place in the wider world, proving that even the most maligned creatures can find a path to redemption.

Predator Slime

Stats: Fortitude +2
Size: Medium
Speed: Walk 5
Creature Type: Slime
Typical Alignment: Any
Racial Resistance: None
Lifespan: Predator slimes reach maturity in a few months and have no maximum lifespan.
Racial Languages: Universal, 1 language of choice

Racial Abilities

Predatory Instincts: your gelatinous, amorphous body fits through any gap 1 cm or larger. You take the shape of any humanoid you have consumed, or your original humanoid form.

Specialized Pseudopod: you have a natural weapon dealing 1d6 + Strength or Finesse modifier Piercing, Slashing, or Bludgeoning damage, chosen when you form it. At 1st level and every 5 levels after, add one property: deadly, reach, ranged (30 m), defensive, cleave, or enchanted. Absorb one magic melee weapon at a time to give the pseudopod its special property.

Description

Predator slimes are a dangerous and elusive slime species known for their ability to mimic humanoids. With their humanoid appearance, they can seamlessly blend into human communities, making them incredibly difficult to detect. These slimes possess a cold, clammy touch, and their hardness can be adjusted at will, allowing them to resist sharp cuts and stabs while softening the impact of blunt force.

The predator slime's remarkable ability to change its hardness also grants it the capability to create a weapon similar to steel, seamlessly integrated into its body. This weapon can be shaped into sharp blades, claws, or even blunt instruments, depending on the predator slime's needs and preferences.

To survive and thrive, predator slimes adopt a stealthy and cunning hunting strategy. They patiently observe and integrate themselves into human communities, earning the trust of unsuspecting residents. Once they have gained familiarity with their surroundings, they strike swiftly and lethally, killing and consuming their victims without leaving a trace.

Predator slimes possess an insatiable hunger and are known to devour entire communities before moving on to the next target. Their ability to mimic humanoids allows them to infiltrate and manipulate communities, creating an atmosphere of fear and paranoia.

Predator slimes, despite their predatory nature, harbor an unusual aspiration to assimilate into the society they hunt. If a city is large enough to sustain them, they may spend their entire lives there, blending in as ordinary people. Remarkably, some predator slimes even form families with partners and have children. Their ability to perfectly mimic a person results in the child being a full member of their partner's race.

Aquarii

Stats: Strength +2
Size: Medium
Speed: Walk 5, Swim 5
Creature Type: Humanoid
Typical Alignment: Any
Racial Resistance: Cold 10
Lifespan: Aquarii mature at 25 and live up to 100 years.
Racial Languages: Universal, Aquarian

Racial Abilities

Depthlurker: you have lungs and gills, so you breathe underwater, and you are immune to the effects of water pressure at extreme depths.

Echolocation (3 AP): send low sound pulses, imperceptible to most creatures, through a 10 m radius. You learn which objects and creatures there are physical, which exposes illusions with no physical form. Gain +5 on Sense Environment checks until your next turn. Pulses stop at solid barriers.

Description

Deep within the fathomless abyss of the sea, where sunlight fades into a distant memory, dwell the Aquarii, a civilization of aquatic beings. Created eons ago by the enigmatic Marissa, they were designed to hold her in reverence and uphold her teachings.

Aquarii are predominantly fearful of land dwellers, perceiving them as a threat to their underwater domain. This fear stems from centuries of exploitation and encroachment by humans, who engage in fishing and other activities that disrupt the delicate balance of the deep sea. The Aquarii view these actions as a form of thievery, pilfering the treasures of the depths.

Their unwavering devotion to Marissa lies at the core of their culture. She is the source of all wisdom and guidance, and her teachings permeate every aspect of Aquarii society. Staying in the water, except in exceptional circumstances, is a cardinal tenet of their beliefs. Violating this sacred edict results in swift expulsion from the depths, a fate worse than death for an Aquari.

The Aquarii reside in the magnificent city of Coraloach, a sprawling metropolis carved into the living coral reefs. While their society is generally harmonious and peaceful, it is ruled with an iron fist by Marissa, who maintains her position as a benevolent but tyrannical leader. In the absence of any other viable alternatives, the Aquarii acquiesce to her rule, knowing that her guidance is essential for their survival.

Despite their fear of land dwellers, the Aquarii are innately kind and compassionate creatures. They possess a deep understanding of the interconnectedness of all living things and strive to maintain a harmonious balance with the marine ecosystem. Their knowledge of the ocean's secrets is vast, and they possess an uncanny ability to communicate with aquatic life, forming deep bonds with the creatures of the deep.

As the tides ebb and flow, so too does the existence of the Aquarii, forever bound to the depths and the enigmatic Marissa who created them. Their tale is one of reverence, resilience, and a deep connection to the mysteries of the underwater world.

Moon Elf

Stats: Finesse +2
Size: Medium
Speed: Walk 5
Creature Type: Humanoid
Typical Alignment: Any
Racial Resistance: Fire 10
Lifespan: Moon elves share the lifespan of elves.
Racial Languages: Universal, 1 language of choice

Racial Abilities

Ashborn: choose 2 Minor Pyromancy spells. Cast each once per full rest without spending Mana.
Shared Memories: Moon Elves have the Elf's Shared Memories ability.

Description

Moon elves, known for their ethereal beauty, share striking similarities with their elven counterparts in terms of physical features. However, one distinct characteristic sets them apart: the captivating shape of their pupils, resembling a crescent moon. This unique trait adds an enchanting allure to their gaze, making them stand out among other elves.

The skin tones of pure moon elves are equally captivating, ranging from the deep, obsidian black of volcanic ash to the pale shade of white ash appearing as the contrast between the moon and the night sky.

All moon elves trace their origins to Gloomwood, a mystical island bathed in eternal night under the watchful eye of the goddess Kelria. This island serves as their ancestral home, shrouded in dense forests illuminated by the soft glow of a large, ever-present full moon. The moon's gentle light casts an otherworldly ambiance upon Gloomwood, creating an atmosphere of mystery and enchantment.

In ancient times, the original moon elves were said to be crafted from volcanic ash, granting them an extraordinary resistance to extreme temperatures. This unique origin allowed them to establish their original cities within the unforgiving interiors of volcanoes, where they thrived amid the fiery heat. The moon elves' connection to volcanic ash also imbued them with a deep understanding of the earth's elemental forces, shaping their culture and traditions.

Throughout history, the moon elves have developed a profound connection with the natural world. They hold a special reverence for the moon, seeing it as a source of spiritual guidance and enlightenment. Their rituals and ceremonies are often centered around the lunar cycles, and they draw upon the moon's energy to enhance their magical abilities.

In terms of personality, moon elves tend to be introspective and reserved, valuing tranquility and harmony. They possess a strong sense of community and are fiercely loyal to their kin. However, they can also be fiercely protective of their traditions and may be wary of outsiders.
