Hero's Rally

Master
Casting Time: 1 Minute
Requirements: Conviction
Type: Enhancement
Range: 30 meters
Duration: 1 Hour

Concentration: No

The martialist rallies their allies in a 1 minute speech, inspiring them to fight with renewed vigor and determination. All allies within 30 meters gain a +2 boost to all stats for the duration and become inspired. If the player gives a motivational speech OOC the bonus is increased to +4 or may choose to remove one of the following conditions from all allies effected by the rally. Fearful, Hopeless, Charmed, and Nauseated.
