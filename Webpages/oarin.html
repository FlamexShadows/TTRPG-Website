Information

Oarin the god of war is famed as literally being his sister Kelria's shadow. Oarin is known to be the most violent and temperamental of the gods. His will is for war and he craves chaos, blood and the glory it brings to his fabled warriors. Those who follow Oarin believe that war is a glorious trial and accept all fights. It is a strict code passed by Oarin to only attack those who are capable of defending themselves or be seen as weak by the order for being incapable of fighting those who can defend themselves. Death in battle is the only way to not have your soul dammed to the underworld

Appearance

Oarin is a tall man with a perfectly symmetrical face with matching chest. He has dark olive skin with bright blue eyes. He wears full plate armor and has a flaming long sword.
