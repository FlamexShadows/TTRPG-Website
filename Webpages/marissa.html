Information:

Marissa is a revered deity among the Aquarii, the dominant race of underwater dwellers. Her worship is characterized by opulence and grandeur, reflecting her own divine nature and expectations. The temples dedicated to Marissa are architectural marvels, adorned with the finest pearls, gems, and precious metals. These underwater sanctuaries are not only places of worship but also symbols of wealth and power. Marissa's temples are masterpieces of aquatic architecture, constructed with materials that shimmer and shine beneath the ocean waves. The interior of each temple is lined with an array of treasures, including pearls of unparalleled luster, radiant gemstones, and precious metals. These treasures are offerings from her devoted followers, symbolizing their reverence and loyalty. At the heart of each temple stands a statue of Marissa, crafted to her exacting specifications. These statues are made from solid gold, with eyes fashioned from the finest pearls and hair intricately carved from vibrant rubies. Any deviation from this standard is met with Marissa's wrath, manifesting in destructive water riptides that obliterate any subpar depiction. She demands only the best, reflecting her own divine majesty and disdain for anything less than perfect.

Marissa is known to bestow considerable powers upon her clerics and paladins, granting them dominion over the sea and storms. These powers are coveted, but Marissa's favor is notoriously capricious. Her followers must constantly perform elaborate rituals and offer substantial tributes to maintain her blessings. The rituals are often elaborate, involving intricate dances, chants, and the offering of valuable items. Those who dare to question Marissa's authority or fail to show proper devotion face severe punishment. Dissidents are often exiled to the surface world, where they are left to suffer and perish without the protection of the sea. In more extreme cases, Marissa's enforcers will rake the gills of her enemies, condemning them to a slow and agonizing death by suffocation.

Appearance:

Marissa presents herself as an ethereal aquatic humanoid, exuding an eerie yet captivating presence. Her eyes are hauntingly empty, pure white voids that seem to pierce through the soul. Flowing around her face is a mane of scarlet red hair that contrasts strikingly with her blue and gray mottled skin. Her body is covered in scales that resemble serrated teeth, creating an unsettling texture that seems designed to inflict harm. Indeed, those who dare to touch her often find themselves bleeding from the mere contact with her scales. Her physique transitions into a form reminiscent of an eel, yet with a menacing twist that evokes the fearsome appearance of a shark. Her elongated lower body undulates with a sinuous grace, enhancing her otherworldly allure. Marissa's mouth is a cavern of terror, lined with countless rows of razor-sharp teeth that hint at her predatory nature. Adorning her form are various fins – pectoral and dorsal – which lend her an even more distinctive and fearsome silhouette.
