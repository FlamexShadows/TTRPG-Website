Shield of Light


Range: 5 Meters
AP Cost: 3
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Heavenly
Subtype: Protection
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: 10 Minutes
Concentration: Yes

The caster chooses up to 5 creatures bathing them in the light of heaven. Each creature may add 1d4 to any Resistance (Body), Resistance (Mind) or Evasion save. In addition to this the creature becomes immune to the Fearful or Hopeless conditions
