Find Animals


AP Cost: 4
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Nature
Subtype: Primal
Range: Self
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: 1 Minute

The caster selects one of the following effects as they emit a wave of magic that affects all animals in the area.

Sign of Life
The caster commands the lesser souls of the animals in the wild to reveal their presence. All beasts within 100 meters of the caster make a loud sound or perform another action that reveals its presence within the area.

Nature's Sight
The caster becomes aware of all beasts within 10 meters of themselves.

Locate Beast
The caster names a specific beast. If the animal is within 1 kilometer of the caster they learn the location of the closest one.
