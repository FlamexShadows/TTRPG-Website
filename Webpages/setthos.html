Setthos

Power: Demigod

Titles:
Champion of Chance
Serendipity Savior

Tenants: Let Luck Guide the way

Portfolios: Luck

Follower Alignment: Any

Alignment: Chaotic Neutral

Information

Setthos, the god of games and luck, is a revered deity, known to be the ultimate arbiter when it comes to matters of chance. His divine presence is felt whenever people gather to test their luck, whether it be at a simple game of cards or the grandest casino in all of Nova Strados. Setthos is the patron of those who seek to improve their fortunes. The poor and the downtrodden often pray to him, asking for his blessing before venturing into games of chance. Those who seek his favor are often rewarded, their fortunes shifting in their favor, and their hopes becoming reality. The casino in Nova Strados is a testament to Setthos' power. The lavish establishment is a place where fortunes are made and lost, and where the lives of those who enter are forever changed. Here, Setthos presides over the games, officiating every roll of the dice, every spin of the wheel, and every card dealt. He is the final authority on all matters of chance, and his word is law.

Appearance

Setthos appears as a humanoid wearing a mask holding a deck of cards . On his right hand is a glove on which has the joker card and on his left hand shows an ace. His tall and lanky figure is similar to an elf and he is always seen in a suit with a large wheel depicting roulette wheel.
