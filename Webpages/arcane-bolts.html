Arcane Bolts


Range: 15 Meters
AP Cost: 4
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Arcanomancy
Subtype: Destruction
Requirements: Sigils
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster creates 3 beams of condensed mana sending the projectiles to any creatures in range. The bolts automatically hit dealing 1d4+1 magic damage.

When upcast the caster doubles the range and adds an additional 2 beams for each tier upcast.
