Transfer Vitality


Range: 10 Meters
AP Cost: 1
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Necromancy
Subtype: Tether
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster transfers vitality from an undead under their control within range to heal a creature. The caster chooses a creature within range sacrificing health from the undead and healing the target of the spell. Any undead targeted by this spell is instantly killed sending half of its current health to the target.
