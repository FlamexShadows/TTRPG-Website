Narolith's Touch


Range: Touch
AP Cost: 6
Spell Rank: Grand
Class: Necromancy
Subtype: Death
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster targets a creature that is not undead or an outsider attempting to kill it. The target must succeed a Resistance (Body) save. If the creature fails it is instantly slain. On a successful save the creature takes 15d6 Necrotic damage. Creatures with physical armor automatically succssed the save
