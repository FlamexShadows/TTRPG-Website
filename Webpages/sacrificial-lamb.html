Sacrificial Lamb

Advanced
AP: 6
Requirements: Coup De Grace
Type: Attack
Range: Touch
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The martialist attempts to sacrifice an intelligent creature to a dark power in order to achieve greatness. This functions identically to Coup De Grace but if the target dies as a result of this maneuver the martialist preforms its next Martial Maneuver before a full rest without expending Stamina.
