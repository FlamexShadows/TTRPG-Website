Ice Wall


Range: 15
AP Cost: 4
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Hydromancy
Subtype: Ice
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: No

A solid wall of ice erupts at a point of choosing from the caster within range. The wall can reach up to 10 meters long and 5 meters high. If a creature is caught in the formation of the ice it must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or take 5d6 cold damage and become frozen inside of the wall. On a successful save the creature takes half as much damage and is not freezing. The wall has 30 health, is immune to cold, poison, and psychic damage and vulnerable to fire damage. Any saves the wall makes uses the spell casting modifier of the caster.
