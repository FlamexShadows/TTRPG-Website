Information

Long ago, an ancient follower of Lemara betrayed her first tenant by creating an artistic statue of the goddess based on her descriptions in the scriptures. Lemara, aware of his obsession with seeing her, chose mercy over banishment to the void. Instead of casting him out of creation, she placed him into an endless slumber, locking him away in a tomb. In his perpetual sleep, his subconscious created a manifestation of its own world. From this dream, Osmos was born and began to shape it. All dreams fell under Osmos's control, and as this phenomenon spread, the dream world evolved into an alternate plane under his dominion. Over time, many became entranced by this dream world and began to worship it, choosing to sleep as long as possible to remain within its confines. These devotees became known as Sleepwalkers. Often followed by the poor, Osmos offers refuge from the harsh realities of the waking world within his dream realm. Although he is unable to leave this plane due to his curse, he allows others to enter private bubbles of his dream world during their sleep, providing them with solace and escape from their troubles.

Appearance

In the realm of dreams, Osmos is composed of thousands of ethereal worms that form the constructs within one's dreams. These entities are known to his worshippers as Dream Larvae. Those who garner Osmos's favor are visited by the Dream Larvae, which appear as a creature of their greatest interest for those in good standing with him, and as their worst nightmare for those who have fallen out of his favor. Gazing upon a Dream Larva, which presents an image uniquely tailored to each person, can inspire or drive them to despair. Those fortunate enough to survive a terrifying encounter with a Dream Larva often go mad, their fear of sleep leading to eventual death.
