Rewind Creature


Range: 20 Meters
AP Cost: 6
Spell Rank: Grand
Class: Chronomancy
Subtype: Time
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster rewinds time on a creature. Any creature that has spent mana, stamina or taken damage in its last turn has it reset to before they took their turn. A creature with any effects or spells on it also has them reset and continues to have them on their turn. If the creature is unwilling to receive this spell it must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save. Casting Rewind Time costs an additional 25 Mana.
