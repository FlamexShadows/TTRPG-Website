Cloud Scribe


Range: Sight
AP Cost: 2
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Nature
Subtype: Nurture
Requirements: Sigils
Duration: 1 Hour
Concentration: No

The caster morphs the clouds allowing for them to become words or art. The caster may write a message in the clouds or create a sign or sigil in them that can be seen by any creature able to view them.
