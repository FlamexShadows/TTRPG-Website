Deflect

Advanced
Casting Time: Reaction
Requirements: Shield
Type: Attack
Range: 1 Meter
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The martialist attempts to deflect an attack directed at a creature within 1 meter of themselves to a nearby creature or empty space adjacent to them. If the attack roll is higher than the martialist's maneuver attack the martialist fails to deflect the attack. If the martialist's maneuver attack roll is higher than the attack roll the martialist may choose a creature or empty space within range of the original attack instead. For example the original target was hit targeted by an attack from a ray that has 15 meters and the target creature of the ray was 10 meters from the caster, the martialist can pick a new creature within 5 meters.
