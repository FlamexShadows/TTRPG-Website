Reflexive Riposte

Basic
Casting Time: Reaction
Requirements: -
Type: Attack
Range: Self
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The martialist makes a Evasion save against the opponent's attack roll. If successful, they dodge the attack and attack dealing an extra 1d6 damage. Additionally, the martialist has a +2 bonus on their next attack roll against this opponent until the end of their next turn.
