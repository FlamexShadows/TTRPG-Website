Creature Types

Basic Rules, updated November 3, 2026

Cryptid

Cryptids are always known to the local people through stories passed down from generation to generation. Information about Cryptids can be gathered from locals in the area. A common way adventurers determine if creatures are cryptids is simply asking local legends and seeing if the creature they are hunting fits the description.

A character from the cryptid's region rolls Knowledge (History), DC 10 + TR. Divide the result by the DC and round down. The character learns one fact per whole multiple: what it hunts, why it exists, or how to stop it.

Example: Kelly rolls 25 against DC 12. 25 ÷ 12 = 2, so Kelly learns 2 facts.

Angel

Angels are the servants of the deities of the seven heavens. Angels always manifest as extensions of their deities' powers. Angels of Lemara have spectral wings made of pure mana, while an angel of Marissa has scaled wings and gills along the sides of its ribs. Angels always serve to further their deities' goals and uphold the tenets they set. Angels who become disobedient fall, severing their grace and destroying their connection to the heavens. Fallen angels appear as dark, pale, and hollow aspects of their former selves. Fallen angels are amongst the most evil in the cosmos and often strive for power. People know of angels, yet few have seen one. Demi angels are the main living proof.

Fiend

The origins of fiends are heavily debated, as the stories of their creation or subjugation appear in multiple stories about the gods. Fiends serve the deities of the underworld. They are organized into several ranks, ranging from demons to half fiends. Fiends are portrayed as evil, though stories of heroes amongst them saving mortals from torment are prominent.

Fiends are wary of holy symbols. Holy symbols affect weaker fiends more. Any time a holy symbol is presented to an exposed fiend, the fiend must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save, DC 10 + TR, or become Fearful of the symbol.

Fiends are known for their ability to corrupt and possess mortals. They seek to spread corruption amongst them, damning souls to the underworld to raise their ranks.

Beast

Beasts are creatures of the wild. They hold little to no intelligence and live everywhere in the world. They include all animals and insects except those created or modified by magic. Many people bond with beasts. Most beasts feel complex emotions such as love or hatred, and some species live alongside others.

Magical Beast

Magical beasts are like their non-magical counterparts, made more adept at certain skills or tasks such as hunting or war. The deities made the first magical beasts, then gave the magic to mortals. Mortals now create and modify their own for hunting and war. Magical beasts often star in legends of great triumph, such as a hero slaying the great sea serpent or hunting an animal terrorizing a kingdom.

Elemental

Elementals represent the primal forces of nature such as air, earth, fire, etc. They reside in the planes of substance and are the living embodiment of the elements they represent. Elementals vary in power drastically, a famous example of them being the phoenix compared to their base fire embers. Elementals hold no strong emotions resulting in them not swaying strongly towards good or evil.

Golem

Golems are almost identical to Elementals but are created by a master using magic to serve them. They hold just enough intelligence to follow the orders of their masters but are considered not sentient. Powered by a core they animate themselves and are highly resistant to physical attacks and certain elements but are always weak to another, usually the opposite or natural counterpart of the material they are constructed from.

Humanoid

Humanoids are bipeds that are intelligent. The most prevalent of which being humans, elves, and dwarves. Mostly a term for any human-like creature.

Plant

Plants are the flora of the world. Most are non-sentient and immobile. A few plants evolve from this basic form, becoming close to humanoids or beasts. They gain intelligence and, like humanoids, are not strongly aligned with any alignment. Sentient plants often reside in elven society, as they share a close bond and are seen as equals.

Spirit

Spirits are a mystical force residing in a realm just below the border world. Spirits are viewed mostly negatively, with a reputation as tricksters and bringers of ill omen. Spirits are commonly mistaken for Cryptids or Magical Beasts due to the similarity between them. Spirits include races such as faeries and yokai. A vast majority of them are renowned for mimicking humans and beasts almost perfectly. Most spirits descend from Cecylla and are said to be her first angels, dismissed from the heavens and sent to the spirit world. Because Cecylla created them, many spirits worship her and guard the nature around them.

Slime

Slimes are composed of a soft gelatinous semi fluid that is highly acidic or basic resulting in objects and creatures that come into contact with them to disintegrate. Vulnerable to water they rapidly absorb it into their body causing them to explode and die. Slime reproduction is usually a result of them splitting in two. Slime grow in size as they consume and most are blind beyond certain radiuses.

Undead

Undead are creatures raised from the dead incomplete, leaving a hollow semblance of life, or transformed through a curse, disease, or other magic. The force raising an undead creature stays bound to its soul until the creature is destroyed. Undead are feared. The most famous of them are the Queen of Undeath and her bride, the Witch King, who wished to usher in an era of undeath by raising all to undead. Undead are animated at their state of decomposition, making some nothing but skeletons while others, such as zombies, keep most or all of their flesh.

Outsider

Outsiders are native to no plane and hail from the Empty. Outsiders are a primordial evil and incarnations of entropy, always searching for ways into one of the worlds to turn it into an extension of the Empty. They influence creatures inside the planes to create portals for them to enter. Every power in the cosmos fights outsiders at any cost. They exude a presence of nondescript malice, causing even the worst of enemies, such as fallen angels and angels, to form temporary alliances to rid the world of them.

Draconid

Draconids look like reptiles but form their own type, separate from magical beasts. At the top of the draconids are true dragons, known for their powerful environmental effects and their ability to breathe a force causing massive damage to anything it touches. Most draconids are proud creatures who look down upon any creature they believe weaker than themselves. Among the draconids are the Lung. Lung are dragons of Cecylla's creation representing a force of nature. Her followers revere them, and many places Lung visit in legend have shrines dedicated to them for protection against spirits.
