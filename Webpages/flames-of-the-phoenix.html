Flames of the Phoenix


Range: Self
AP Cost: 6
Spell Rank: Grand
Class: Pyromancy
Subtype: Flame
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: No

When the spell is activated, the caster's body is engulfed in brilliant flames that can incinerate anything they touch. The caster gains immunity to fire and heals 2d6 health whenever subject to fire damage while the spell is active. During the spell's duration the caster can unleash a ray of flame at one creature within 15 meters. The targeted creature must succeed on an Evasion save or take 15d10 fire damage. The flames then leap to up to 7 creatures within 5 meters of the original target extending the effect to them. A creature struck by a ray begins burning for 10 minutes. While burning the creature takes 2d6 fire damage at the beginning of each of its turns. These magic flames are so potent they cannot be extinguished by no means shorter than Grand Hydromancy magic.
