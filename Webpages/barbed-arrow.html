Barbed Ammunition

Tier: Basic
Action Cost: 3 AP
Requirements: Ranged Weapon
Type: Ranged Attack
Range: Weapon
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No
The martialist makes an attack roll. On a hit the arrow gets stuck in the target causing it to bleed until healed or a successful Knowledge (Medical) check. A creature with regeneration or that doesnt have blood such as a skeleton is immune to this effect. At the start of each of their turns until healed the creature takes 2d6 true damage. This damage can be stacked up to a maximum of 10d6.
