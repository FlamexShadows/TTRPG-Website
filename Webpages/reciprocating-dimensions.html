Reciprocating Dimensions


Range: Self
AP Cost: 2
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Chronomancy
Subtype: Dimension
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: No

The caster swaps between the border world and back the mortal plane. At the start of the caster's turns they roll a percentile die and on rolling their primary statisic or lower they phase to the border world or back to the mortal plane. At the end of the spell's duration the caster remains on whichever plane they are currently on.
