Complete Spells Organized by School and Rank

Spells turn a creature's source into mana and shape it into effects. The schools of magic are Arcanomancy, Heavenly, Otherworldly, Nature, Chronomancy, Demonology, Enchantment, Necromancy, Transmutation, Hydromancy, Pyromancy, Geomancy, and Aeromancy. Spells are ranked by the mana they cost: Minor, Adept, Major, Grand, and Divine. Mortals do not cast Divine spells. Lemara enforces this law.

Anyone learns spells from Necromancy, Transmutation, Hydromancy, Pyromancy, Geomancy, and Aeromancy. Heavenly, Demonology, Arcanomancy, Nature, Otherworldly, Enchantment, and Chronomancy are rare. You learn them only through a feature granting access.

Your casting stat is your class's primary stat.

Upcasting

Magic is volatile. Adding mana to a spell upcasts it and greatly empowers its effects. You upcast only to a tier you are able to cast: a level 5 character casts Minor spells only, so no upcasting to Adept or Major.

Upcast effects are geometric: a spell upcast from Minor to Grand is 8 times as potent. Each step, choose one effect:

  • Double the dice rolled.
  • Double the range.
  • Add your casting modifier if not already included.
  • Increase the duration one step: 1 round → 1 minute → 10 minutes → 1 hour → 6 hours → 12 hours → 1 day → 1 week → 1 month → 1 year → permanent. Instant spells gain no duration.
