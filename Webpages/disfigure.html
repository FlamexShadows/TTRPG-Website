Disfigure


AP Cost: 1
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Otherworldly
Subtype: Chaos
Range: Touch
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: No

The caster targets a creature causing the creature's body to dimorph ...

  • 1-10: The creature grows an extra arm for the duration. If it has a weapon available for the arm each time it takes the attack action it makes an extra attack.

  • 11-20: The creatures body density morphs to unproportionate levels. The creature adds +2 to its AC.

  • 21-30: The creatures brain begins to expand. The creature adds +5 to its Intelligence, and Wits but takes a -5 Charisma.

  • 31-50: The creatures Muscles expand and surges with unnatural adrenaline. The creature adds +5 to its Strength and Fortitude but takes a -5 penalty to Finesse.

  • 51-70: The creature's body morphs causing it great pain in the process. The creature becomes crippled

  • 71-100: The creature's body morphs into an unnaturally slim yet perfected version of its race. The creature gains a +5 to Finesse and Charisma but suffers a -5 penalty to Strength and Fortitude
