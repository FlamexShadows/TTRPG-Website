Thunderclap Step


Range: Self
AP Cost: 4
Spell Rank: Major
Class: Aeromancy
Subtype: Lightning
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster becomes living lightning, instantly moving up to 20 meters in a straight line to a space it can see. Each creature within 3 meters of the space the caster left takes 4d8 air damage from the thunderous crack and must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or become Deaf for 1 minute. When upcast the caster adds an additional 2d8 air damage for each tier upcast.
