Control Flame


Range: 15 Meters
AP Cost: 2
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Pyromancy
Subtype: Flame
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: Instant
Concentration: Yes

The caster sizes control over a fire molding it in a way they wish. While the caster is unable to extinguish the flame they can alter its form such as making shapes, splitting it in two to make a pathway or any other way they wish. If the caster moves the flame it moves up to 5 meters per turn.
