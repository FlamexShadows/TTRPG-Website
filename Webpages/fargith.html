Background

Fargith, the benevolent deity of life, ascended to his divine status through the act of creation, birthing the first humanoids into existence. Revered for his nurturing and life-giving qualities, he holds dominion over the realms of vitality and renewal. Within the celestial realms, Fargith resides in the hallowed sanctum of the first ring of heaven, where he is eternally joined by his wife, Lemara.

The followers of Fargith are a diverse congregation, united in their dedication to compassion and altruism. Among his devout are priests and priestesses who devote their lives to serving the needs of others, selflessly offering solace and aid to those who seek it. Fargith's temple stands as a beacon of hope and refuge, renowned throughout the lands as a sanctuary where all are welcome, regardless of race, creed, or circumstance. Within the hallowed halls of Fargith's temple, his devoted clergy tirelessly tend to the afflicted and infirm, utilizing their gifts of healing to alleviate suffering and restore vitality. Through their ministrations, they offer comfort and support to the downtrodden and despairing, embodying the divine virtues of empathy and kindness. While the miraculous workings of Fargith's followers are rare, they are not unheard of. Among the most extraordinary manifestations of their faith is the gift of resurrection, a sacred and awe-inspiring act that defies the natural order of life and death. Though such miracles are few and far between, they serve as a testament to the boundless compassion and divine grace of the god of life.

Appearance

Fargith is depicted as a male of no distinct race. He has a muscular figure with soft accentuated facial features. His long brown hair flows just past his shoulders and his golden eyes can see through any living creature.
