Acid Cloud


Range: Touch
AP Cost: 4
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Geomancy
Subtype: Poison
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster creates a 5 meter diameter cloud of acid that makes targets vulnerable to earth damage. Any creature that in the cloud must succeed a Resistance (Body) save or lose 5 resistance to earth damage. Any creature that isnt immune or have resistance to earth damage is vulnerable
