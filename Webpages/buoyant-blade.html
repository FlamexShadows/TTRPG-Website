Buoyant Blade

Advanced
AP: 6
Requirements: Finesse 20
Type: Attack
Range: 25 meters
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The martialist jumps into the air creating strikes of pure magic to slash their foes from a distance. The martialist may make up to 3 attacks at any creature within 25 meters of themselves and move their movement during these slashes. The martialist must make an attack roll and upon a hit the creature takes 3d6+maneuver modifier magic damage.
