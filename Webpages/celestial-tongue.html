Celestial Tongue


Range: Self
AP Cost: 2
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Heavenly
Subtype: Virtue
Requirements: Sigils
Duration: 1 Hour
Concentration: No

The caster's voice booms in all languages at once guided by the heavens. All creatures that hear the caster speak hear the language they are most fluent in allowing all creatures to understand its speech even if it doesn't understand any language.
