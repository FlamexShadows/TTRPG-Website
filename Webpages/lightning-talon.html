Lightning Talon


Range: 15 Meters
AP Cost: 2
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Aeromancy
Subtype: Lightning
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: 1 Round
Concentration: No

The caster creates a claw of lightning that attempts to crush a creature within 1 meter of itself of the caster's choice. That creature must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or take 8d8 air damage and be shocked. A creature wearing or made of metal receives a -5 penalty to its save. The claw acts at the end of the caster's next turn.
