Whispering Madness


Range: 15 Meters
AP Cost: 4
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Otherworldly
Subtype: Illusion
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: No

The caster chants a phrase inaudible to all but the target of the spell. The target must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save or take 4d6 psychic damage and become blind. On a successful save the target takes half as much damage and suffers no additional effects.
