Create Ice Golem


AP Cost: 6
Spell Rank: Major
Class: Hydromancy
Subtype: Ice
Range: Touch
Requirements: Sigils, Words of Power
Duration: Permanent
Concentration: No

The caster creates a golem of ice. The golem has 10 hit points per kilogram of ice used and can be used to a maximum of 10 kilograms. The golem must remain in an area below the freezing point of water or begin melting taking 10 damage at the start of each of its turns. The golem can only make skill checks that are Finesse, Fortitude and Strength checks though it is still subject to Resistance (Mind) saves. The golem uses half the casters spell casting modifier for all checks, attack and damage rolls.

The Ice golem is vulnerable to fire and immune to psychic and cold damage. A caster can have no more than one golem under its control from a create golem spell. Any golem created previously by the caster by a create golem spell is instantly destroyed if the caster casts another create golem spell or is slain.

A golem created by Create Ice Golem can only be healed by being completely submerged in water and having Freeze Liquid on it restoring 10 health per casting.
