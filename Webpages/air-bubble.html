Air Bubble


Range: Touch
AP Cost: 1
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Aeromancy
Subtype: Wind
Requirements: Sigils
Duration: 1 Hour
Concentration: Yes

The caster touches a creature creating a temporary bubble of clean breathable air on the target's head. While this bubble is active on the creature, it can breathe normally underwater and is unaffected by hazards in the surrounding air. Casting Air bubble on a creature that has been effected by a vacuum spell supresses the vaccum spell's effects for its duration
