Vile ray


AP Cost: 4
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Demonology
Subtype: Damnation
Range: 20 Meters
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster directs a beam towards a creature striking it with a ray of unholy energy. The caster makes a magic attack roll on a hit dealing 2d4 unholy damage. In addition, the target must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save. On a failure the target is cursed until the end of its next turn.
