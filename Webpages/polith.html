Information

Polith drags the sun across the sky every day on his chariot, pulled by his celestial steed. His radiant glory illuminates the world, allowing life to thrive. At the end of each day, when his horses grow tired from their journey, Polith provides them with rest by rolling Kelria's moon in front of his carriage. This grants them shade and a few hours of respite before he removes the moon and resumes his journey. During an eclipse, one of Polith's horses has become injured, prompting him to move the moon in front of his chariot to protect it while he tends to his horse's needs. Polith resides in the seventh heaven, where his palace floats among the clouds. He is almost exclusively worshiped by the people of the desert and is a key figure in the Triarchy. Occasionally, Polith sends his angels to fulfill various tasks, extending his influence and maintaining the balance of his celestial duties.

Appearance

Polith is a breathtaking sight to behold, commanding attention with his imposing presence and a radiant aura that outshines even the sun itself. His armor speaks volumes of his strength, a resplendent cuirass adorned with bold red and white stripes, symbolic of both his ferocity and unyielding purity. Atop his head rests a majestic golden headpiece, intricately crafted and exuding magnificence, a clear emblem of his divine status. His skin, a luminous yellow akin to the brilliance of the sun, emits an ethereal glow, bathing those around him in its celestial light. With each movement, his hair, composed of countless golden strands of light, sways gently in the breeze, creating a mesmerizing spectacle. These strands of light, reminiscent of a star-studded night sky, sparkle and shimmer with every motion, casting a rainbow of colors that dance in harmony with his divine presence.
