Wall of Wind


Range: 10 Meters
AP Cost: 5
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Aeromancy
Subtype: Wind
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: 10 Minutes
Concentration: Yes

The caster creates a wall of wind up to 3 meters long and 3 meters high interuppting incoming projectile attacks, spells and maneuvers. Ranged attack rolls that pass through the wall have a 50 percent chance to pass through the wall. Attacks that fail to pass through have no effect.
