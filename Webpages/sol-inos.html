Sol Inos

Sol Inos, also known as "The Holy Land," is said to be the first continent created by Azaria in the second world. Shortly after its creation, Azaria, the first deity, disappeared, and Lemara assumed the mantle of lead deity. As the only remaining portion of Azaria's legacy, Sol Inos is regarded as the center of the world, with everything else built around it. Azaria and her nine chosen defended the land from the Great Evils, establishing its sacred significance.

After Azaria's fall, chaos swept across the lands. Sol Inos was first claimed by Dragon Lords who emerged after the defeat of the Tyrant Serpent. The serpent's corpse fell from the heavens, and its blood poisoned the once rich and beautiful lands, turning them into barren deserts. The collapse of the soil cleaved the massive continent of Sol Inos in two, creating Arendell and the Black Desert.

The Dragon Lords, once ordinary lizards twisted by the serpent's blood, were transformed into true dragons. Led by Bhaalnuva and Gwenivene, the ten Dragon Lords became the first mortal creatures to wield fourth-tier magic. They subjugated the denizens of Sol Inos, reducing them to mere slaves under their tyrannical rule.

After centuries of subjugation, a seraph of Fargith gave birth to a demi-angel named Simon. Nurtured and guided by his mother, Simon mastered fourth-tier magic. Tired of seeing his people in chains, he led a rebellion in which five of the dragons were slain. As he marched upon the capital, the remaining dragons fled south, biding their time and growing in power, waiting to reclaim their land.

In the aftermath, Simon was proclaimed the rightful heir to the Holy Land. Rising to power, he became the God King and protector of his people and their land, restoring hope and order to Sol Inos.
