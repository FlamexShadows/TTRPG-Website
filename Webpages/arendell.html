Ashten

Ashten is a large metropolis of trade and people. Ashten is one of the two capitals of Arendell. Forty years ago, it merged with the holy Capital during the great war, and it concluded. The marriage of King Esetebon and Queen Araphiene was a sign of union.

Ashten's primary source of income is mining Gold and Iron and exporting them to Nova Strados. The money in Ashten is unevenly split between the royals and nobility, leaving the commoners often to starve outside the central city of Ashten.

Old legends tell of a material called Halo crystals located in the mountains of Ashten. These crystals are precious as owners can trade them for a very steep price to underwater cities. These legends gained the attention of new settlers into Ashten, and they began to set up a small mining city to find these crystals. Shortly after, the noble house of Estebon arrived, funding more construction of homes and claiming the city as their own. They then called themselves the royalty of Ashten, And this angered the Holy Capital as it was essentially the people of Ashten claiming independence instead of being a colony. The Capital sent in forces but was met with resistance as the humans of the Capital fought back against the elves.

The war lasted 1000 years before coming to a halt as both capitals were sieged. The Holy Capital offered Princess Araphiene to marry Prince Estebon on the 6th to end the war. The new royal family would be indicted into royalty, and the Holy Capital would reassert control over Ashten.



Jivia

Also known as Sanctuary, Jivia is a remarkable desert city governed by the church of Polith. This vibrant city is renowned for its awe-inspiring monuments and architectural marvels that captivate scholars and visitors alike. Jivia serves as a bustling hub for traders, drawing merchants from far and wide. The city is safeguarded by a massive magical shield, which protects it from the scorching desert heat and frequent sandstorms.

The marketplace is a lively expanse filled with numerous vendor stands offering a variety of goods. To cope with the intense heat, the residents wear lightweight, breathable clothing. The houses, constructed from thick clay, provide excellent insulation, keeping the interiors cool and comfortable.

One of Jivia’s most notable features is a mysterious black pyramid that serves as the entrance to an underground city and mines operated by the moon elves of Kelria. Before reaching this subterranean realm, visitors must navigate a vast network of tunnels leading to the copper mines and the underground city. These labyrinthine tunnels are filled with twists and turns, necessitating the guidance of an experienced navigator to avoid becoming lost. Although these tunnels are largely empty due to resource depletion, their complexity poses a significant risk to those unfamiliar with the terrain.

The surrounding desert is dotted with numerous forges where skilled artisans transform the abundant sand into exquisite glass. These forges are a testament to the ingenuity and resourcefulness of Jivia’s inhabitants, who have turned the harsh desert environment into a thriving center of trade and craftsmanship. Additionally, small farms cultivate silkworms, producing high-quality silk. This silk is a coveted export to Sol Inos and Ashten, making the silk trade one of Jivia’s primary connections to the outside world.
