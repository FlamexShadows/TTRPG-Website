Information

In the shadowed corners of the abyssal realm, Belfast's followers lurk, embracing the darkness that seeps into their hearts. Drawn to his sinister allure, they seek power and dominion in a world where morality is but a fleeting illusion. Belfast, the enigmatic lord of the underworld, cares solely for the souls that descend into his shadowy domain. His pacts with infernal fiends forever bind those foolish enough to seek his favor, leading them down a perilous path toward damnation.

While fiendish pacts swiftly bestow extraordinary power upon the unwary, the creatures that grant it are reluctant to bestow more. For them, the soul of the target is the sole prize they covet. Yet, despite his malevolent reputation, Belfast is known to uphold the sanctity of contracts, ensuring that his fiendish servants honor their bargains.

Those who dare to forge direct pacts with Belfast, the Pale Lord, are met with unexpected generosity. He allows for modifications to the terms of the agreement, as long as they do not violate the core essence of the pact. This unusual compassion sets Belfast apart from his infernal peers and his absolute power over the underworld creates a sense of order in the once lawless land..

Belfast's union with the demon goddess, the Mistress of Misery, further solidifies his formidable position within the Abyssal Brotherhood. As the leader of the seven demons, Belfast maintains uncontested control over the underworld, a dominion granted to him by the legendary Narolith after he vanquished Misery in battle.

The soul of Misery remains trapped within Belfast's grand palace, a poignant reminder of their past conflict. However, in a twist of fate, Misery accepted Belfast's proposal to spare her soul from banishment to the Empty. This act of mercy only served Belfast’s interests even further as he gained control over the true demons of the underworld as they were loyal to Misery. This forced allegiance solidified Belfast's position as a ruler of the Underworld

The six remaining demons, witnessing Belfast's might and the transient nature of their own existence, bowed to his authority. They learned a harsh truth: their immortality was an illusion, and only by acknowledging Belfast's sovereignty could they ensure their survival in the treacherous underworld realm.

Appearance

In the annals of the hidden realm, Belfast emerges as an enigmatic figure, a tall human adorned in a dark hood that shrouds his face from the world's gaze. Just above his veiled visage, a necklace of human skulls adorns his neck, serving as a grim reminder of his sinister nature.

Belfast possesses an uncanny obsession with opulent jewelry, adorning himself with pieces worth millions of gold. Each piece sparkles and gleams with a sinister allure, reflecting his insatiable desire for wealth and power. His skin is pale as moonlight, almost translucent, as if drained of all life. His hands, in stark contrast, are as black as the darkest night, as if coated in a layer of charcoal. They are said to be the instruments of his malevolent deeds, capable of extinguishing life with a touch a leftover gift from his loyalty to Narolith.

When Belfast manifests as an echo, his true form unravels, unleashing a wave of despair that saps the hope and courage from even the most valiant warriors who dare to oppose him. His mere presence drains the life force from the battlefield, leaving a trail of desolation in his wake. Whispers of his name instill terror in the hearts of the brave, paralyzing them with fear and hopelessness.

Belfast's motivations remain shrouded in mystery, leaving those who encounter him to speculate about his origins and intentions. Some of the most ancient texts claim that he was Narolith’s first seraph that ascended to divinity. Others whisper that he is a manifestation of the collective manifestation of the cruelty of the underworld given form in the realm of shadows.
