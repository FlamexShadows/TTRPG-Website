Polearm Mastery

Expert
AP: N/A
Requirements: Polearm Expertise
Type: Passive
Range: Self
Concentration: No

The martialist's ability with polearm has become mastery. When a creature enters their melee range it no longer requires a reaction to make an OA. In addition they can now wield heavy shields with a polearm without the heavy property and a small shield with a polearm with the heavy property.
