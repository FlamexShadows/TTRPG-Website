Conditions

Basic Rules, updated November 26, 2026

Conditions are lasting effects on a creature. Each entry says what the condition does.

Bleeding

While bleeding, a creature loses 1 health at the start of each of its turns.

Blind

A blinded creature misses many attacks. Whenever a blind creature rolls an attack, it must roll a Percentile die. On a 50 or higher, the attack automatically misses. In addition, the blind creature is automatically Unaware of all things that rely on sight to perceive unless previously aware of it. Attacks made against a blind creature gain advantage on attack rolls. Blind creatures gain disadvantage on Evasion saves.

Burning

Any creature that is burning has its resistance to fire lowered by 20 and its resistance to cold raised by 20. Any creature that is burning and is not immune or resistant to fire damage becomes vulnerable to fire damage and takes 2d6 fire damage at the start of each of its turns. Any creature that is burning that gains the freezing condition cancels both.

Charmed

A creature becomes under the influence of another. While charmed, the charmer gains a +10 to all Charisma-based checks made with the charmed creature. While charmed, creatures may be convinced to perform actions that they would not perform without the influence of a charm. Charmed creatures will not perform actions that they perceive as directly harmful to themselves.

Corroding

While corroding, a creature loses half of its current physical armor at the start of each of its turns.

Crippled

A target has taken devastating damage, leaving it incapable of moving. A crippled creature is Pinned and is incapable of using features and spells that allow or require movement. A crippled creature may still be moved by allies or enemies.

Cursed

A cursed creature is marked with vile magic that weakens them. A cursed creature takes a -1 penalty on all checks and attack rolls. While cursed, a creature is treated as having 0 armor for the purpose of applying effects even while the target still has physical or magical armor.

Deaf

Deaf creatures are incapable of hearing their surroundings. A creature who is deaf is automatically Unaware of anything that relies on hearing to perceive it unless it was previously aware of its presence.

Diseased

While diseased, a creature gains disadvantage on all checks and attack rolls. A creature that is diseased must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save, DC 10 + damage taken, or gain the Nauseated condition.

Dominated (Charmed)

While dominated, a creature follows all commands given by its charmer.

Draining

While draining, a creature loses half of its current magical armor at the start of each of its turns.

Dying

  • Resurrection: to resurrect a creature, its soul must be free and willing to return to its body. If the soul is trapped, unable, or unwilling, the spell fails. Attempting to resurrect a creature into an unsurvivable body (such as one missing its head) still returns the creature, but it is instantly slain and the resurrection materials are wasted.

A creature reduced to 0 Health or below gains Dying.

  1. Unconscious: the creature falls unconscious and has 0 AP.
  2. Negative Health: track how far below 0 its Health falls (−5, for example). If the number passes its Fortitude score, it dies.
  3. Death saves: at the end of each of its turns, the creature rolls d100 and succeeds at or under its Fortitude modifier + level. Success: no change. Failure: mark one failed death save. Damage taken while Dying marks one failed save automatically.
  4. Stabilizing: another creature rolls Knowledge (Medical) against a DC equal to the negative Health (DC 5 at −5). Success: the creature is stable at 0 Health and loses Dying.
  5. Healing: any healing bringing the creature above 0 Health ends Dying.
  6. Death: the creature dies at 3 failed death saves, after 1 minute (5 rounds) of Dying, or when its negative Health passes its Fortitude score.

Exhausted

An exhausted creature is fighting every drop of energy it expends. While exhausted, a creature has the Tired condition. A creature that gains the exhausted condition gains a cumulative -2 penalty to its physical statistics for each time it gains the exhausted condition. A creature that gains a penalty higher than -10 must succeed on a DC 30 Resistance (Body) save each time it is gained after or die. The DC for this increases by 10 each time until the creature is no longer exhausted. Exhaustion can be removed from a full rest.

Fearful

A fearful creature is afraid of a single creature in its line of sight. A creature that is fearful has disadvantage on Resistance (Mind) saves and attack rolls while it is aware its source of fear is in its presence. If the creature gains the fearful condition again, it gains the Hopeless condition. A creature that becomes Motivated or Inspired cancels both effects.

Freezing

Any creature that is freezing has its resistance to cold lowered by 20 and its resistance to fire raised by 20. Any creature that is freezing and is not immune or resistant to cold damage becomes vulnerable to cold damage and has its AP maximum reduced to 2. Any creature that is freezing that gains the burning condition cancels both.

Grappling

While grappling a target, you move with the target you are grappling. A grappled creature can spend 3 AP to make a contested Overpower check against the grappler; if it wins, it breaks free. If the grappler is force-moved, it drops the grappled creature, and teleportation also ends a grapple.

Hastened

While hastened, a creature has its maximum AP increased by 1, doubles its movement, and gains 2 AC. A slowed creature that becomes hastened removes both effects.

Hopeless

A creature who is hopeless is Fearful and believes it has no chance of success against a source. While acting towards a source of hopelessness, a creature gains a -5 to all checks and attack rolls and has disadvantage against effects that apply the Fearful condition. While hopeless, each time a creature fails a check, gets hit, or misses an attack against a source of hopelessness, it subtracts an additional -5 to all checks and attack rolls towards overcoming its source. A creature that becomes Inspired cancels both effects.

Inspired

An inspired creature is motivated to continue its task and believes its odds of success are guaranteed because of its source. While acting directly towards its goal, a creature gains a +5 to all checks and attack rolls towards its goal. While inspired, each time a creature succeeds a check or lands an attack directly towards its goal, it gains an additional +5 to all checks and attack rolls towards completing it. A creature that becomes Hopeless cancels both effects.

Invisible

All creatures that rely on sight gain the Unaware condition against you unless previously aware of you. Invisible creatures gain advantage on attack rolls against creatures that cannot see them. Attack rolls made against the invisible creature by creatures that cannot see them must roll a Percentile die. On a 75 or higher, the attack automatically misses.

Knocked Down

A creature who is knocked down moves at half speed. Knocked down creatures also gain disadvantage on Evasion saves. While knocked down, attack rolls and contesting overpower checks made on contestants who are not knocked down have disadvantage. A knocked down creature may spend 1 AP to stand up if they are not Pinned or Crippled.

Motivated

A motivated creature gains advantage on all Resistance (Mind) saves and attack rolls towards directly overcoming its goal. If the creature gains the motivated condition again while motivated, it gains the Inspired condition. A creature that becomes Fearful or Hopeless cancels both effects.

Nauseated

While nauseated, a creature wastes its AP taking no actions as it spends its turn vomiting or some other action caused by sickness. A nauseated creature cannot take reactions.

Paralyzed

A creature who is paralyzed is Crippled. The creature is incapable of any bodily function that requires the movement of any body parts. All vital body operations still function while paralyzed, such as breathing and heartbeat. A paralyzed creature has its AP reduced to a maximum of 0 and regains no AP at the start of each of its turns.

Petrified

A petrified creature has its AP reduced to 0 and gains 10 resistance to Piercing, Slashing, and Bludgeoning damage. While petrified, a creature gains the Unaware condition for all creatures and effects in its surroundings. While petrified, a creature does not require sustenance and shows no signs of aging until freed.

Pinned

Pinned creatures are incapable of using AP for the movement action.

Poisoned

A poisoned creature gains disadvantage on all checks that use Finesse, Fortitude, or Strength.

Possessed

A force has invaded the body of a creature. As a result, the creature can no longer spend AP to perform actions. Instead, the creature may spend 2 AP to roll a contested Resistance (Mind) against its inhabitant to attempt to regain control of itself. On a successful check, the inhabitant loses control over the creature for a number of minutes equal to the amount of time it lost the contest by. If the inhabitant loses the check by 10 or more, it is forced out of the creature. A creature who allows an inhabitant to possess it has disadvantage on attempts to force it out. An inhabitant uses the host's physical stats but its own mental stats unless stated otherwise.

Severed

While severed, a creature is incapable of using magic items and spending mana or stamina for the duration. Any effects on the creature gained from mana or stamina resources are dispelled, while magic items are no longer responsive to its severed wielder and cannot be reapplied until its severance ends.

Shocked

Any creature that is shocked has its resistance to air lowered by 10. Any creature that is shocked and is not immune or resistant to air damage becomes vulnerable to air damage and must roll a d100 at the start of each of its turns. On a 50 or lower, it wastes its turn doing nothing.

Shoved

A creature who is shoved is physically moved by an opposing force. A shoved creature is moved 1 meter for each point it failed the check by (minimum one meter). Shoved creatures only interact with the initial and final meter they land on for environmental effects. If shoved into an object, the GM determines if the creature passes through or stops on the obstacle. A creature shoved takes 5 damage for each meter it is prevented from moving if it stops on an obstacle. A creature shoved less than 2 meters must pass a balance check determined by the GM to prevent being Knocked Down after being shoved. Creatures shoved more than 2 meters are Knocked Down.

Silenced

A silenced creature is incapable of speaking. While silenced, it is impossible to cast spells that require Words of Power or perform any other action that requires speech.

Slowed

While slowed, a creature has its maximum AP reduced by 1, loses half its movement and 2 AC. A hastened creature that becomes slowed removes both effects.

Stunned

A stunned creature cannot take reactions and has its AP reduced to 0.

Tired

A tired creature teeters on the brink of exhaustion. The creature gains disadvantage on all Strength, Finesse, and Fortitude checks. If a creature were to gain this condition again, it would gain the Exhausted condition. The tired condition is removed on a partial rest.

Unaware

A creature who has this condition does not know that the creature that applied this condition is around it. Any creature that applies the Unaware condition to a creature gains advantage on attack rolls, and its first attack is a Critical Strike against the target. A creature loses the Unaware condition whenever the current turn ends after it becomes aware of its applicant.

Unconscious

An unconscious creature has its AP reduced to 0. An unconscious creature gains the Unaware condition for all creatures and effects in its surroundings. Hits on unconscious creatures are automatically critical hits and allow for decisive strikes. An unconscious creature must succeed on a (DC 10 + Damage Taken) Resistance (Body) check or die.
