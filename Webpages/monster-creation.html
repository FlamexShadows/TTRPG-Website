Monster Creation

Basic Rules, updated November 26, 2026

One monster of Threat Rating (TR) X is a fair fight for one character of level X. An encounter budget equals the party's total levels. Four level 5 characters have a budget of 20.

Tiers

Every monster is a minion, an elite, or a solo. Pick the tier first. It sets most other numbers.

Example: a budget of 20 buys one TR 10 solo, four TR 5 elites, or eight TR 5 minions.

Statistics

  • The primary stat starts at 10 + TR. The secondary stat starts at 8 + TR. The other four start at 8.
  • Spread the tier's stat points across any stats.
  • Then add size Strength from the Sizes table. Tiny, Small, and Medium add nothing.
  • Modifier = (score − 8) ÷ 2.

  • A shove uses Strength for its DC. A poison bite uses Fortitude. A charm uses Charisma.
  • Bonus elemental dice and area damage add no modifier.
  • A basic attack costs 3 AP. A utility action costs 2 AP. A big area or control action costs 4 AP, once per round.
  • Area damage: about 3d6 at TR 4, 3d8 at TR 6, 4d8 at TR 10. A successful save takes half.
  • Resistances come in steps of 5 or 10.

Weapon and Magic Traits

These go on the monster's signature attack. Spells never get them.

  • Weapon traits are free. A real weapon keeps its traits from the Weapon Table.
  • Natural attacks pick fitting weapon traits: 1 for a minion, 2 for an elite, 3 for a solo. Hooked suits grabbing claws. Cleave suits sweeping tails. Deadly suits vicious bites.
  • Magic trait points: minion 0, elite TR ÷ 2, solo TR, never more than 10. Spend them on Weapon Magic Traits at their listed cost.
  • Bonus: the points spent add the same amount to the attack's attack and damage rolls. Accuracy adds its own attack bonus on top.
  • Format: write a line under the attack. Traits: Heavy, Deadly I, Reach II, Skilled. Magic +4: Flaming, Unholy, Accuracy I.

Caster Minions

A caster minion knows one spell. It is a new Minor or Adept spell found nowhere in the spellbook. Write it in full in the stat block, in spellbook format, using the monster's spell attack or spell save DC.

Example: Tithe of Cinders (unique). Minor. Demonology (Damnation). Range 15 meters. 3 AP. Requires Words of Power. Instant. The caster flings burning blood at one creature. Spell attack. On a hit the target takes 2d6 unholy damage. If the caster drops to 0 Health before its next turn, the target takes 2d6 unholy damage again.

Example Abilities

Minion (no saves)

  • Pack Tactics: +2 to hit a creature an ally is adjacent to.
  • Shield Wall: +2 AC while an ally of the same kind is adjacent.
  • Last Gasp: when it drops to 0 Health, it makes one attack against an adjacent creature.
  • Swarm Mind: +1 to attack for each other minion of its kind within 5 meters, up to +3.
  • Thrown Junk: 2 AP ranged attack, 10 meters, weapon dice with no modifier.

Elite

  • Guardian (reaction): when a creature attacks an ally within 1 meter, make one attack against that creature.
  • Blood Frenzy: +2 to attack and damage while below half Health.
  • Draining Strike: regains Health equal to half the damage of its signature attack.
  • Terrifying Presence: an enemy starting its turn within 5 meters makes a Charisma-based Resistance (Mind) save or is Fearful until the end of its turn. Once per creature per day.
  • Mist Step: 2 AP. Teleport up to 6 meters to a space it sees.
  • Poison Spit: 4 AP, once per round, 5 meter cone. Fortitude-based Resistance (Body) save. Failure: area damage in poison and Poisoned for 1 minute. Success: half damage.

Solo

  • Unbreakable Will: once per round, when it fails a save, it succeeds instead.
  • Second Phase: at half Health it changes: a new attack, a new resistance, or a burst of area damage.
  • Lair Pulse: at the end of each round the lair acts: falling rocks, spreading fire, rising water, or new minions.
  • Call the Swarm: once per combat, 2 AP. Summon minions whose budget adds up to TR ÷ 2.
  • Shake It Off: at the start of each of its turns, it ends one condition on itself.

Building a Monster

  • 1. Idea. Write one sentence: who it is, where it lives, what it does in a fight.
  • 2. Tier and TR. Fodder is a minion. A real threat is an elite. A boss is a solo. TR equals the level it challenges.
  • 3. Size, type, alignment. Size sets the Health die and size Strength.
  • 4. Primary and secondary stats. Weapon fighters use Strength or Finesse. Tough creatures use Fortitude. Casters use a mental stat.
  • 5. Stat points. Spend 3, 5, or 7 × TR. Add size Strength. Work out each modifier.
  • 6. Defense. Armor Value, AC, and Health. Halve Health for a minion.
  • 7. Saves and skills. Three saves. Spread TR skill ranks over 1 to 3 skills.
  • 8. Signature attack. Dice and traits, attack modifier, damage, then magic trait points and their bonus.
  • 9. Abilities. Minions 0 to 2 basic. Elites 1 to 3, with one 4 AP action. Solos 3 to 5, with one answer to control.
  • 10. Spells or maneuvers. Use the formulas above. A caster minion gets one unique spell.
  • 11. Lore. Two or three sentences and one detail players will remember.
  • 12. Check. Stat points, AC, Health, attack, every DC, and the magic budget all match the formulas.

Example: Bog Goblin Spearman

Minion, TR 2, Small Humanoid. Budget cost 1.

  • Stats: Finesse primary 12, Strength secondary 10, others 8. Spend 6 points: 2 each in Finesse, Strength, and Fortitude. Strength 12 (+2), Finesse 14 (+3), Fortitude 10 (+1), others 8 (+0).
  • Defense: Armor Value 10. AC 10 + 1 + 3 + 1 = 15. Health 2 × (3 + 1 + 2) + 1 = 13, halved to 6.
  • Saves and skills: Mind +1, Body +2, Evasion +4. Sneak +5 (2 ranks).
  • Attack: Spear, 1d20+5 to hit, 1d6+1 piercing (half of +3). Traits: Reach II, Thrown (10), Two Handed. No magic.
  • Ability: Pack Tactics.

Example: Ashen Knight

Elite, TR 8, Medium Undead, Lawful Evil. Budget cost 8.

  • Stats: Strength primary 18, Fortitude secondary 16, others 8. Spend 40 points: Strength 10, Fortitude 10, Finesse 6, Wits 6, Intelligence 4, Charisma 4. Strength 28 (+10), Finesse 14 (+3), Fortitude 26 (+9), Intelligence 12 (+2), Wits 14 (+3), Charisma 12 (+2).
  • Defense: Armor Value 90. AC 10 + 9 + 3 + 4 = 26. Health 8 × (5 + 1 + 8) + 9 = 121 (8d10+77).
  • Saves and skills: Mind +7, Body +13, Evasion +7. Overpower +14, Intimidate (Str) +14.
  • Attack: Burning Halberd. Base +18. Magic +4: Flaming (1), Unholy (2), Accuracy I (1). Attack 1d20+23. Damage 1d10+14 slashing plus 1d8 fire, plus 4d4 against good-aligned creatures. Traits: Heavy, Deadly I, Reach II, Skilled.
  • Ashen Sweep: 4 AP, once per round. Each creature within 2 meters makes a DC 24 Evasion save. Failure: 3d8 fire and Knocked Down. Success: half damage.
  • Undying Oath (reaction): when a creature attacks an ally within 1 meter, the knight makes one Burning Halberd attack against it.
  • Maneuvers: Stamina 44, maneuver attack +18, maneuver save DC 24.

To make it a solo: spend 56 stat points, give it 8 magic points, set Max Turns to 2, and add a solo ability. It then costs 16.
