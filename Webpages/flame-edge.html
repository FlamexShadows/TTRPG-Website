Flaming Weapon
Expert
AP: 1
Requirements: Weapon
Type: Enhancement
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: Yes

The martialist magically imbues a weapon they are holding with heat causing its edge to burn white hot. For the next minute their weapon deals an extra 2d10 fire damage on a hit and any flammable objects struck are ignited and the martialist ignores any AC bonus provided by nonmagical shields or armor.
