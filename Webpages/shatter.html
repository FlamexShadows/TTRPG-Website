Shatter


Range: 20
AP Cost: 3
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Hydromancy
Subtype: Ice
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster chooses a number of tiles of ice equal to their casting modifier. The ice shatters, sending sharp shards cutting and freezing each creature or object on a tile that has shattered. Anything standing on a shattered tile takes 1d4 cold and 1d4 piercing for each tile it takes up that is shattered.
