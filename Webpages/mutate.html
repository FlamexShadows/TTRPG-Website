Mutate


Range: Touch
Casting Time: 10 Minutes
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Biomancy
Subtype: Fleshcraft
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils, 2 Mutagen
Duration: Permanent
Concentration: No

The caster touches a creature as it grows one of the following choices of the casters choosing. A creature cannot have more than one mutation at a time. These mutations can appear in any way the caster desires.

Thicken Skin
The target gains resistance 5 to physical damage.

Noxious Blood
Whenever a creature strikes the target with a melee attack the creature takes 1d6 poison damage.

Dense Body
The target counts as one size larger for the purpose of overpower checks.

Gill Growth
The target gains the ability to breathe underwater.

Players may create thier own mutations to be used but should not be more powerful than any of the listed options or replicate the effects of a higher level spell.
