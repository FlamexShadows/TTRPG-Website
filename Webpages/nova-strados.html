Nova Strados

The Capital

Nova Strados is a nation known for its shipments of exotic fruits and fine wines to various countries and regions around the world. Seafood is a staple in the local cuisine, with sushi being particularly popular. The pace of life is relaxed, yet the society places a strong emphasis on honor and pride. Families strive to maintain a high social standing, often going to great lengths to uphold their reputation, including exiling those who break the rules to save face and disassociate from them.

Primarily a military state, Nova Strados is ruled by the royal family with an iron fist, instilling fear in the hearts of would-be criminals. The government enforces strict laws and metes out harsh punishments for even minor infractions, maintaining order and discipline throughout the nation.

One hundred and twenty years ago, Nova Strados was attacked by a group of malevolent spirits known as the Court of Crows, led by a Yuki Onna and an Oni. While the spirits were ultimately defeated, the victory came at a great cost. Many lives were lost, and several islands of Nova Strados remain trapped in perpetual winter due to the lingering magic. Despite the passage of a century, the empire has made no significant progress in reversing the effects caused by these vengeful spirits. The frozen islands stand as a stark reminder of the devastation wrought by the Court of Crows, their icy grip a lasting testament to the battle fought long ago.
