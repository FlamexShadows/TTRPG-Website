Leveling

Basic Rules, updated November 26, 2026

Total bonus points are a running total, not new points each level. Total XP is the experience a character needs to reach that level. A character levels up at the end of a full rest once it meets the requirements.

Earning Experience

Characters earn XP for defeating monsters. Defeated means killed, driven off, captured or otherwise overcome. Add up the XP of every monster defeated in an encounter and divide it evenly among the characters who took part.

A minion is worth half an elite of the same TR and a solo is worth twice as much, matching their cost in the encounter budget. A party that wins fair fights levels up after about 8 of them at most levels.

The GM may also award XP for goals met without a fight, such as a rescue, a solved mystery or a deal struck. A good award is the XP of an elite whose TR equals the party's average level, for each character.
