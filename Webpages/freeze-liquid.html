Freeze Liquid


Range: 10
AP Cost: 1
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Hydromancy
Subtype: Ice
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster freezes a body of water or another liquid no hotter than boiling water within range that is no larger than a 10 meter diameter sphere. If a creature is fully submerged in water subjected to freeze liquid it is freezing for 1 minute. When upcast to Adept or greater the diameter doubles for each tier higher.
