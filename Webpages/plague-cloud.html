Plague Cloud


Range: 25 Meters
AP Cost: 4
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Necromancy
Subtype: Decay
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: Yes

The caster creates a 5 meter diameter cloud of pestilence that infects all creatures within range. Each creature within the cloud must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save or become nauseated for 1 minute. A creature may repeat its save at the start of each of its turns.
