Leech Positive Energy


Range: 10 Meters
AP Cost: 1
Spell Rank: Major
Class: Necromancy
Subtype: Tether
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: Yes

The caster attempts to siphon the positive energy fueling the spells of life given to a target within range. The target must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save. On a failure the target has its soul tethered to the caster's body. While tethered, the target receives half of any healing it receives while the other half instead is sent to the caster.
