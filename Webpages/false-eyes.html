False Eyes


Range: 25 Meters
AP Cost: 4
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Otherworldly
Subtype: Illusion
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: 1 Hour
Concentration: No

The caster targets a creature it can see within range tempting a creature towards insanity. The target must roll a Resistance (Mind) save. On a failure, the creature becomes fearful as it begins to hallucinate eyes from walls, creatures, and other things in its general surroundings are watching it.
