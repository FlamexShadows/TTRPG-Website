Planar Rift


Casting Time: 1 Minute
Spell Rank: Major
Class: Chronomancy
Subtype: Dimension
Range: 10 Meters
Requirements: Words of Power, Material listed below worth 10,000 GP
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: No

The caster creates a rift connecting two planes together, allowing travel between the two worlds. The rift appears as a 1-meter-long bright orange crack in the air that emits a hymn with a sound that correlates with the plane it connects to on the other side. The portal is a two-way portal and appears at a random place in the other world.

Material Requirements

  • Border World: Etherstone
  • 7 Heavens: Halo Crystals
  • Underworld: Hellstone
  • Spirit World: Luminite
  • Empty: Emoulitatie
