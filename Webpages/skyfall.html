Skyfall

Master
AP: 6
Requirements: Strength 30
Type: Attack
Range: 100 meters
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The martialist takes to the air and gathers momentum, building up power as they soar or jump 100 meters into the sky. The martialist can choose to land anywhere within 100 meters of its origin. Any creature within 10 meters of the landing point must succeed on an Evasion save or take 40d12 bludgeoning damage. In addition if the martialist lands directly on a creature that fails its Evasion save they take an additional 10d12 bludgeoning damage and must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or become crippled for 1 minute. A creature may repeat this save at the end of each of its turns.
