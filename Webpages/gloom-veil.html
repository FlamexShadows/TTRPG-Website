Gloom Veil


Range: Self
AP Cost: 2
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Tenemancy
Subtype: Penumbra
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: 10 Minutes
Concentration: No

The caster gathers the dimness around itself into a clinging veil. For the duration, the caster gains a +5 to checks made to hide or move unseen while in dim light or darkness.
