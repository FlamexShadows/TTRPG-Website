Banishment


Range: 15 Meters
AP Cost: 2 AP
Spell Rank: Major
Class: Tenemancy
Subtype: Penumbra
Requirements: Words of Power, A symbol that opposed the force banished
Duration: 1 hour
Concentration: Yes

The caster attempts to banish a creature to a twilight demiplane. The creature must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save or be shunted to the demiplane for the duration of the spell. If the creature leaves the demiplane during its duration the spell automatically ends.
