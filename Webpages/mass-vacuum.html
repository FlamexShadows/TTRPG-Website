Mass Vacuum


Range: 100 Meters
AP Cost: 4
Spell Rank: Grand
Class: Aeromancy
Subtype: Wind
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: Yes

This spell functions as Vacuum but affects a 30 meter cube instead of a single target. Spells that require air or water such as Wall of Wind, Tornado, or Tsunami have no effect on creatures inside mass vacuum.
