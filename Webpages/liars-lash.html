Liar's Lash


Range: 1 Meter
Casting Time: 1 Minute
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Demonology
Subtype: Torment
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: No

The caster attempts to conjure spectral burning chains spiked chains to rend a targets flesh that has its maximum AP reduced to 0. The target must succeed a Resistance (Mind) save or be covered in spikes for the duration of the spell. The target is magically forced to answer a question. If the target lies it takes 1 true damage. The same question may be asked more than once and 1 question can be answered per round. It is common to have several translators present to interpret answers and other tricks attempt to thwart the loopholes only forcing an answers that are truthful but not clear.
