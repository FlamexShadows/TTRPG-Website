Smoke Screen


Range: 5 Meters
AP Cost: 2
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Pyromancy
Subtype: Ash
Requirements: Words of Power, Charcoal
Duration: 10 Minutes
Concentration: No

The caster creates a screen of smoke that blocks the vision of creatures. A strong wind that is natural or created by a gust, tornado, blizzard, or any other spell that creates strong winds disperses the smoke cloud. The smoke cloud can be as large as a 5 meter radius sphere.
