Hag's Eye


Range: 15 Meters
AP Cost: 2
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Enchantment
Subtype: Charm
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: No

The caster selects a creature and must succeed a Resistance (Mind) save or become cursed. The target may repeat its save at the end of each of its turns.
