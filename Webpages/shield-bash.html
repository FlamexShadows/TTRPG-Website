Shield Bash

Basic
AP: 3
Requirements: Proficiency with shields, Strength 13
Type: Attack
Range: 1 Meter
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The martialist uses their shield as a weapon, slamming it into their opponent. The martialist makes an overpower check against the targets Armor Class. If successful, they deal damage equal to their shield's AC Bonus + Strength modifier and the target must make a Resistance (Body) save or be stunned until the end of its next turn.
