Angel's Grace


Range: 5 meters
Casting Time: Reaction
Spell Rank: Major
Class: Heavenly
Subtype: Protection
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster summons a guardian angel to protect a target it can see from a harmful Resistance (Mind) or Demonology spell. The angel interferes with the effect offering protection. The target may add +10 to any save made to avoid or end the effect.
