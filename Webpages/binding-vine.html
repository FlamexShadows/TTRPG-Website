Binding Vine


Range: 20 Meters
AP Cost: 4
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Nature
Subtype: Primal
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: Yes

The caster targets an area within range causing vines to burst from the ground in a 10-meter radius sphere. Each creature within the range of the spell must succeed on an Evasion save or become Pinned.
