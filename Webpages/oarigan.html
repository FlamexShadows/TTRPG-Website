Information

Born from the fires of the Phoenix Forge itself. They are revered and worshiped by those who seek to master the art of crafting and to create items of great power and beauty. Oarigan is known as the heavenly weapon smith rumored the create the arsenal of the divine himself. His palace within Marissa's domain is by a water vent heating allowing his forge to operate underwater.

Appearance

The appearance of Oarigan is at the forge and is commonly depicted crafting Marissa’s trident. Oarigan has a muscular build and stands tall, towering over most mortals in depictions of them entering his forge. His is skin the color of smoldering embers and hair that glows like molten gold. Their eyes are a deep, fiery red, and their voice resonates like the sound of a hammer striking metal.
