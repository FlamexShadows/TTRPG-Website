Information

In the realm of deities, Cecylla stands as the radiant goddess of nature, embodying the boundless beauty and bounty it bestows. Under her divine guidance, myriad Grove Keepers, devoted guardians of the woods, vigilantly safeguard the verdant landscapes from those who inadvertently or intentionally inflict harm.

Cecylla's sacred symbol, revered by her followers, depicts a grain of wheat nestled behind a scythe. It symbolizes the harmonious coexistence of nourishment and growth with the natural cycle of renewal. Moreover, Cecylla possesses a unique ability among the deities: she can manifest on the mortal plane without the need for an avatar, enabling direct divine intervention. This extraordinary power sets her apart and allows for profound interactions with her worshippers.

Often mistaken for a simple dryad dwelling in the depths of the forest, Cecylla's true identity is veiled in mystery. Yet, her benevolent nature shines through in her unwavering dedication to preserving the delicate balance of the natural world. She welcomes both druids and dryads into her sacred fold, entrusting them with the noble task of protecting the forests and their animal inhabitants. Never directly intervening in even the greatest of conflicts, Cescylla chooses champions to carry out their will. Cecylla bestows upon them a fragment of her will. The will empowers her champions to confront threats with unwavering courage and restore harmony.

Appearance

Cescylla emerges from the depths of the ancient forest as an ethereal figure. Her body, slender and graceful, is enveloped in a cloak of lush foliage. Verdant leaves and delicate flowers intertwine with her skin, creating a seamless connection between the elf and her natural surroundings. Her hair, a cascade of dark vines, falls freely over her shoulders. The tendrils move and sway as if alive, responding to the whispers of the wind. Her pale skin, smooth and flawless, resembles the texture of hard bark, a testament to her resilience and deep connection to the earth. Cescylla wears a gown that is a masterpiece of nature's artistry. Woven from intricate spider webbing and adorned with leaves that change with the seasons, her attire mirrors the cyclical beauty of the forest. In spring, the dress bursts with vibrant hues, reflecting the burgeoning life. In summer, it transforms into a tapestry of verdant leaves, shimmering in the warm sunlight. However, as winter's icy grasp descends upon the world, the leaves fall from the gown, revealing a pristine white canvas. The spider webbing, now coated in a layer of frost, glitters like diamonds.
