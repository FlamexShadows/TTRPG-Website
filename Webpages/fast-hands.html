Fast Hands

Tier: Basic
Action Cost: 3 AP
Requirements: Finesse 13
Type: Attack
Range: Weapon
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No
The martialist attempt to steal an item from a target that isnt being held or secured to the target such as armor. The martialist rolls a contested sleight of hand check against the creature's armor class. On a successful check the martialist successfully steals the item off the creature.
