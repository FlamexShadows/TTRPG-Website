Crystalize


Range: Touch
AP Cost: 1
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Geomancy
Subtype: Crystal
Requirements: Sigils
Duration: Permanent
Concentration: No

The caster touches a liquid creating glass-like crystals on its surface. These crystals are made of the same material as the liquid. The crystals made by this spell can be thrown or used as ammunition for weapons. Any weapon that uses ammunition made from crystalize deals an additional 1d6 earth damage. Crystals created by this spell are worthless to any jeweler.
