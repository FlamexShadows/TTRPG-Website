Name: Feint
Basic
AP: 1
Requirements: -
Type: Attack (Melee)
Concentration: No

The martialist feints their current attack. When a creature takes the attack action it can forgo the attack reducing its AP cost to 1. If it takes the attack action in the same turn or makes an opportunity attack again it adds a +5 to its attack roll. The creature keeps its +5 bonus until it misses an attack roll until the start of its next turn.
