Devourer of Hope


Range: 60 Meters
AP Cost: 6
Spell Rank: Grand
Class: Enchantment
Subtype: Charm
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils, An Obsidian Skull worth at least 3000 Gold consumed upon casting
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster targets a number of creatures equal to their casting modifier. All targets within range take 15d12 psychic damage and must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save. On a success the creature suffers no further effects. On a failure the creature becomes Hopeless. If the creature is already Fearful or Hopeless and has no magical armor is instead instantly slain.
