Information

The origins of Oana are shrouded in mystery, but many believe that she was born from the flames of love itself. Some say that she was once mortal, but ascended to godhood after a lifetime of passionate affairs and intense emotions. Regardless of her origins, Oana is revered and worshiped by those who seek to kindle the fires of love and lust in their hearts.

Appearance

Oana's appearance is divine and majestic. She has long, flowing hair that glows with a soft pink light, and her eyes shine with a fiery intensity. Oana's skin is a pale, milky white, and she is often depicted wearing a flowing white dress that shimmers like silk. Her voice is sweet and melodious, with a hint of sultriness that can captivate anyone who hears it.
