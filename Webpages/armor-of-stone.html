Armor of Stone


Range: 10 Meters
AP Cost: 3
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Geomancy
Subtype: Rock
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: No

The caster targets one creature and covers it in stone. The creature gains 5d10 physical armor and gains 10 slashing, piercing or bludgeoning resistance while it has this armor. The armor lasts until the armor gained from the spell is reduced to 0 or the duration ends.
