Phasos

Power: Demigod

Titles: The Predator

Tenants:
Only the strongest and most cunning survive. Embrace your role as the apex predator, and let no prey escape your grasp.
Assert your dominance over the wild. Show no mercy to those who challenge your supremacy, whether beast or fellow hunter.
Offer the hearts of your prey to the god of the hunt. These sacrifices honor the divine and strengthen your bond with the primal forces.

Portfolios: The Hun t

Follower Alignment: Any

Alignment: Chaotic Neutral

Information

Long ago, Marissa and Cecylla met and engaged in an archery contest. As they shot, Marissa became enraged upon realizing that Cecylla was a better shot than she. In a fit of anger, Marissa took her bow, laced an arrow with her poisonous saliva, and shot it into a deer, killing it instantly. Unaware of the poison, Cecylla healed the beast's wound. However, the poison had already transformed the deer, which now stood tall as a bipedal creature, part man and part deer. Marissa's saliva granted this beast the ability to survive underwater, coating its lungs and enabling it to breathe in water. The creature, now known as Phasos, uprooted a large tree before leaping from the heavens into the water. There, he swam and slew a thousand sea creatures for every blade of grass his blood touched. Phasos, the father of the beastmen, is said to watch over his progeny. He bestowed upon them the rage he felt at the moment of his creation, allowing them to transcend their physical limits and become formidable beings both on land and in the water. The beastmen revere Phasos, drawing strength from his primal fury and their shared origins.

Appearance

Phasos appears as a towering beast-man, a formidable figure with an imposing presence. His antlers, adorned with small stripes of warpaint, tell the tale of his conquests: blue for creatures of the sea, green for those of the land, and yellow for those of the skies. The stripes are so numerous and densely packed that it is impossible to distinguish where one color ends and another begins, creating a seamless tapestry of his victories .
