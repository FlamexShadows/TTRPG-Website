Necrotic Ray


AP Cost: 3
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Necromancy
Subtype:Ray
Range: 10 Meters
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster focuses a beam of necrotic energy at a creature within 10 meters of themselves. The target creature must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or take 2d6 necrotic damage or half as much on a success.
