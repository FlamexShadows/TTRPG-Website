Gust


Range: 10
AP Cost: 2
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Aeromancy
Subtype: Wind
Requirements: Words of Power, SIgils
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster creates a gust of wind on a 2-meter radius within range. Any open flames in the area are snuffed out and creatures with the burning condition are no longer burning. Any creature within the area must succeed on a Balance check or be knocked down.
