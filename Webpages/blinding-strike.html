Blinding Strike

Basic
AP: 3
Requirements: -
Type: Attack
Range: Weapon
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No
The martialist enhances a melee weapon with blinding light before striking their foe. If the attack hits the creature takes an additional 2d8 holy damage and must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or be blind for 1 minute. The target can repeat this save at the start at each of their turns.
