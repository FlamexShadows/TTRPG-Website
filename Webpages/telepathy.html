Telepathy


Range: 10 Meters
Casting Time: 10 Minutes
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Arcanomancy
Subtype: Divination
Requirements: Sigils
Duration: 1 Hour
Concentration: No

The caster picks up to 5 creatures within range and grants them telepathy. Each creature that has this telepathy can only communicate with creatures they share a language with.
