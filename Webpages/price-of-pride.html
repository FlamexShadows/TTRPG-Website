Price of Pride


Range: Sight
Casting Time: 1 Minute
Spell Rank: Major
Class: Demonology
Subtype: Curse
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils, An Obsidian Skull worth at least 3000 Gold consumed upon casting
Duration: Permanent
Concentration: No

The caster lays a curse upon a paralyzed, stunned, or unconscious creature it can see, naming aloud a task that would take no longer than 1 month to complete. The target must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save or have one stat of the caster's choice reduced by 5. The curse can only be ended by a grand purge of Grand rank or by fulfilling a condition the caster names when the curse is laid.
