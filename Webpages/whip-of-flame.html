Whip of Flame


Range: 5 Meters
AP Cost: 4
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Pyromancy
Subtype: Flame
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster conjures a whip of embers. The caster makes a spell attack roll. On a hit the creature takes 4d6 fire damage and the creature must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save or begin to burning for 1 minute. The creature may repeat its check at the start of each of its turns.
