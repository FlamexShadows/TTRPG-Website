Purge


Range: Touch
AP Cost: 4
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Heavenly
Subtype: Retribution
Requirements: Sigils, Words of Power
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster targets a creature, object, or spell effect it can see. The purge spell can create one of the following effects.

Burden of Sins
If the target is a creature and is evil-aligned it must succeed on a Resistance (Mind) save or become paralyzed for 1 minute.

Exorcise
The caster forces a creature causing the Possessed condition to succeed a Resistance (Mind) save or be forcefully removed from the creature they inhabit.

Dispel Magic
The caster targets an ongoing magical effect. If the target is an item that is not an artifact or relic any ongoing effect it produced ends. If it is a spell that is the same level or lower than the purge the spell ends.
