Age of Reclamation

After the Great Purge, most forms of magic were lost, and a large portion of what remains can only be found in ancient manuscripts that survived in the darkest corners of creation, where they escaped divine desolation. The fortunate few, known as the Fragmented, can recall their old magic, but they are incapable of using it. Others had the knowledge siphoned from their minds entirely.



To this day, the world remains in the Age of Reclamation, a period dedicated to rediscovering various old forms of magic. Explorers and scholars tirelessly seek out these hidden manuscripts, hoping to unlock the secrets of the past and restore the lost arts of magic to their former glory.
