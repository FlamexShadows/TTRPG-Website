Umbral Tether


Range: 15 meters
AP Cost: 1
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Tenemancy
Subtype: Umbra
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: 1 Hour
Concentration: No

The caster creates a tether that links themselves and a willing target's source together. The caster and the target combine magical and physical armor and share this linked pool for the duration of the spell.
