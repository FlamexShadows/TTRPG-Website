Magnetize


Range: 20 Meters
AP Cost: 2
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Geomancy
Subtype: Metal
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: Yes

The caster targets a piece of metal creating a strong magnetic field around it. Even if smaller and or lighter, other objects are pulled towards the Magnetized object as a result of the pull. All metal objects not fixed in place within 5 meters of the object become attached to it. Creatures carrying metal or otherwise magnetic objects must succeed on an Overpower check or lose the object as it attaches itself to the polarized object. Metal armor and creatures made of metal have difficulty moving in affected area. All creatures within 5 meters of the object wearing or made of metal must succeed on an Overpower check or have its speed reduced to 0 and be pulled 1 meter closer to the object. To remove an object attached to the Magnetized object a creature must succeed on an Overpower check but needs not roll again until it's next turn to move if successful. A creature may spend 2 AP to repeat or trigger an additional Overpower check. Any creature subjected to the spell is forced to repeat the Overpower check at the start of each of its turns unless it is already attached to the magnetized object.
