Crippling Strike

Tier: Basic
AP: 3
Requirements: N/A
Action Cost: 3 AP
Range: Weapon
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No
Upon landing a melee attack the martialist may declare a Crippling Strike. If the target of the attack has no physical armor it must succeed on a Resistance (Body) save. On a failure the creature becomes crippled for 1 minute.
