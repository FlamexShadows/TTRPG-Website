Armor of Magic


Range: 15 Meters
AP Cost: 2
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Arcanomancy
Subtype: Apotropaic
Requirements: Sigils, Words of Power
Duration: 8 Hours
Concentration: No

The caster is enveloped in a semi-transparent purple barrier that grants the target 5d6 magic armor and ends one of the following effects. Burning, frozen, paralyzed, or stunned condition.
