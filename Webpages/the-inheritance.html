The Inheritance

Long ago, Hate launched a brazen invasion of the Winter Isles with the intent of seizing control of the Spire of Permafrost. Advancing through the northwestern portal, the fiendish forces made swift progress, reaching the spire within a matter of weeks. However, their ambitions were thwarted when Raq Quaal, a powerful guardian, confronted Hate and banished it back to the underworld. Despite being vanquished, Hate managed to grasp a fragment of the Permafrost, dragging it down to the underworld with them, leaving a swath of freezing devastation in their wake upon their return.

Driven by the humiliation of their defeat, Hate continued to send various forces to the Winter Isles in a relentless pursuit to fully claim the Permafrost. One hundred years ago, Hate's cunning general, Azadriel, a succubus of formidable power, seized an opportunity when a significant distraction left the spire momentarily undefended. Exploiting this vulnerability, Azadriel managed to seize control of the Permafrost, furthering Hate's dark agenda.

The Winter Isles were left weakened, their primary source of power stolen. Once-powerful blizzards dwindled to a mere layer of snow. Meanwhile, the Underworld thrived, growing in power as it absorbed the stolen Permafrost. No longer dominated solely by soot, sulfur, and molten rock, the underworld now shared its landscape with frigid lakes of ice and snow, a testament to Hate's insidious influence over the realms of both light and darkness.
