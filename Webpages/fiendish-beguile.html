Name: Fiendish Beguile


AP Cost: 2
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Demonology
Subtype: Curse
Range: 10 meters
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: 1 Hour

Conjuring the power of the underworld, a fiend offers advice during its speech. While speaking, the target of the spell gains a +5 bonus to a check depending on the fiend summoned. Any creature with the ability to see into the border world sees a figure whispering in the target's ear.

Fiends:
Succubus: The target receives a bonus to Allure checks.
Doomsayer: The target receives the bonus to Intimidate (Charisma) checks.
Garizinio: The target receives the bonus to Deception checks.
Slov: The target receives the bonus to Persuasion.
