Shroud of Ghosts


Range: Self
AP Cost: 3
Spell Rank: Major
Class: Necromancy
Subtype: Puppetry
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: Yes

The caster creates a shroud of ghosts that surround them protecting the caster from harmful effects. While shrouded in the dead the caster is treated as always having magical armor for effects and gains 5 resistance to necrotic damage. The ghosts are otherwise harmless.
