Prismatic Ray


Range: 10 Meters
AP Cost: 3
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Arcanomancy
Subtype: Destruction
Requirements: Words of Power, Sigils
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

A spell commonly used to evaluate the defenses of a target, the caster makes an attack roll. On a hit, they deal 1d4 fire, cold, earth, air, magic, piercing, slashing, bludgeoning, and necrotic damage.
