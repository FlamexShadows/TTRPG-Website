Flame Shroud


Range: Self
AP Cost: 3
Spell Rank: Adept
Class: Pyromancy
Subtype: Flame
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: Yes

The caster creates a shroud of flame around them burning those who touch them. The caster gains cold resistance of 20 and any creature that hits the caster with a melee weapon takes 2d12 fire damage.
