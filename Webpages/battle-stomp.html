Battle Stomp

Expert
AP: 5
Requirements: Strength 25
Type: Attack
Range: 10 Meters
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The martialist powerfully stomps their foot into the ground creating a 10 meter cone. All creatures within the cone must succeed on an overpower check or be knocked down and take 15d8 bludgeoning damage or half as much on a success. Any creature knocked down is pinned for 1 minute. A creature may repeat its check at the start of each of its turns ending the effects on a success.
