Information

Kelria holds a profound place in the hearts of the Moon Elves, serving as their protector and revered deity. Once a year, during the Silver Moon Festival, Kelria descends from her celestial abode to grace the mortal plane. Adorned in a delicate dove mask, she dances with her devoted followers throughout the night, her movements as graceful as the celestial bodies she represents. As the moonlight bathes the land, Kelria's presence is hidden amongst the masquerade in her name. In ancient times, Kelria was one of the leader of the Triarchy, her church order of assassins engaged in a brutal and seemingly endless war with the cultists of Narolith, Belfast, and Elesnia. However, the relentless conflict eventually took its toll, leaving Kelria's followers weary and disillusioned. Recognizing the futility of endless bloodshed, Kelria underwent a profound transformation. She renounced her warlike ways and embraced a newfound philosophy of peace and tranquility. Kelria's church order disbanded, and she retreated to the secluded realm of Gloomwood, where she sought solace and renewal.

In her newfound sanctuary, Kelria emerged as a cheerful and benevolent deity, advocating for peace and harmony among all living beings. She no longer engaged in direct battles, instead entrusting her brother, Oarin, to wage wars on her behalf when necessary. Kelria's transformation inspired a wave of change throughout Gloomwood, as her followers embraced her teachings of compassion and understanding. To this day, Kelria remains a beloved and revered figure in Gloomwood. Her temples and shrines are adorned with intricate carvings and murals depicting her celestial beauty and her unwavering commitment to peace. The Moon Elves, under her watchful gaze, live in harmony with nature and strive to uphold the principles of kindness and unity. Kelria's legacy extends beyond Gloomwood, as her teachings have spread to distant lands, inspiring countless souls to embrace the path of peace and understanding. Her story serves as a reminder that even in the darkest of times, the light of hope and compassion can prevail, guiding us towards a brighter and more harmonious future.

Appearance

Kelria, the celestial goddess of the moon, embodies a beauty so profound and otherworldly that it defies replication even by Pavar, the master of illusions. Her divine allure captivates the hearts and minds of all who behold her, an ethereal presence that transcends mortal comprehension. Kelria's skin is a rich, dark olive, imbued with a natural radiance that seems to glow from within. Her eyes, a striking shade of white, sparkle with the same luminescence as a full moon on a clear night. These eyes, ever-changing, reflect the phases of the moon, their pupils shifting from the slender crescent to the perfect roundness of the full moon, capturing the celestial dance of the lunar cycle. Atop her head rests a lunar crest, a symbol of her dominion over the night sky, a crown that gleams with the soft light of the moon itself. Her attire is as mesmerizing as her presence. Kelria is often depicted in a flowing black lavender dress, its fabric shimmering with a subtle iridescence. The dress becomes semi-transparent at her waist, blending seamlessly with the night and hinting at the mysteries of the cosmos. The gown's ethereal quality mirrors the delicate balance between light and shadow, the known and the unknown, which Kelria so effortlessly embodies. Her hair, a cascade of midnight black at the roots, gradually transitions to a lustrous silver towards the ends
