Loosen Earth


Range: 10
AP Cost: 3
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Geomancy
Subtype: Rock
Requirements: Sigils
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: Yes

The caster loosens the earth around them transforming it into a more malleable variation of its previous self. Rocks turn to sand, hard dirt turns to loose soil, etc. The malleable substance created by this spell reverts back to its original shape and hardness after the duration of the spell pulling all small objects able to be moved with it. If an object or creature is stuck in the earth during or after the duration of the spell it requires a DC 20 Overpower check to escape. This DC can be raised or lowered by the Game Master depending on the material that is transformed. The DC 20 represents a target in hard dry soil or quicksand. Despite altering the terrain, structures do not lose their stability from having their stone or other material affected.
