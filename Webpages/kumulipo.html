Kumulipo


Range: 100 meters
AP Cost: 6
Spell Rank: Grand
Class: Tenemancy
Subtype: Umbra
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: 1 Minute
Concentration: Yes

The caster creates a 5 meter radius origin at a point they can see in range. Each creature within 20 meters of the point must succeed on an Overpower check or be pulled to the origin of the spell. Any creature that touches, enters or starts its turn in origin point takes 20d12 magic damage. A creature reduced to 0 health dies. If the creature is medium or larger it merges with the Kumulipo increasing its radius by 5 meters and resetting its duration to 1 minute.
