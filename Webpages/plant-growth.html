Plant Growth


Range: Touch
Casting Time: 1 Minute
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Nature
Subtype: Nurture
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster touches soil enriching it with nutrients allowing for plants to flourish. In a 100 Meter radius the soil is now enriched. Plants in the area are enriched allowing them to grow. All poisonous plants in the area become safe for consumption for all creatures.
