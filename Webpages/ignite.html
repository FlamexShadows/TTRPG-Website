Ignite


Range: Self
AP Cost: 1
Spell Rank: Minor
Class: Pyromancy
Subtype: Flame
Requirements: Words of Power
Duration: Instant
Concentration: No

The caster creates a 5 meter cone generating a harmless wave of heat to all creatures but igniting all flammable objects within the sphere. Any creature wearing or carrying a flammable object takes 4d6 fire damage for armor and 1d6 for equipment or weapons worn or carried. Damage from equipment or armor may only be applied only once even if the creature is carrying multiple flammable equipment or is wearing multiple pieces of flammable armor.
